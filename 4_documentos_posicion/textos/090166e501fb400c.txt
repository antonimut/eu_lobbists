Recommendations for the proposed EU act on circularity requirements for vehicle design and on management of end-of-life vehicles  The Finnish Innovation Fund Sitra supports the European Commission’s proposed act on circularity requirements for vehicle design and the management of end-of-life vehicles.   In Sitra’s view, the proposed act is key to advancing the data economy and the circular economy in Europe. The initiative emphasises smart manufacturing, improved recycling and sustainability of vehicles, and rightly identifies data as the key enabler to meet these goals. It also contributes to strengthening the EU’s strategic autonomy and resilience at a critical time, as it aims to tackle the flow of end-of-life vehicles and critical raw materials out of the EU.   Sitra, as a public fund that catalyses pilot projects, is active in this area with a specific project that explores the management of end-of-life vehicles and the so-called missing vehicles. Sitra is actively working on concrete solutions to these problems in close cooperation with stakeholders from across society.   Sitra’s 2022 study on the smart use of data for more efficient car recycling explored how increased data use and sharing could significantly increase the annual recycling rate of cars in Finland from the current level of about 70%. The proposed solution was to develop a common digital platform for the entire vehicle recycling business ecosystem that enables the registration and tracking of every car at all stages of the recycling process.   This digital platform is now being developed with funding from Sitra and will go live in Finland in early 2024.  Sitra has been accelerating the transition to a circular economy for many years by providing information, tools and solutions to implement the change. For example, Sitra funded a study showing that circular solutions involving material reuse are highly effective in reducing CO2 emissions in heavy industry1. More recently, in a memorandum, Sitra has proposed mainstreaming circularity within the EU Single Market as a means to increase resource efficiency and the EU’s open strategic autonomy2. The role of the data economy has been identified as essential in enabling circular solutions.   Sitra recommends that the European Commission consider the following when developing and implementing the proposal:  1. Establish a digital platform to tackle the challenge of missing vehicles  The digital platform currently being developed in Finland by Sitra is the model for an innovative solution on how the car recycling process can benefit from being more data and AI-driven across the EU. In Sitra’s view, this platform has the potential to set the standard for how the problem of missing vehicles can be tackled and how a stronger vehicle recycling ecosystem can be created in other member states as well, with very tangible benefits for all stakeholders.  The digital platform will enable more accurate monitoring of the entire end-of-life vehicle recycling process and bring together the entire vehicle recycling ecosystem, consisting of all official recycling operators, government agencies and other relevant stakeholders. This unique approach provides a benchmark solution for creating a data-driven ecosystem that spans the private and public sectors.   1 Material Economics 2018  2 Putting nature at the heart of the European Green Deal - Sitra

Sitra strongly supports the European Commission’s proposal that member states should strengthen national and cross-border cooperation in order to reduce the number of missing vehicles. As stated in the proposed act, the digitisation and use of data will help to improve the issue of missing vehicles by introducing digital reporting in authorised treatment facilities and sharing vehicle registration information between member states.   Sitra is happy to work with interested parties outside Finland on this solution.    2. Implement an EU-wide data space for end-of-life vehicles   The implementation of digital platforms in all member states is the next step for the recycling of end-of-life vehicles, which requires considering how to create an EU-wide IT network. Sitra believes that this can best be achieved by creating an EU-wide data space for the entire end-of-life vehicle recycling market covering all member states.    As stated in the proposed legislation, there is a clear need to develop IT systems and data exchange in the cross-border environment. However, the creation of a single unified IT system implemented in all member states would not be a viable solution.   Sitra proposes the creation of a European data space architecture for end-of-life vehicles. This solution would use and build on the principles of decentralised data structures. Data space would allow transparency and coordination in end-of-life vehicle management on a European level, while still providing sufficient flexibility at member state level.  Sitra is participating in developing European data space initiatives through the Gaia-X project and acts as the coordinator for the Gaia-X network in Finland.   3. Increase the EU’s strategic autonomy and resilience regarding critical materials and components   The proposal brings forward the creation of a circularity vehicle passport. In Sitra’s view, this is an important step and is aligned with the broader need to implement digital product passports across different categories and verticals in the EU.   The circularity vehicle passport could bolster the EU’s resilience and strategic autonomy, in addition to its circular economy aims. By having a clear understanding of the materials and components in each vehicle, the EU could reduce its dependency on critical raw material imports and thus its vulnerability to disruptions in global value chains. This approach would support the EU Commission's overarching strategy to ensure a secure and stable supply of critical raw materials, as presented in the EU Critical Raw Materials Act in March3.   Digital product passports not only promote circular business models but also position the EU at the forefront of innovation. This would help to ensure that the European automotive industry remains competitive in a rapidly evolving global market.   Sitra is actively funding several pilot projects on digital product passports in the circular economy. These include vehicle batteries and the tracking of emissions data from logistics transport.  3 COM(2023) 160 - Proposal for a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for ensuring a secure and sustainable supply of critical raw materials

Conclusion  Sitra supports the proposal as a vitally important step towards achieving the transition to a competitive European economy that is climate-neutral, circular and data-driven. Sitra is actively working on practical solutions in this area by funding pilots, conducting studies, and connecting stakeholders. Sitra provides the Commission with insights from the end-of-life recycling data platform in Finland and the digital product passport initiatives, among others. These trials underscore the importance of data and circular economy measures in solving complex problems, as envisaged in the Commission’s proposal.

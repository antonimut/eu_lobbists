Waste Framework Directive revision – environmental impact of waste management 
 
 
PRE,1 the association representing the plastic recycling industry at the EU level, welcomes the EC’s initiative 
to revise the Waste Framework Directive (WFD), as an opportunity to improve the waste management of the 
textile sector, in line with the EU waste hierarchy and the objectives of the EU Textiles Strategy. 
 
PRE supports the overall objectives of the WFD revision to establish Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) 
schemes for textiles products , which is a key measure to make producer s responsible for the end-of-life 
management of textile products they place on the market.  
 
In view of the separate collection target for textiles by 2025, EPR schemes will help in boosting the collection, 
sorting, preparation for re-use and recycling of textile products, thereby incentivising necessary investments 
in the EU recycling infrastructure . Another key element that EPR schemes can promote is  related to  the 
development of research to improve technologies to sort and recycle textiles, especially containing polymeric 
fibres. 
 
Further, the introduction of eco-modulation based on requirements included in the Ecodesign for Sustainable 
Products Regulation should also be supported as  a drive towards designing textile products so that they are 
easily recyclable at their end-of-life. 
 
However, PRE believes that to strengthen the requirement in Article 22a paragraph 7(b) on self-certification, 
it should be linked with Article 22b by requiring producers to provide a proof of their registrations for each 
Member States where they place on the market textile products via online platforms . 
 
 
PRE remains available to further discuss the elements included in this paper with the relevant policy officers 
at DG ENV. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
ABOUT PLASTICS RECYCLERS EUROPE 
Plastics Recyclers Europe is an organization representing the voice of the European plastics recyclers who 
reprocess plastic waste into high quality material destined for production of new articles. Recyclers are 
important facilitators of the circularity of plastics and the transition towards the circular economy.  
 
Plastics recycling in Europe is a rapidly growing sector representing over €8.7 billion in turnover, 11.3 
million tonnes of installed recycling capacity, more than 730 recycling facilities, and over 30.000 
employees.

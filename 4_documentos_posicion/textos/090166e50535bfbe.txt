PRE amendments to the Regulation on circularity requirements for vehicle design and on 
management of end-of-life vehicles 
 
Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE),1 the association representing the plastic recycling industry at the 
EU level, welcomes the European Commission’s (EC) proposal for a Regulation on circularity 
requirements for vehicle design and on management of end -of-life vehicles 2 as an effective 
instrument to increase the circularity and sustainability of plastic components used in vehicles. 
 
The proposal for a Regulation, by introducing new articles on EPR schemes, separate collection, 
dismantlability and design for recycling requirements, represents a pivotal step towards 
expanding the end-market for plastic recyclates. 
 
PRE advocates for the recyclability of plastic packaging by design and the inclusion of recycled 
content in parts and components for new vehicles placed on the market. Therefore, PRE welcomes 
the requirements of the EC proposal for all vehicles to be desig ned according to their end-of-life 
management.  
 
To summarise our list of amendments, we would like to focus on the following priorities that 
would provide full support to the plastic recycling industry and the EU circular economy for 
plastic waste:  
 
- Recycled content targets: mandatory recycled content targets from plastic post-consumer 
waste materials are key drivers for the uptake of plastic recyclates in the automotive 
industry. Recycled materials stemming from plastics used in parts and components in end-
of-life vehicles boosts the innovation in the waste management of vehicles towards design 
for recycling, and post-shredding technologies, hence creating new market opportunities. 
 
- Verification and traceability of the origin of the waste materials : the introduction of 
targets on recycled plastics to the automotive industry sector can be enforced only via a 
verifiable traceability of plastic waste materials. For this reason, PRE calls on the type -
approval competent authorities to require manufacture s to provide certification on the 
traceability of waste materials, on the origin and nature of the plastic waste used in a new

- Level-playing field between EU and its economic partners: the Proposal must ensure that 
verification and traceability requirements, in addition to all the requirements of the 
Articles, must apply to any economic party interested in placing vehicles on the Union 
market. 
 
- Ban on landfilling of vehicles: should include any vehicles without exceptions. 
 
 
PRE remains available to further discuss the key focus areas and the full list of amendments to the 
Regulation on circularity requirements for vehicle design and on management of end -of-life 
vehicles. 
 
 
 
 
ABOUT PLASTICS RECYCLERS EUROPE  
Plastics Recyclers Europe is an organization representing the voice of the European plastics recyclers who 
reprocess plastic waste into high quality material destined for production of new articles. Recyclers are 
important facilitators of the circularity o f plastics and the transition towards the circular economy.  
 
Plastics recycling in Europe is a rapidly growing sector representing over € 8.7 billion in turnover, 11.3 
million tonnes of installed recycling capacity, more than 730 recycling facilities, and over 30.000 employees

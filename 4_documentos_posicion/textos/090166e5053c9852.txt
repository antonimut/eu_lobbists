OBSERVATIONS ON THE PROPOSED REGULATION ON THE CIRCULARITY OF VEHICLES (EU) 2018/858 AND (EU) 2019/1020 REPEALING DIRECTIVES 2000/53/EC AND 2005/64/EC       Rafael Romualdo Azor Castaño,  AAD President  
Ref. Ares(2023)8236195 - 01/12/2023
 
The Andalusian Association of Dismantlers (AAD) considers positively that a new regulation is 
drafted that clarifies and improves the design and circularity of vehicles, and very positively that it 
has the status of a Regulation instead of a Directive. However, we understand that this should 
serve to move forward and not take steps backwards, Spain has a RD 265/2021 of April 13, very 
mature and with great advances, we find in the proposal of the new Regulation, parts in its 
content that can generate interpretations contrary to the objective of the norm, which we proceed 
to point out in order to consider its replacement in the norm.

Art. 3 29) "reconditioning" means actions 
carried out to prepare, clean, test and, if 
necessary, repair a part or component that 
has been removed from a vehicle or vehicle 
at the end of its useful life in order to restore 
performance or functionality of said part or 
component within the intended use and the 
range of features initially conceived in the 
design phase applicable at the time of its 
introduction into the market; 
This definition mixes the meaning of “preparation 
for reuse” with that of “reconditioning.” 
 
A reconditioning or reconstruction involves a repair 
by altering the part, replacing its components, 
software updates, etc. to return the component to 
its original state. However, the vast majority of 
pieces are only dismantled and their condition is 
checked without the need to alter anything of the 
rescued piece. External cleaning, as well as testing 
the operation of a piece, does not alter its condition 
such that it is considered reconditioned. 
 
For example, let's imagine an engine with 100,000 
km of life, when it arrives at the scrapyard it has 
50,000 km, indicating that an engine has been 
reconditioned implies that it will have another 
100,000 km of useful life again, when it is only 
being reused. 
Art. 3 29) "reconditioning": actions carried out 
that involve an alteration of the part in order 
to restore the performance or functionality of 
said part or component within the intended 
use and range of performance initially 
conceived in the design phase applicable in 
the time of its introduction into the market;

Art. 11.2 Manufacturers shall ensure 
cooperation with authorized treatment 
operators and repair and maintenance 
operators by establishing the necessary 
communication platforms to provide and keep 
up-to-date the information referred to in 
paragraph 1 and the information specified in 
Annex V. 
 
Manufacturers will provide the information 
referred to in the first paragraph free of 
charge. Manufacturers may charge fees to 
waste management operators and repair and 
maintenance operators in the amount 
necessary to cover the administrative costs of 
ensuring access to the required information 
through communication platforms.. 
Whoever manufactures and/or puts a product on 
the market must be responsible for covering the 
collection and treatment of its products and 
components when they acquire the status of waste 
(polluter pays principle). Vehicles are complex 
products, which are made up of a set of parts that 
must be reused before reaching said condition 
(principle of European waste hierarchy.) Therefore, 
through the Extended Producer Responsibility 
Systems, the treatment costs of the vehicle and its 
components must be covered when the cost of 
treatment is negative. 
 
Therefore, this measure transfers new costs for 
reuse to repair operators and authorized treatment 
centers, contrary to the aforementioned principles 
(it transfers obligations from the producer to the 
reuser), aggravating reuse. This measure will 
make it difficult to reuse its components, will make 
it more expensive to reuse vehicles with positive 
value and will nullify the possibility of reusing 
components from vehicles with negative value.

FERVER INPUT ON THE PROPOSED ELV Regulation 
 
  
FERVER is the European Federation of Glass Recyclers. Our federation currently 
consists of 24 members spread across 13 EU - Member States and 3 other countries. 
They recycle around 70% of the packaging glass waste collected in Europe.   
 
The European glass recyclers warmly welcome the publication of the proposal for a 
Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on circularity 
requirements for vehicle design and on management of end-of-life vehicles, amending 
Regulations (EU) 2018/858 and 2019/1020 and repealing Directives 2000/53/EC and 
2005/64/EC. 
 
FERVER supports and welcomes the choice for a regulation instead of a directive in 
order to harmonize legislation across the EU. In particular, FERVER welcomes the new 
rules included with the aim to ensure that exported vehicles are roadworthy and are 
not in fact ELV’s as they will enable additional recycling of glass in end-of-life vehicles 
in the future.    
FERVER strongly supports the future dismantling obligation  contained in article 
30 of the proposal for an ELV Regulation, to dismantle the windshields, rear and 
side windows made of glass in end -of-life vehicles .  This will considerably 
improve the quality of the recycled material glass , as the same level of quality of 
glass material cannot be achieved after shredding and not even after post-shredding 
technologies.  
Furthermore, FERVER suggests adding also glass roofs to the dismantling obligation  
and to make it mandatory for the dismantled glass materials to be stored 
separately in clean storage places free of impurities in order to avoid severe quality 
problems.  
As far as the feasibility of such system setup is concerned, FERVER refers to the EPR 
Valorcar system (www.valorcar.pt) as it already exists and works very well in Portugal. 
Through this system, the glass is recycled through dismantling. The windshields are 
dismantled prior to shredding with electric cutters. The side windows are broken and 
the glass is collected in bags. This glass goes then to the glass recyclers. As result, 
thousands of tonnes of glass have already been recycled from ELV’s.  For the year 
2021, 169.868 kg glass was reused and 2.132.212kg was recycled through the 
Valorcar system.

The dismantling obligation of windshields will also enable the recycling of the PVB-
fraction, which is layered in between the glass fraction. This PVB -fraction is very 
promising for the future and studies are currently being conducted in this respect , 
e.g. under the Horizons Project Sunrise (SUNRISE project | Efficient recycling of PVB material 
from laminated glass (sunrise-project.eu), in order to improve its quality. FERVER participates 
to this Horizons project. Note that PVB fraction recycling can also not be achieved 
through post-shredding technologies.  
The future recycling of the PVB-fraction in windshields will improve the business case 
for recycling the glass of End of Life Vehicles. It also underscores the need to have 
good storage practices for the dismantled windshields, and good standards for the 
removal, both for manual dismantling as for any type of mechanical dismantling, in 
order to ensure good  quality of the glass materials and the PVB.  
Finally, in conjunction with the above  and in order to close the loop, FERVER also 
proposes to include a recycling target of 50%  for glass in end of life vehicles and a 
recycled content target of 10% for glass in new cars. Such targets would help the car 
industry to become more circular, to reduce the CO2 footprint in producing the cars, 
and at the same time create a sustained  demand for the recycled glass in end of life 
vehicles.

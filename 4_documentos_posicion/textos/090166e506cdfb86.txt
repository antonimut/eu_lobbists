 
 
 
Proposal for a Regulation on the welfare of dogs and cats and their 
traceability 
 
 
EUCED – E.E.I.G. welcomes and fully supports the initiative of the Proposal for a 
Regulation on the welfare of dogs and cats and their traceability at Member State 
level. EUCED believes that we must contribute to common well -being, and this 
englobes much more than just humans. It is necessary to create measures that can 
defend those who cannot speak, despite being able to express themselves the way 
they know better; Those who should have as many rights as we do, after all, just like 
the five senses, we both hear, breathe, feel, taste, and see, we just have different 
ways of demonstrating it due to our anatomy. We are very similar in several specific 
aspects, different in many others, but we both deserve equal respect. 
We hope that th is new law gives the due importance that should be given to this 
subject, which is so relevant! It's not just about safeguarding the well -being of 
animals, it's more than that. It is teaching current and future generations to do the  
good in the face of incorrect situations, because the past has passed, but we are 
always on time to take correct actions and save lives from suffering, providing them 
with the same thing that we have waiting for all of us, at the end of the day when we 
leave work, after an exhausting day... a home, with people who love us. 
 
 
The importance of looking out for others 
The truth is that the values that once remained rooted in each person in the past 
have been lost over time, especially with new generations. But who  has the fault? 
Nobody, or better, the guilty one s are the circumstances. It is true that over the 
years a lot has been improved, we have the most recent and certainly great example 
of Artificial Intelligence, which came to give us a boost as a society; but, in the same 
way that we, the most recent people, learned to work and accept this change from 
paper to screens, I question why there has not also been positive growth in terms of 
care for animal welfare? If dogs were previously seen as a beneficial element in a 
relationship for commercial exchange, such as hunting, why have these values been 
lost? The answer is nothing less than a locked drawer. New tools have emerged to 
help us, trying to show that animals are currently  and merely used as companion 
animals and due to this factor, in our view, the respect that there was for them has 
been diminished, and they are beginning not to be appreciated. 
It is time for change, and the proposed initiative establishes that animals deserve to 
be treated with the respect for life that any human being (even the smallest insect) 
deserves to have. These are basic rights. 
In order for us to follow this process and for it to actually be effective and not just a 
few more pages that society scrolls through and does not pay due importance to, we 
need to re -educate our people to do good, through initiatives, or even fines in 
extreme cases. 
We should not close our eyes when we see a lost or suffering animal, we should take 
action, whether is it directly if we have the power to do so, or indirect ly, contacting 
specific authorities and exposing what is wrong.

• Improvement of the surveillance 
When it comes to this topic, it should be essential to reinforce supervision, and this 
could be at the level of conditions to provide a home for an animal, or at a more 
external level of controlling illegal actions such as dogs and cat sales and the use of 
the female for procreation, both in order to obtain some monetary compensation 
through this. 
The implementation of daily checks on the streets would help controlling and keeping 
a record of the number of abandoned animals through microchip verification and the 
issuing of fines to owners when its checked that an animal that does not meet the 
necessary requirements for its own safety; the inspection, even on a externally way, 
of residences in order to verify any deplorable conditions that the animals could be 
found, especially at times of extreme hot and cold temperatures; harder insistence, 
especially online, as it is through a screen that the most peculiar cases of sales tend 
to occur, and not only on platforms registered for the purpose of selling dogs or cats 
of race, always having in mind  that there are many sellers who does not have the 
license or permission to sell, and this public usually use side ways to publish animals 
for sale, using applications such as Facebook, as an example of many. 
Last but not least, and this last paragraph is directly connected and related to the 
following paragraph of this essay, which refers to the precarious use of the female s 
as a money machine, in which female dogs or cats are constantly obligated to give 
birth, a consequence of abusive procreation , and at some point some can even die 
due to lack of conditions in which the body ends up giving in, because there is no 
resting between cycles.  This is not built or supposed to be this way, and  it is 
imperative that this needs to end, by having a closer control record of each female's 
gestation periods, or their sterilization to avoid not only unwanted pregnancies, but 
also the probability of serious illnesses developing over time, and death. 
 
 
• Accessibility and health monitoring 
In the society, there are various social classes, with different pockets, and by this, I 
mean that not every dog and cat owner, has the ability provide their pet the health 
care they need, due to lack of money, yet they still treat them the way we all should. 
They feed them and give them the essential that they need to live a happy life, and 
of course, to have a roof over their heads.  
 
 
 
One of the situations that we consider most recurrent over the years, and which is in 
line with what was written in the previous paragraph, is the lack of accessibility to 
free sterilizations, which is still a problem nowadays. 
There are few places that do this, but not much  places that can give answer to all  
the pets that exist among us, which means that for example, this type of health care, 
is not going to be available for everyone. S terilizations are important not only in 
preventing unwanted pregnancies for the females, but also in reducing the chance of 
the animal , dog or cat, male of female,  developing problems of health such like 
cancer, over the years. 
There is a way that we could think about it to solve this problem, which could be 
private clinics to perform this type of surgical intervention… but, if these clinics  
usually work with paid services, how can we make them help us? Our proposal is that 
the government of every State Member of the Europe, grants fiscal benefits to every 
clinic that joins this initiative, subsequently allowing us to use for free this type o f 
service.  
If we could have several places spread around the countries with this policy, not only 
dogs and cats would be so much healthier now and, in the future, as also they would 
be prevented to procreate excessively and maybe the rates o f adoption could even 
get higher, reducing the level of abandoned animals, due to the clinics  by helping 
when it comes to health. 
 
 
• Creation of public shelters for animals 
Not everything has to be done with money, many of the elements around us can be 
transformed with the help of recycling. 
We currently have access to the necessary means to make change s with our own 
hands when it comes to collecting materials and recycling them, transforming them 
into animal shelters. The intention is to create several spaces spread across different 
countries, where animals can rest and protect themselves from the rain a nd the hot 
summer sun. These shelters can be made by gluing bottles, with scraps of fabric that 
are no longer being used and can be transformed into something that creates outside 
protection next to walls or sheltered spaces, with wooden pallets at the base. 
Kennels are sometimes unable to respond to all the animals they find abandoned, 
and there is an awareness that not all of us have the means to be able to open our 
doors to more animals, but if we all provide the minimum living conditions for our 
animals while they don't have a home, we are already helping. 
If we all contribute with what  we can, we are warming the hearts of those who, 
despite not being able to speak, know how to express their gratitude. 
 
 
• Recognition 
As with everything in life, it is only when it is recognized that there is a problem that 
measures are taken to solve it, and this case of abandonment and mistreatment of 
dogs and cats, it is no exception to the rule. 
The problem exists, it was recognized, and it came to light, so it is time for it to end 
and to be solved. If each of us doesn't look out for the smallest and most defenceless, 
who will?

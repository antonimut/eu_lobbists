 
FFE contribution to the European Commission consultation on the Package 
Travel Directive  
4 December 2023 – 13 March 2024 
 
The French Equestrian Federation (FFE), the French sport federation for equestrian sports with 
9500 equestrian holdings and more than 700 000 licence holders , welcomes the revision of 
the Package Travel Directive.  
In France, the equestrian sector represents 35 000 direct jobs, making horse riding the largest 
private employer for sport in France.  
Considered as farmers by the French law, equestrian centres have an atypical activity, strongly 
linked to education, tourism and of course sport for all, including people with disabilities. 
Thanks to the diversity of its disciplines, the French system is a ffordable by sharing the 
facilities, coaching and horses with tailored project to everyone.  
Despite its comments in the former consultations, the FFE is disappointed by the fact that the 
revision is concentrated on the core activity of package trav el, ie. tourism and transport, due 
to the covid-19 pandemic and the Thomas Cook bankruptcy, whereas the other activities that 
fall under the scope of the directive are not taken into account. 
In that perspective, it wants to reiterate its comment that the equestrian centres – organised 
either in non -profit organization or with commercial status should not be considered in the 
scope of the PTD. 
Indeed, they organise training or equestrian hiking, which are mainly the extension of the daily 
or weekly practice of horse riding. In this perspective, the consumers are already clients of the 
centres (through a sport license for example). Only a very fe w agencies in France are 
specialized in the organisation of travel package of riding trekking. In the vast majority, the 
itinerary organisation, the board and lodging are organised by the equestrian centre itself.  
We strongly believe that the obligations resulting from the Directive are not appropriate for 
such stakeholders , which are not in any case organised as a travel agency. In France, the 
organisation of equestrian training for minors requires already to be compliant with 
national legislation (civil liability insurance, several administrative declarations (for the 
accommodation of minors, presentation of the educative project…).  
The PTD created new obligations, which represent extra costs and an administrative burden 
for SMEs, which comply already with sport obligations. The obligations related to the 
insolvency protection do not correspond to the economic reality of these sport and 
agricultural structures.  
It seems to the FFE that the aim of the Directive  and the revision – which is laudable – is not 
suitable to the equestrian centres which most of the time, propose these activities to their 
members. The FFE is asking again for a specific derogation for members of sport federations,

which organise themselves the package dedicated to their license holders in their own 
countries (especially equestrian training for minor people), not only for the ones organised as 
associations but also for the ones organised as SMEs. 
As a conclusion, the FFE calls for the legislators to adapt the scope of the PTD Directive. It is 
crucial to exclude some small stakeholders, inter alias the sport organizations, thanks to an 
exemption of the scope of the directive. From the FFE’s perspective, the equestrian centres 
- whether they are organised as an association or not - should be excluded from the Directive 
when they are  
- members of a sport federation recognised by their Member State and/or the 
international sport federation,  
- And when they offer the package with sport activity to their licence holders , as an 
extension of their regular training.

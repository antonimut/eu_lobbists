 
 Verband der Elektro- und Digitalindustrie 
 
ZVEI statement  
Measuring instruments – technical update of EU rules  
 
 
Key comments from the perspective of the electrical and digital 
industry 
 
   
The MID should remain as stable as it is today and demand more clarification of the current MID across 
countries! ZVEI proposes that it should be an optional provision if the meter may or may not have a display.   
The values for its electricity consumption are collected in the gateway or backend. A display in mobile 
applications is conceivable. Especially as there are countries that already allow this in their MID clarification.  
 
 
In detail:  
Art. 50 entry into force  
ZVEI welcomes a transposition time of 12 months after publication in OJEU and entry into force 24 months after 
publication considering a 10 years transition time for measuring instruments with a valid EU-Type approval 
certificate or national type approval certificate (e.g. MessEG/MessEV,… ). Proposal: Include explicitly a 10 year 
transition period for existing Type-approval certificates. 
 
Annex 1: Direct sales 
To be open for innovation ZVEI welcomes the deletion of “at place” for the annex for EVSE in the definition of 
transaction. Nevertheless it should be possible even if the transaction is not done at the place to have technical 
means for the integrity and authenticity of the energy use for the transaction. Consumer protection and 
reclamation possibilities should nevertheless be possible for a certain period of time after the transaction. 
 
Annex I: 8.4  
It is not clear which modification are foreseen. ZVEI recommends only use case targeted modifications that are 
considering the technological state of the art. 
 
Annex I: Adjustment of point 10.2. on the indication of results.  
Protection against accidental deletion of the measurement result is a useful addition to Annex I. 
 
Annex V electrical energy meters table 2 
ZVEI is questioning the modification as in table 2 already includes -40°C and +70°C 
Annex V electrical energy meters: deletion of points 5.1, 5.2 and 5.3 
The deletion of the subsections in section 5 would not lead to any reduction in bureaucracy or simplification for 
manufacturers. 
Annex V electrical energy meters: adjustment of point 5.4, 5.5 
It is not possible to take position as the modification foreseen are not known 
Annex V electrical energy meters: adjustment of point 4.2, 4.3 
As the specific test parameters are not clearly defined and the voltage limits in 5.4 no longer apply, this may 
lead to the necessity of new developments by manufacturers. It is uncertain what future harmonized standards 
will describe. 
Annex Va EVSE 
ZVEI welcomes a new annex for the measuring instruments used for EVSE 
Ref. Ares(2024)7421935 - 18/10/2024

 
2 
The scope shall cover all use cases. It is not the best option to split according residential, commercial and light 
industrial. In our view a split is not necessary.  
Proposal: Delete the text “ intended for residential, commercial and light industrial use.” 
Annex Va EVSE: rated operating conditions 
ZVEI recommends to align the operating conditions with them use in annex V 
Annex Va EVSE: cable replacement  
When the cables are not replaceable the seal should be a metrological seal put during the conformity 
assessment. If the cable are replaceable an installer seal on the cable connection at the energy measuring point 
is enough. In addition as an alternative to a compensation method, a 4-wire measurement should be another 
option. Proposal: delete the word “is sealed” in the second bullet point 
Annex Va EVSE: disturbance 
The written text on page 19 doesn’t enable to judge about the modification and the impact of it. This is a critical 
issue. 
Annex Va EVSE: Regarding putting into use. 
This annex defines the requirements for an EVSE measuring system. Therefore defining accuracy for the meter 
alone doesn’t make sense. The requirements of a specific class for an EVSE shall be made for the measuring 
system. In addition defining different classes for different use cases e.g. residential vs commercial. 
Define class requirements for EVSE measuring systems instead of class requirements for meter. Allow class A 
for EVSE measuring systems. It could be added that national authorities may demand stricter class 
requirements.

Subject: Response to call for evidence on the Initiative Measuring Instruments – targeted technical 
update of EU rules 
 
 
To whom this concerns, 
 
As manufacturer of flow meters that are intended for gas metering and compressed gas mobility 
applications, we embrace the initiative of the EU to extend the MID directive, with the purpose to: 
a. Adjust the requirement of Annex IV on gas meters. 
b. Harmonizing the metrological requirements for the hydrogen refilling infrastructure within 
the EU. 
 
Having read the technical input from the various WELMEC groups, Emerson would like to bring up 
some points of attention. 
 
Gas meters 
We are happy to see that next to gas meters and electronic volume conversion devices, a dedicated 
part (III) is created for energy measurement / determination.  
The points of attention we would like to bring up are: 
i. As the accuracy classes within each individual part can vary, yet influence each other, we 
wonder how this will be applied in practice. 
ii. With the emerging demand for measurement hydrogen and CO2, the current limitation of 
MID being specifically intended for fuel gas, leaves a gap in the harmonized essential 
characteristics for these non-fuel yet essential gases for the energy transition and Carbon 
Capture Utilization and Storage (CCUS). 
Harmonization of the MID annex IV with OIML R137, applying the directive to gas meters in 
general will both cover these emerging applications and provide harmonization in the 
harmonization recommendations and directive. 
iii. As hydrogen has great potential within the industrial applications (<100bar pressure), it is 
crucial that this is measured properly. Given the nature of hydrogen being extremely light, 
great volume flow rates and velocities are expected in the pipelines.  
In the current revision of MID, a 20:1 turndown ratio is expected, which is very challenging to 
meet for direct mass measuring principles. 
Harmonization of the turndown requirements between MID annex IV and OIML R137, 
allowing a rangeability from 5:1, will not exclude the direct mass measuring instruments.  
An additional advantage is harmonization of the harmonization recommendations and 
directive.

iv. The current scope of the MI-002 is residential and light industrial use; the boundary between 
light industrial and heavy industrial use however, differs per country within the EU. 
As for some countries, also large flow rates fall within MID, it is important that, mainly 
industrial providers and consumers, have possibilities to have their gas meters certified to 
the highest possible accuracy.  
For this reason, we propose to add accuracy class 0.5 to MI-002, as already implemented in 
OIML R137.  
An additional advantage is harmonization of the harmonization recommendations and 
directive. 
 
hydrogen refilling infrastructure 
We are happy to see that compressed gas dispenser will become part of the European 
harmonization. In the past few years, we have had many discussions with customers in which we had 
to explain that hydrogen dispensers were not part of MID and local legislation had to be applied. 
The points of attention we would like to bring up are: 
i. Although the process conditions like pressure, density and volume flow rate are subject 
to change throughout the complete transfer, we expect that the mass flow rate 
throughout the transfer is relatively stable.  
Limiting the turndown to at least 10:1, might not be  
As MID MI-005 and OIML R117 prescribe a minimum turndown of 5:1 to liquefied gases, 
our proposal is to bring rangeability for compressed gases and liquefied gases in-line. 
ii. We would like to emphasize that it is very important that, when talking about measuring 
systems for compressed gas dispensers for road vehicles, not only light-duty vehicles are 
taken in account. We foresee that hydrogen mobility has great potential for the heavy-
duty vehicles, such as, but not limited to trucks; trains and planes could also come in the 
scope, as electricity is less preferable for these kind of applications for practical reasons 
like weight and capacity.

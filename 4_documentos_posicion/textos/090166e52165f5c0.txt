 
Keyword: EU climate target for 2040 
  
What is it about? 
On 22 July 2025, the European Commission launched a ‘call for feedback on its initiative to amend the 
European Climate Law’. Feedback can be submitted until 15 September 2025.  
The aim of the initiative is to amend the European Climate Law to enshrine the 2040 climate target as 
a binding interim target of minus 90%. This is to ensure that the EU becomes climate neutral by 2050.  
The proposal is intended to give businesses planning security and mobilise private investment. It aims 
to strengthen competitiveness and enable a fair, technology-neutral and cost-effective transition by 
2050. The initiative follows on from a communication on the EU's 2040 climate target, which launched 
the process of setting this target.  
 
What are the proposals from the socially oriented housing industry? 
GdW supports the goal of climate neutrality. However, we reject the newly planned blanket target of a 
90% reduction in CO2 emissions by 2040, because experience shows that this will lead to political 
pressure to introduce unaffordable, uneconomical and socially unbalanced measures to reduce 
greenhouse gases. These proposals tie up enormous resources that would otherwise be channelled 
into supporting greenhouse gas reduction. 
Last but not least, unfinanceable, uneconomical and socially unbalanced proposals are demotivating 
and turn the population against them. Examples include the EU requirement for 3% of public build-
ings to be renovated to the lowest energy standard and the planned ban in Germany (admittedly very 
short-lived but far-reaching) on the operation of all fossil fuel-fired boilers over 30 years old. 
We propose replacing the fixed target of a 90% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions with measures 
that will enable a 90% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions by 2040 in a way that is financially via-
ble, economically and socially acceptable, and recommend the following amendments to the pro-
posed text: 
Article 1, second paragraph 
„This Regulation also sets out a binding measures to achieve Union target for 2040.“ 
Article 4, paragraph 3 
„3. In order to reach the climate-neutrality objective set out in Article 2(1), the binding Union 2040 cli-
mate target reduction of net greenhouse gas emissions (emissions after deduction of removals) 
by 90 % compared to 1990 levels shall should be a reduction of net greenhouse gas emissions 
(emissions after deduction of removals) by 90 % compared to 1990 levels  achieved by 2040 through 
affordable, economical and socially acceptable measures.” 
Article 4, paragraph 4 
“4. With a view to the period after 2030, the Commission shall review relevant Union legislation for 
affordability, economic viability and social acceptability in order to enable the achievement of 
the 2040 target set out in paragraph 3 of this Article and the climate-neutrality objective set out in 
Article 2(1) and consider taking the necessary measures as appropriate and based on detailed impact 
assessment, in accordance with the Treaties. 
While some enabling policies have already been implemented and their impact is  already visible, this

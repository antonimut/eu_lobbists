The City of Stockholm’s comments to the 
European Commission’s digital omnibus  
The City of Stockholm welcomes the European Commission’s 
proposal to reduce administrative burdens within EU digital 
legislation.  
From a municipal perspective, the Commission’s intention of 
harmonising EU legislation is positive. In terms of practical 
implementation, several EU regulatory frameworks are in conflict 
with one another. This creates legal uncertainty and inefficiencies 
that hinder effective governance and policy outcomes on the local 
level. This must be taken into account when harmonising EU 
legislation.  
It should also be noted that the Commission’s primarily focus on 
the needs of industry may unintentionally shift administrative 
complexity and workload onto public sector organisations. Such a 
shift could lead to increased costs and resource demands at the local 
level, counteracting the overall objective of simplification. 
The City of Stockholm therefore urges the Commission to 
systematically involve local and regional authorities throughout the 
legislative process. The local level plays a key part in the 
implementation of EU digital legislation, and a significant share of 
the associated administrative burden is carried by local 
administrations.

●  The  NIS2  Directive mandates  businesses  notify  competent  national  authorities  of  significant  cybersecurity  incidents  .  The  Implementing  Act (Articles  3  and  11)  sets  out  the  different  scenarios  which  make  an  incident  ‘significant’  and  thus  notifiable.    ●  The  GDPR requires  businesses  to  notify  the  competent  Data  Protection  Supervisory  Authorities  in  the  event  of  personal  data  breaches,  unless  there  is  unlikely  to  be  a  risk  to  the  rights  and  freedoms  of  individuals  (Article  33).  If  the  breach  is  likely  to  result  in  a  high  risk  to  the  rights  and  freedoms  of  individuals,  businesses  must  also  inform  affected  individuals  (Article  34).   ●  The  EU’s  Digital  Operational  Resilience  Act (DORA)  mandates  that  financial  entities  notify  (national  competent)  authorities  of  significant  ICT-related  incidents  (Articles  17-19) .  These  include  cybersecurity  incidents  that  could  impact  the  financial  system’s  operational  
resilience.
 
 ●  The  Directive  on  Payment  Services (PSD2)  requires  data  breaches  involving  payment  information  to  be  reported  to  the  Dutch  Central  Bank  (DNB).   These  regimes  generate  significant  overlap  and  double  reporting  at  both  the  EU  and  
national
 
level.

                                                                                          
   
 
PHOENIX group contribution to the  
European Commission’s Call for Evidence on 
Digital Omnibus 
13 October 2025 
 
PHOENIX group welcomes the Commission’s initiative aimed at setting digital simplification 
agenda for the years to come and bringing immediate relief to businesses across the EU. 
PHOENIX group is the largest European integrated healthcare provider with 224 sites in the 
business areas of pharmaceutical wholesale and pre-wholesale across 29 European 
countries and with more than 3,200 pharmacies in 17 European countries.  
 
With the ever-increasing digitalization, we rely heavily on the EU digital policy to set the right 
context for ensuring patient safety and access to medicines in the EU. That said, we strongly 
believe that some EU digital rules should be revised to simplify administrative procedures 
whilst maintaining highest levels of patient safety and access in the world. Therefore, we 
propose: 
1. Simplifying rules on cookies and tracking technologies 
 
Current cookie consent mechanisms in the EU are overly complex and lead to a 
suboptimal patient experience. PHOENIX group argues for seeking an appropriate 
balance between compliance with the current data protection requirements and 
providing a patient-friendly interface. 
 
Against this background, we call on the Commission to use Digital Omnibus to clarify 
ruling of the Administrative Court Hannover (judgement, March 19, 2025, Ref. 10 A 
5385/22) concerning the use of Google Track Manager (GTM) without prior consent. 
It is our understanding that, based on the court ruling, website operators might need 
to reconfigure how tags are deployed or consider server-side tagging approaches. 
These solutions require quite expensive code patches, hence putting additional 
burden on businesses offering their products and services online. We need a 
harmonized, EU view on the issue to ensure data protection conform and easily 
accessible platform solutions for patients across the EU. 
 
2. Simplifying European Digital Identity Framework 
 
Today, PHOENIX group, as the largest pharmacy operator in the EU, often needs to 
request from patients to come down to the pharmacy for a physical ID verification by 
the pharmacist.  
 
That said, we support facilitating patient authentication for online ordering of 
prescription (Rx) medicines via range of nationally approved electronic identity 
verification methods (eID, bank ID, trusted video ID, or equivalent). However, this 
should be done only in ways that do not alter Member State competences over 
authorization, dispensing and distribution of medicines. 
Ref. Ares(2025)8676054 - 13/10/2025

                                                                                          
   
 
3. Simplifying European Data Governance Act 
PHOENIX group supports simplifying European Data Governance Act. However, with 
an important safeguard: access to e-prescriptions or patient records must not be 
interpreted as authorization to ship Rx medicines across borders. 
 
We therefore call upon the Commission to use the Digital Omnibus to draw a firm 
legal distinction between (a) access to patient records and e-prescriptions (e-Rx) for 
clinical verification from anywhere in the EU and (b) any authorization of 
pharmacists to ship medicines across borders. The Commission should further 
make explicit that nothing in the Omnibus should diminish national regulatory 
authority over access to or distribution of Rx products. 
PHOENIX group remains available for any further questions from the Commission and looks 
forward to continuous engagement on this subject matter.

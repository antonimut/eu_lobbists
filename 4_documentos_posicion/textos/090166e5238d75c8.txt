Yet

 This  matters  because  market  placement  triggers  obligations  including  technical

(Article

(Article

Treating

  Ambiguity  also  arises  when  platform-speciﬁc  adaptations,  such  as  on-device  inference

ﬂows

high-risk

  Article  72(1)  requires  providers  to  “actively  and  systematically  collect,  document  and  analyse

“immediately

7  

 Requiring  constant  connectivity  could  prevent  AI  deployment  in  exactly  the  contexts  where  it

manifests

could

across
 
providers.
  This  approach  preserves  the  intent  of  Articles  72  and  73,  accommodates  legitimate  oﬄine  
designs,
 
reduces

uncertainty,
 
and

ensuring
 
proportionate,
 
risk-based

of

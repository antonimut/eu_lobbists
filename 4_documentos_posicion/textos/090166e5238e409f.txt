Executive  Summary  
Europe’s  ability  to  lead  in  the  AI  era  depends  not  only  on  investment  and  innovation,  but  also  on  clear,  aligned,  and  proportionate  rules.  Earlier  this  year,  in  our  EU  Economic  Blueprint,  we  laid  out  a  vision  for  how  Europe  can  harness  AI  to  drive  growth  and  prosperity,  calling  for  a  tripling  of  
compute
 
capacity
 
by

deepening
 
data
 
and
 
energy
 
infrastructure,
 
and
 
streamlining
 
regulation
 
so

enables

Building  on  this  vision,  OpenAI’s  Hacktivate  AI  report -  produced  in  partnership  with  Allied  for  Startups  and  65  policy  hackathon  participants  from  governments,  startups,  academia,  and  civil

OpenSSF’s

While  open  source  software  is  an  essential  building  block  of  Europe’s  digital  infrastructure,  
non-commercial

should

Instead,

These

Within  that  framework,  OpenSSF  identifies  the  following  systemic  challenges  in  the  current  
reporting

1.  Excessive  duplication  and  fragmentation  of  reporting  requirements.  
Today,

These

NIS

The

This

Harmonising

data
 
formats
 
would

 2.  Lack  of  a  single,  coordinated  reporting  pathway.  
Entities

This

There

 3.  Downstream  responsibility  and  accountability.

(CRA)

Building  on  the  CRA  and  toward  a  Single  Reporting  Platform  
The  CRA  provides  a  strong,  harmonised  foundation  for  definitions  of  vulnerabilities,  incidents,  
and
 
reporting
 
obligations.
 
Building
 
upon
 
this,
 
the
 
Commission
 
should
 
establish
 
an
 
EU
 
Single
 
Cybersecurity
 
Reporting
 
Platform,
 
potentially
 
under
 
the
 
Cybersecurity
 
Act
 
(CSA)
 
or
 
the
 
CRA’s
 
implementing
 
framework,
 
serving
 
as
 
a
 
unified,
 
secure,
 
and
 
interoperable
 
entry
 
point
 
for
 
all
 
EU-level
 
reporting.
 
Such  a  platform  should:  
●  Apply  consistent  definitions,  thresholds,  and  reporting  formats  across  frameworks;  ●  Provide  a  single  interface  for  all  notifications,  preventing  duplicate  submissions;  ●  Include  a  clear,  transparent  list  of  competent  authorities  with  defined  escalation  paths;  
and
 ●  Enable  automated  routing  and  machine-readable  data  exchange  with  existing  CERT  and  
CSIRT
 
systems.
 
 
Relying  on  the  CRA’s  model  of  downstream  responsibility  and  introducing  a  Single  Reporting  
Platform
 
for
 
all
 
related
 
regulations
 
would:
 
●  Eliminate  duplicative  obligations  and  reduce  compliance  costs;  ●  Clarify  accountability  across  the  software  supply  chain;  ●  Improve  situational  awareness  and  coordination  among  EU  and  national  authorities;  and  ●  Safeguard  the  openness  and  sustainability  of  Europe’s  OSS  ecosystem.  
 
By  aligning  future  simplification  measures  with  the  CRA’s  principles  and  implementing  a  single,  
harmonised
 
reporting
 
interface,
 
the
 
Commission
 
can
 
achieve
 
its
 
dual
 
objective:
 
simplifying
 
compliance
 
while
 
preserving
 
high
 
cybersecurity
 
protection,
 
while
 
also
 
making
 
compliance
 
more
 
accessible
 
and
 
affordable
 
for
 
SMEs
 
and
 
ensuring
 
that
 
open
 
collaboration
 
continues
 
to
 
underpin
 
Europe’s
 
digital
 
resilience.

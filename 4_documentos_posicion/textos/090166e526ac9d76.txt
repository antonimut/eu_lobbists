 
Public Consultation: Trade measure addressing the negative trade-related 
effects of global excess capacity on the EU steel sector 
 
    Milan, 15th  December 2025 
 
We Italian producers of Steel Wire welcome the Euro pean Commission’s proposal of 7th October 
2025 defining the key elements of the new trade measure instrument announced in the Steel Metals Action 
Plan published on March 2025. Specifically, the Com mission has recognized the need of a new “highly 
effective” mechanism to protect the European steel industry and to address market distortions caused by 
global overcapacity.  
 
Since the introduction of the steel safeguard, the steel wire sector has been only partially protected 
from market distortions. Unlike other product categories for which the safeguard affects all steel gra des, 
only non-alloy wire (category 28) is included in the product scope of the measure. It should also be noted 
that both other alloy (HS 7229) and stainless wire (HS 7223) are subject to 232 US since its institution.  
 
The exclusion of other alloy steel wire (HS 7229) has led the possibility to circumvent the measure 
as shown by import data evolution. Since the introduction of the safeguard till 2024 European imports of 
non-alloy wire shrank by 20%, whereas imports of ot her-alloy steel wire rose by 55%, demonstrating 
clearly the strong substitution between the two products and the need to include other-alloy steel wire in 
the product scope. Moreover, it should be noted tha t the addition of only one alloying element, for 
example, boron, results in a change in the custom code from non-alloy steel products (HS 7217) to other-
alloy steel products (HS 7229), without changing th e product's technical and mechanical characteristics; 
thus, allowing easy switching between codes.  
 
A similar import surge has been also recorded in the case of stainless wire, with imports increasing 
by 5% in the period 2019-2024 and by almost 14% on annual basis in the first nine months of 2025.  
 
Furthermore, it should also note that there are no trade defence measures on steel wire, neither in 
the case of non-alloy and other alloy or stainless products, such as antidumping and anti-subsidy, that can 
somehow protect the industry from marked distortion caused by global overcapacity and unfair practice.  
 
We therefore urgently request to the Commission the inclusion of both other-alloy (HS 7229) and 
stainless-steel (HS 7223) wires, by adding other-alloy steel wire in category 28, given the substitution 
relation with non-alloy products, and introducing a  new category for stainless steel wires . Such 
inclusion is necessary to fully foster the effectiv eness of the new instrument in the case of steel wi res 
industry.

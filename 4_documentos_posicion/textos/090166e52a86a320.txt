CECIMO is the European Association of Manufacturing Technologies. With a primary focus on machine tools and
additive manufacturing technologies, we bring together 15 national associations representing approximately 1500
industrial enterprises in Europe (EU + UK+ EFTA + Türkiye), over 80% of which are SMEs. CECIMO covers 97% of
the total machine tool production in Europe and about 1/3worldwide. It accounts for approximately 150,000
employees and a turnover of around 25.8 billion euros in 2024.

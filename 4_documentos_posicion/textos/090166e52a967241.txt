Introduction  
Allied  For  Startups  (AFS)  welcomes  the  Commission’s  proposals  to  simplify  rules  on  AI,   
cyber
 
and
 
data
 
as
 
a
 
long-overdue
 
shift
 
from
 
“more
 
rules”
 
to
 
“better
 
rules”
 
for
 
Europe’s
 
digital
 
economy.
 
A
 
simpler
 
and
 
more
 
coherent
 
regulatory
 
framework
 
is
 
not
 
only
 
good
 
regulatory
 
practice;
 
it
 
is
 
essential
 
to
 
strengthen
 
the
 
Single
 
Market
 
and
 
build
 
a
 
more
 
competitive,
 
resilient
 
European
 
economy.
 
For  years,  startups  and  scaleups  have  warned  that  overlapping  obligations,  legal  uncertainty,  
and
 
fragmented
 
implementation
 
undermine
 
the
 
very
 
advantage
 
the
 
Single
 
Market
 
should
 
deliver:
 
the
 
ability
 
to
 
operate
 
and
 
grow
 
seamlessly
 
across
 
27
 
Member
 
States.
 
It
 
is
 
therefore
 
positive
 
to
 
see
 
the
 
Commission
 
acknowledge
 
that
 
Europe’s
 
digital
 
framework
 
needs
 
simpliﬁcation
 
rather
 
than
 
another
 
layer
 
of
 
complexity.
 
At  the  same  time,  simpliﬁcation  must  deliver  real  legal  certainty  for  companies  navigating  
the
 
startup
 
journey.
 
Several
 
elements
 
of
 
the
 
proposal
 
risk
 
replacing
 
rigid
 
obligations
 
with
 
conditional
 
or
 
unclear
 
mechanisms
 
that
 
remain
 
difﬁcult
 
for
 
startups
 
and
 
scaleups
 
to
 
operationalise
 
in
 
practice.
 
In
 
the
 
AI
 
Omnibus,
 
ﬁrm
 
timelines,
 
predictable
 
enforcement,
 
and
 
a
 
clearer
 
recognition
 
of
 
high-growth
 
companies’
 
speciﬁc
 
constraints
 
are
 
essential.
 
In
 
the
 
Digital
 
Omnibus,
 
deﬁnitions
 
and
 
workable
 
frameworks
 
are
 
critical.
 
Without
 
these,
 
there
 
is
 
a
 
risk
 
that
 
simpliﬁcation
 
remains
 
theoretical
 
rather
 
than
 
tangible.
 
The
 
Digital
 
Omnibus
 
is
 
an
 
important
 
course
 
correction,
 
but
 
its
 
success
 
will
 
depend
 
on
 
whether
 
it
 
provides
 
startups
 
with
 
the
 
clarity,
 
proportionality,
 
and
 
predictability
 
they
 
need
 
to
 
innovate
 
and
 
scale
 
in
 
Europe.
 
 
PART  1  -  AI  OMNIBUS   
Startups  are  not  SMEs
 
While  the  Omnibus  rightly  strengthens  support  for  SMEs  and  small  mid-caps,  it  largely  
overlooks

and
 
scale-ups
 
as

category.
 
High-growth

particularly

operating
 
in
 
high-risk

that

These

supervisory

deliver
 
tangible
 
results.

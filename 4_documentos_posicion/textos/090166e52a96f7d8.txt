 
Digital  Omnibus  on  AI  Consultation  Response

We

The

53

GPAI

2.  Making  AI  Act  Article  55  fit  for  the  future  
The  Omnibus  retains  Article  55's  obligations  for  GPAISR  providers  —  including  systemic  risk  
assessment,
 
incident
 
reporting,
 
and
 
cybersecurity
 
—
 
while
 
removing
 
the
 
Commission's
 
empowerment
 
to
 
give
 
codes
 
of
 
practice
 
general
 
legal
 
validity
 
(amended
 
Article
 
56(6)).
 
We
 
support
 
publishing
 
adequacy
 
assessments
 
of
 
the
 
Code,
 
but
 
note
 
that
 
the
 
final
 
GPAI
 
Code
 
of
 
Practice,
 
published
 
July
 
2025,
 
contains
 
significant
 
gaps
 
that
 
should
 
be
 
addressed
 
at
 
the
 
legislative
 
level.
 
Pre-deployment  checks  are  absent.  The  Code  of  Practice  requires  notification  of  training  
runs
 
but
 
no
 
model-specific
 
assessment
 
prior
 
to
 
deployment.
 
This
 
means
 
highly
 
capable
 
frontier
 
models
 
could
 
be
 
released
 
in
 
the
 
EU
 
without
 
the
 
AI
 
Office
 
having
 
adequate
 
opportunity
 
to
 
evaluate
 
their
 
readiness.
 
Article
 
55
 
should
 
be
 
strengthened
 
to
 
require
 
a
 
lightweight
 
but
 
meaningful
 
pre-deployment
 
dialogue
 
and
 
course
 
correction
 
for
 
GPAISR
 
models,
 
enabling
 
the
 
AI
 
Office
 
to
 
request
 
information
 
or
 
flag
 
concerns
 
before
 
market
 
placement.
 
Incident  response  readiness  is  missing.  The  Code  mandates  serious  incident  reporting  
but
 
removed
 
earlier
 
requirements
 
for
 
pre-established
 
response
 
plans,
 
which
 
is
 
standard
 
practice
 
in
 
aviation,
 
pharmaceuticals,
 
and
 
nuclear
 
sectors.
 
Article
 
55
 
should
 
require
 
GPAISR
 
providers
 
to
 
maintain
 
documented,
 
periodically
 
tested
 
incident
 
response
 
protocols,
 
not
 
merely
 
report
 
after
 
the
 
fact.
 
Transparency  provisions  are  too  weak.  Code  of  Practice  Measure  10.2  requires  
publication
 
of
 
Safety
 
and
 
Security
 
Frameworks
 
and
 
Model
 
Reports,
 
but
 
permits
 
extensive
 
redactions.
 
Article
 
55
 
should
 
establish
 
minimum
 
transparency
 
standards
 
ensuring
 
published
 
summaries
 
provide
 
substantively
 
comparable,
 
meaningful
 
information.
 
As
 
public
 
concern
 
over
 
AI
 
grows,
 
public
 
transparency
 
will
 
be
 
essential
 
to
 
ensure
 
AI
 
adoption.
 
Pour  Demain  supports  the  Digital  Omnibus's  goal  of  a  workable,  innovation-friendly  AI  Act.  
But
 
the

(1)
 
resourcing

AI

2)

GPAI

A

AI
 
at
 
scale.
 
We
 
urge

to

Conclusion 
The Digital Omnibus proposal represents a welcome eﬀort by the European Commission to simplify and 
update the EU's digital regulatory framework. By creaƟng a more coherent and streamlined set of rules, the 
proposal is aimed at reducing administraƟve and compliance burdens, while supporƟng long-term

measures would further erode the economic foundaƟons of media publishers, whilst strengthening the 
posiƟon of major technology plaƞorms, which have already consolidated signiﬁcant market power. Such an

The Digital Omnibus proposal introduces a framework that could reshape market dynamics, with major 
technology operators likely to beneﬁt from exisƟng structural assets and network eﬀects. While its intent is 
to streamline processes, it will neither substanƟally alleviate consent faƟgue nor simplify compliance 
pathways for all businesses. If implemented in its current form, the proposal will reinforce the business model

governing consent. Complementary measures should be introduced to promote interoperability, 
transparency, and support innovaƟon while granƟng fair and open market condiƟons.

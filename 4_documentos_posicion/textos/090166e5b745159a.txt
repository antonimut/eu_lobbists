Brussels	15	December	2017		CECE	feedback	on	the	Inception	Impact	Assessment	related	to	the	Road	Circulation	requirements	for	Mobile	Machinery.		CECE	is	the	recognized	organization	representing	and	promoting	the	European	construction	equipment	and	related	industries.	CECE	represents	the	interests	of	national	construction	equipment	manufacturer	associations	in	13	European	countries,	including	Germany,	the	UK,	France,	Italy,	Russia	and	Turkey.	The	sector	counts	around	1200	companies	that	employ	about	300,000	people	directly	and	indirectly.	Their	annual	revenues	amount	to	ca.	40	billion	euros.	CECE	welcomes	the	initiative	of	the	Commission	to	fill	one	of	the	remaining	gaps	in	the	single	market:	the	harmonisation	of	technical	requirements	related	to	road	circulation	aspects	of	mobile	machinery.		CECE	totally	agrees	with	the	main	policy	objective	the	legislative	initiative	to	achieve	a	single	market	for	mobile	machinery	(self-propelled	mobile	and	towed	machinery)	which	may	occasionally	circulate	on	the	public	roads.	We	welcome	that	the	initiative	will	address	the	specific	objectives	of	reducing	costs	and	administrative	burden,	removing	market	obstacles	deriving	from	different	safety	requirements	for	the	design	and	manufacturing	of	such	machinery	in	various	Member	States.		The	regulation	should	take	into	account	the	complexity	of	our	sector	with	a	multitude	of	machine	types	(also	in	a	multitude	of	sizes)	and	quantities	produced.		For	mobile	machinery,	occupational	safety	aspects	are	already	covered	by	the	machinery	directive	and	we	believe	that	this	approach	should	be	used	for	a	new	regulation	on	road	circulation	aspects.	CECE	agrees	with	the	proposed	objections	and	policy	options	and	CECE	is	of	the	opinion	that	the	first	two	options	(do	nothing	and	the	options	of	improving	implementation	and	enforcement	of	existing	legislation	or	doing	less/simplifying	existing	legislation)	would	not	lead	to	better	regulation.	CECE	prefers	the	alternative	policy	approach	based	on	a	"New	approach"	harmonised	regulation	for	the	whole	approval	of	mobile	machinery	via	the	“New	Legislative	Framework”	(NLF).	CECE	is	not	in	favour	of	having	only	system	approvals.

The	new	regulation	will	provide	a	high	level	of	safety	throughout	the	whole	EU.	CECE	believes	that	most	aspects	of	the	road	circulation	technical	requirements	can	be	handled	and	tested	by	the	manufacturers	without	the	need	for	third-party	intervention.	According	to	CECE,	only	safety	critical	elements	installed	in	the	mobile	machines	may	require	third	party	verification.	For	CECE,	the	safety	critical	aspects	are:	braking	and	steering	performance	of	the	mobile	machine.	Any	overlap	with	existing	legislation	should	be	avoided	and	the	legislator	should	make	sure	that	mobile	machinery	fulfilling	the	EU	technical	requirements	of	this	regulation	are	no	longer	subject	to	any	further	technical	requirements	for	road	circulation	in	the	member	states.

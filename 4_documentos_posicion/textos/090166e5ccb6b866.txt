Transposition de Bâle IV en Europe 
 
AXES DE LOBBYING du groupe SFIL  
 
Under the SA for credit risk, the risk weight applicable to RGLAs (Regional Governments or Local Authorities – 
CRR, Article 115) and PSEs (Public Sector Entities – CRR, Article 116) is not harmonis ed at the level of the 
European Union. In the objective of setting -up an harmonized framework and ensuring European RGLAs and 
PSEs are treated on an equal footing as regard access to funding, it is of the utmost importance to review the 
existing lists of exceptions defined by the EBA in connection with Articles 115 and 116 of the CRR, so as to 
grant a 0 % risk weight under the Standardise d Approach to a larger scope of RGLA and PSE counterparties 
(PROPOSAL 1). Among the 4 proposals elaborated on in this document, the first one is the most in line with 
the general objective of fostering greater integration within the European Union. It shal l however in our 
opinion be complemented by 3 additional proposals.  
 
Moreover, it shall be noted that the nearly unchanged treatment of sovereign exposures, combined with the 
remaining reforms of the A -IRB, which includes the removal of the A -IRB approach for PSEs and RGLAs, would 
lead to a deep discrepancy in the treatment of entities of the same nature: depending on their treatment 
under the SA (classification as exposures to CGCB or as Institutions), the risk weight granted would be 
completely different under IRB approaches. For those classified as institutions exposures, a significant increase 
in RWAs amounts is to be expected: the application of the SA approach, or even worse, the F -IRB approach 
with a LGD value of 45 % for counterparties benefitting f rom model LGDs usually significantly lower than 5 % 
would be huge.  
 
So as to avoid such undesirable effects and in addition to PROPOSAL 1, it is of the utmost importance that the 
use of the IRB approach be permitted as regard RGLAs / PSEs exposures, if possible both:  
 An A-IRB approach (PROPOSAL 2), as it is currently the case. This is the solution proposed by the EBA 
in its recent report dated 02/08/19 ("Policy advice on the Basel III reforms: credit risk") and it appears 
adequate: the creation of a new dedicated exposure category for PSEs and RGLAs, which would 
remain eligible for the application of the A -IRB approach. Under the A -IRB approa ch, the LGD input 
floor should be lower than a 5% level and remain between 1% and 3% (current input floor depending 
on the country); and  
 A F-IRB approach ( PROPOSAL 3). If such a proposal is retained, in addition to the creation of a new 
dedicated exposure category for PSEs and RGLAs, the value of the LGD shall be reviewed: under F -IRB 
approach, the LGD value should not be set above 5 %.  
 
Even if either PROPOSAL 2 and/or PROPOSAL 3 is eventually retained, the European -specific business model of 
Public Development Bank would be disproportionally impacted by the Output Floor due to the significant 
exposure of such banks to RGLAs and PSEs. Giv en the Output Floor is a backstop, just like the Leverage Ratio, it 
seems of the utmost importance for Public Development Banks that the Leverage Ratio exemptions 
specified under CRR2 (Article 429a) as regards the following exposures be extended to the Out put Floor 
(PROPOSAL 4):  
 Where the credit institution is a public development credit institution, the exposures arising from 
assets that constitute claims on central governments, regional governments, local authorities or 
public sector entities in relation to public sector investments and promotional loans;  
 Where the institution is not a public development credit institution, the parts of exposures arising 
from passing-through promotional loans to other credit institutions; 
 The guaranteed parts of exposure s arising from export credits that meet both of the following 
conditions 
o The guarantee is provided by an eligible provider of unfunded credit protection in 
accordance with Articles 201 and 202 (of CRR2), including by export credit agencies or by 
central governments; 
o A 0 % risk weight applies to the guaranteed part of the exposure in accordance with Article 
114(2) or (4) or Article 116(4) (of CRR2)

Object Finance 
 
 
 Financial structure strengths 
•  Repayment Profile: 
- Full pay-out profile, or, in case there is a residual balloon amount (upon 
loan maturity), this amount should be either commensurate with average historical 
asset values, or with recourse or employment to a creditworthy counterparty 
or 
-  Age of the asset upon full pay-out of the loan (including balloon amortization) 
not greater than 2/3rd of asset overall economic lifespan, e.g. ~17 years for a 25-
year life, 
 
•  Secured Cashflows (rental, lease, time-charter) or Recourse: 
- Secured Cashflows in excess of break-even (operating costs + debt service) 
with a creditworthy lessee/chartering group/counterparty. 
or 
- Recourse on a creditworthy counterparty as evidenced by external or 
internal assessment (credit quality step 4 or above), save for commensurate 
residual balloon amounts 
 Asset liquidity 
 
- Value / Appraisal by external independent market expert, at least once a 
year, monitored by the institution 
- Lenders have the benefit of an enforceable first-ranking security (or equivalent) 
- Asset built by a recognized manufacturer / ship builder 
- If asset has the benefit of a very strong employment or is operated by a very 
strong owner/ operator, then liquidity requirement is deemed acceptable. 
 
 External equity, when applicable, brought by experienced sponsor

Project Finance 
 
 
 High quality projects criteria are too restrictive and would not enable to identify high 
quality projects. Hereunder is a proposal of amendments to paragraphs 47 and 48 of d424 
Dec 2017 BIII text: 
• 47. “… Project finance exposures will be risk-weighted at 110% during the pre-
operational phase and 75% during the operational phase. For this purpose, 
operational phase is defined as the phase in which the entity that was specifically 
created to finance the project or which is economically comparable, has (i) positive 
net cash flows to be generated over the  asset life, that are sufficient to cover any 
remaining contractual obligation, and (ii) the project is operating. Project finance 
exposures which are deemed to be high quality, as described in paragraph 48, will be 
risk weighted at 75% in the pre-operational phase and at 50% during the operational 
phase. 
•  48. A high quality project finance exposure refers to an exposure to a project 
finance entity either in the pre-operational phase or in the operational phase, that will 
be able to meet its financial commitments in a timely manner and its ability to do so is 
assessed to be robust against adverse changes in the economic cycle and business 
conditions. 
 The following conditions must also be met: 
• If the project is in pre-operational phase, lenders benefit from satisfactory 
due diligences on construction risk, with notably experienced and credit 
worthy constructors providing adequate protections in case of delay or 
underperformance. 
• The project finance entity is restricted from acting to the detriment of the 
creditors (e.g. by not being able to issue additional debt without the consent 
of existing creditors, which can be provided through pre -approval of  
indebtedness increases expressed in fixed amount or covenants / cover 
ratios thresholds); 
• The project finance entity has sufficient reserve funds or other financial 
arrangements to cover the contingency funding and working capital 
requirements of the project;

• The revenues are availability -based or subject to a rate -of-return 
regulation or take -or-pay contract or over 85% of the revenues are 
regulated or contractually fixed and the project is resilient to downside 
sensitivities regarding price and/or volume risk (like variable production as it 
is the case for renewable assets). 
• The project finance entity’s revenue depends on one main counterparty and 
this main counterparty shall be a central government, PSE or a corporate entity 
with a risk weight of 80% or lower; in case of regulated tariff with a pass-
through off- taker, this one has a RW of 100% or lower. 
• The contractual provisions governing the exposure to the project finance 
entity provide for a high degree of protection for creditors in case of a 
default of the project finance entity; 
• The main counterparty or other counterparties which similarly comply with 
the eligibility criteria for the main counterparty will protect the creditors 
from the losses resulting from a termination of the off-take contract where 
appropriate (in case of a regulated tariff with a pass -through offtaker for 
the sale of the production at market price , no termination amount would 
be needed). 
• All assets and contracts necessary to operate the project (to the extent 
permitted by applicable law), and/or the shares of the entity owning the 
assets, have been pledged to the creditors; and 
• Creditors may assume control of the project finance entity in case of its 
default.

Commodities Finance  
High Quality Criteria  
A. Liquidity  
Deliverable included: the underlying commodity can be sold to a commodity exchange or is 
quoted in liquid and tradable markets. 
Requirement deliverable: Underlying commodity matches the delivery requirements of the 
relevant exchange (for instance metals like aluminium, copper, zinc, nickel etc.) or the 
specifications of other liquid and tradable markets (OTC markets, brokers). 
  
B. Price Risk Mitigation 
1. Derivatives tied into the transaction. Hedge: the price risk of the commodity is hedged via 
derivatives (e.g. futures), with the hedge having matching price terms. 
Example: The borrower has a short future in place as a hedge against the price risk of a 
commodity inventory (long) position. 
Or 
2. Hedge via pre-sold goods: the underlying commodity is pre -sold by the borrower to an off 
taker. 
Example: Against a commodity inventory position, the borrower has a signed fixed price sales 
contract with his customer. In case such sales contract has a floating price (not yet fixed), the 
borrower has an additional derivative in place to hedge against the price risk of the sales 
contract. 
  
C. Country Risk 
Exposure to country risk (in particular, offshore location of reserves in an emerging country) 
Relevant country has adequate legal score [consistent with defined General Bank Policy, 
including external sourced input such as World Bank and/or OECD data 
 
D. Counterparty Risk 
Limited counterparty risk: Receivables from investment grade related counterparty or 
receivables supported by credit instrument (LC, SBLC, Credit Insurance, Guarantee) issued 
by investment grade counterparty 
 
E. Control 
For the financing of the goods : goods held to the order of the bank (Bills of Lading or 
Warehouses receipts or other form of control such as freight forwarder receipt, release 
mechanism, non-discharge confirmation).  
For the financing of receivables: receivables payable in an account with (or under the control 
of) the lender and monitored by the bank for prompt payment on the due date 
For the financing of Borrowing Bases Facilities: Borrowing Base Facilities whereby the goods 
are stored in third party warehouses or monitoring is done through a third party with release 
controls by the bank

Monitoring Criteria 
Transaction/Facility is frequently monitored by a separate monitoring unit in the bank (which 
can be part of the first line). The monitoring function meets the following criteria: 
i. Monitoring frequency at least monthly 
ii. Cash collections and development of the Economic Position of a customer (within 
(sub)limits and defined and agreed advance rates) 
iii. Performance of underlying transactions in line with pre -agreed modus operandi and 
tenors 
iv. Monitoring of inventory positions financed, including price risk management and 
associated hedges 
v. Monitoring and tracking of relevant documents, such as: Letters of Cr edit; Bills of 
Lading or other documents evidencing good such as warehouse receipts or 
transportation documents; Broker statements for hedging; insurance policies etc.

Commercial Real Estate 
 
 
 
  Financial structure strengths 
• The obligation is senior to all other claims other than statutory claims and claims 
from derivatives counterparties with appropriate inter creditor agreements 
• ICR > 1.25 with good tenant quality, notably for single asset financing (investment 
grade) 
 
 
  Asset quality 
o In case of a single asset financing: 
 
• Location: Property is located in desirable established location regarding services 
offered by it to its users (tenants / clients) (ease of access, transportation, energy 
and communication networks, distance to key public equipment or sought -after 
area, neighbourhood overall characteristics, …) 
 
• Property features: Property is favoured by tenants / clients / investors due to its 
external design,  internal configuration, and maintenance status, and is either new or 
recent (<= 10 years) or, if old, remains competitive with new properties. 
 
 
o In case of a portfolio financing: 
 
• Good quality assets in a diversified portfolio: e.g. on a geographical basis or in terms 
of number of tenants or tenant revenue granularity. The assets may not be prime as 
this is mitigated by the diversification of the portfolio.

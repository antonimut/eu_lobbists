II – High quality criteria  
The lowest RW would apply to a “High Quality” category for the different Specialized Lending asset 
class, taking into account the structures and mitigants to the different risks, as well as the quality of 
the assets financed and cover ratios. The objective criteria defined for the different asset classes  are 
detailed below

Object Finance 
 
Financial structure strengths  
• Repayment Profile:  
- Full pay-out profile, or, in case there is a residual balloon amount (upon loan maturity), this 
amount should be either commensurate with average historical asset values, or with 
recourse or employment to a creditworthy counterparty 
or 
- Age of the asset upon full pay -out of the loan (including balloon amortization) not greater 
than 2/3rd of asset overall economic lifespan, e.g. ~17 years for a 25-year life,  
 
• Secured Cashflows (rental, lease, time-charter) or Recourse:   
- Secured Cashflows in excess of break -even (operating costs + debt service) with a 
creditworthy lessee/chartering group/counterparty.  
or 
- Recourse on a creditworthy counterparty as evidenced by external or internal assessment 
(credit quality step 4 or above), save for commensurate residual balloon amounts  
Asset liquidity 
- Value / Appraisal by external independent market expert, at least once a year, monitored by 
the institution 
- Lenders have the benefit of an enforceable first-ranking security (or equivalent) 
- Asset built by a recognized manufacturer / ship builder 
- If asset has the benefit of a very strong employment or is operated by a very strong owner/ 
operator, then liquidity requirement is deemed acceptable.  
 
External equity, when applicable, brought by experienced sponsor

Project Finance 
 
• High quality projects criteria are too restrictive and would not enable to identify high quality 
projects. Hereunder is a proposal of amendments to paragraphs 47 and 48 of d424 Dec 2017 BIII 
text:  
- 47. “… Project finance exposures will be risk -weighted at 110% during the pre -operational 
phase and 75% during the operational phase. For this purpose, operational phase is defined 
as the phase in which the entity that was specifically created to finance the project or which 
is economically comparable, has (i) positive net cash flow s to be generated over the asset 
life, that are sufficient to cover any remaining contractual obligation, and (ii) the project is 
operating. Project finance exposures in the operational phase which are deemed to be high 
quality, as desc ribed in paragraph 48, will be risk weighted at 75% in the pre -operational 
phase and at 50% during the operational phase.  
-  48. A high quality project finance exposure refers to an exposure to a project finance entity 
either in the pre-operational phase or in the operational phase, that will be able to meet its 
financial commitments in a timely manner and its ability to do so is assessed to be robust 
against adverse changes in the economic cycle and business conditions.  
• The following conditions must also be met: 
- If the project is in pre-operational phase, lenders benefit from satisfactory due diligences on 
construction risk, with notably experienced and credit worthy constructors providing 
adequate protections in case of delay or underperformance.  
- The project finance entity is restricted from acting to the detriment of the creditors (e.g. by 
not being able to issue additional debt without the consent of existing creditors, which can 
be provided through pre -approval of indebtedness increases expressed in fix ed amount or 
covenants / cover ratios thresholds);  
- The project finance entity has sufficient  reserve funds or other financial arrangements to 
cover the contingency funding and working capital requirements of the project;  
- The revenues are [mainly] availability-based or subject to a rate-of-return regulation or take-
or-pay contract or part of the revenues are regulated or contractually fixed and the project 
is resilient to downside sensitivities regarding price and/or volume risk (like variable 
production as it is the case for renewable assets).  
- The project finance entity’s revenue depends on one main counterparty and this main 
counterparty shall be a central government, PSE or a corporate entity with a risk weight of 
80% or lower; in case of regulated tariff with a pass-through off-taker, this one has a RW of 
100% or lower.  
- The contractual provisions governing the exposure to the project finance entity provide for 
a high degree of protection for creditors in case of a default of the project finance entity;  
- The main counterparty or other counterparties which similarly comply with the eligibility 
criteria for the main counterparty will protect the creditors from the losses resulting from a 
termination of the project off-take contract where appropriate (where there is a market or

Commodities Finance  
 
High Quality Criteria  
A. Liquidity  
Deliverable included: the underlying commodity can be sold to a commodity exchange or is quoted in 
liquid and tradable markets. 
Requirement deliverable: underlying commodity matches the delivery requirements of the relevant 
exchange (for instance metals like aluminium, copper, zinc, nickel etc. 
B. Price Risk Mitigation 
1. Derivatives tied into the transaction. Hedge: the price of the commodity is hedged either via 
derivatives (e.g. futures) or via a sales contract with customer (at fixed price or same formula as the 
purchase contract), with future proceeds against matching price terms. 
Example: the borrower either has a (short) future in place as a hedge for the price risk of the underlying 
commodity, or it has a fixed price sales contract in place with its customer. 
Or 
2. Pre-sold goods on matchi ng price terms and or fixed price. Pre -sold: the underlying commodity is 
pre-sold by the borrower to an off taker 
Example: the borrower has a signed sales contract with his customer. This can be either at a fixed price 
of floating price 
C. Country Risk 
Exposure to country risk (in particular, offshore location of reserves in an emerging country) 
Relevant country has adequate legal score [consistent with general bank policy, including external 
sourced input such as World Bank and/or OECD data]  
D. Counterparty Risk 
Limited counterparty risk: Receivables from investment grade related counterparty or receivables 
supported by credit instrument (LC, SBLC, Credit Insurance, Guarantee) issued by investment grade 
counterparty 
E. Control 
For the financing of the goods : goods held to the order of the bank (Bills of Lading or Warehouses 
receipts or other form of control such as freight forwarder receipt, release mechanism, non-discharge 
confirmation).  
For the financing of Receivables: receivables payable in an account with (or under the control of) the 
lender and monitored by the bank for prompt payment on the due date 
For the financing of Borrowing Bases Facilities: Borrowing Base Facilities whereby the goods are stored 
in third party warehouses or monitoring is done through a third party with release controls by the bank

Commercial Real Estate 
 
 
Financial structure strengths  
• The obligation is senior to all other claims other than statutory claims and claims from 
derivatives counterparties with appropriate inter creditor agreements 
• ICR > 1.25   with good tenant quality, notably for single asset financing (investment grade) 
when the asset is already income -producing OR minimum level of equity / pre -sales/ pre-
lettings partially or fully covering the market risk when the asset is still under construction 
 
Asset quality 
• In case of a single asset financing:  
- Location: Property is located in desirable established location regarding services offered 
by it to its users (tenants / clients) (ease of access, transportation, energy and 
communication networks, distance to key public equipment or sought -after area, 
neighbourhood overall characteristics, …) 
 
- Property features: Property is favoured by tenants / clients / investors due to its external 
design, internal configuration, and maintenance status, and is either new or recent (<= 10 
years) or, if old, remains competitive with new properties.  
 
• In case of a portfolio financing:  
Good quality assets in a diversified portfolio: e.g. on a geographical basis or in terms of 
number of tenants or tenant revenue granularity. The assets may not be prime as this is 
mitigated by the diversification of the portfolio.  
 
• AND for assets under construction only:  
Well mitigated construction risk with either a completion guarantee provided by an 
investment grade counterpart, or a construction done by an investment grade 
constructor, with a satisfactory construction contract

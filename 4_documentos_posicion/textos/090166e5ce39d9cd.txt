Please find below our main recommendations for an optimised Waste Shipment Regulation:
 
 
1- A uniform interpretation of definitions throughout the EU
 
 
2- Easier, quicker and more harmonized pre-consent and consent procedures
 
 
3- Making use of digital tools to increase efficiency and decrease costs
 
 
4- Decreasing the cost of financial guarantees
 
 
5- Differentiating between unintentional errors and wrongdoings
 
 
6- More flexibility for shipments of waste explicitly destined for laboratory analysis
 
 
7- Alleviating the potential effects of Brexit on secondary raw material markets

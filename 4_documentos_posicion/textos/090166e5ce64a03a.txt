Inception Impact Assessment : Revision  of the Industrial Emissions Directive 
 
Eucopro comments 
 
 
Eucopro – the European Association for Co -processing is composed of waste pre-treatment 
companies that prepare alternat ive fuels and raw material s, mainly from hazardous wastes, with the 
aim of recovering them in co-processing processes, such as cement kilns. 
 
Members of Eucopro operate plants located in sev eral EU countries including Belgium, France, 
Germany, Greece, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Poland, and Romania.  
 
Due to  its industrial activities, Eucopro has been involved for more than 15 years in the major 
regulatory issues related to the Industrial Emissions Directive (IED), notably in the work that led to the 
transition from the IPPC Directive to the IED. Eucopro is a member of the IED forum article 13 and has 
been part of group of experts for several Bref, as the waste treatment Bref.  
 
 
In the context of this consultation, the following points seem important to EUCOPRO: 
 
1. As mentioned in the IIA document, the Directive 2010/75/EU on industrial emissions (the IED) is 
the main EU instrument regulating pollutant emissions from industr ial installations in order to 
achieve a high level of protection of the environment.  
 
Eucopro considers that IED is a very efficient too l and that its requirements are adequate and 
sufficient to ensure the protection of the environment, including linked he alth and safety impa ct. In 
this context, we consider that IED should remain as stable as possible. 
 
However, some improvements may be engaged, mainly focussed on the BREF procedures. 
Eucopro also asks to better monitor the implementation of the IED provi sions in Member States , in 
order to prevent possible discrepancies in the directive application. 
 
2. Inclusion of additional sector. 
 
Eucopro has no particular opinion on this, but that should be based on deeply impact studies. 
  
3. Implementation issues  
 
As mentioned in point 1, c onsistency of Member State s implementation of IED requirements are 
needed. Some BAT conclusions may be subject ed to interpretation by the Member States for 
their implementation. Difficulties of understanding and/or technical implementation of certain 
provisions could also occur. 
 
As a first step, Eucopro suggests  developing exchanges in the Bref procedure framewor k, 
involving, according the topic, both the TWG experts and/or the IEF article 13 forum. 
  
As illustration, after the BAT conclusions publication, regu lar feedback meetings could be 
organized between TWG members. Such exchanges would both  reach to resolve que stions and 
also to identify for the next review of the Bref the subjects that would require it.  
 
Such a proposal is not a matter of provisions s pecific to the Directive, but rather of 
methodological provision related to the BREF process.

4. BREF process 
 
Eucopro followe d both the initial elaboration in 2006 of the Waste Treatment BREF and its 
revision, which ended on August 10, 2018, which was  totally conducted under the IED directive 
requirements. Compared to the first elaboration procedure, Eucopro have seen many 
improvements both in the working process and on the quality of exchanges. These concern 
especially the working methodology which in cludes now, when it is necessary working dedicated 
groups, intermediate exchange meetings…. The  data collection processing relat ing to water and 
air emissions has been great improved. 
 
However, improvements of the Bref procedure could be envisaged mainly on methodology 
aspects, such as asking more details during the data collection of information. 
 
The review process could be also improved to focus on the real need or not of additional “BAT” 
compared to those already existing.  The Eucopro suggestion made in point 3 could help to 
develop such procedures. Procedure adaptation could be done by guidelines. 
 
5.  Eucopro considers that sectorial BREF and their linked BAT conclusions chapter should be the 
main place to better integrate energy efficiency and circular economy issues, as such potential 
additional requirements  are  linked with each industrial sector and processes. 
 
 The IIA also mentioned that weaknesses have been identified on themes such as access to 
information, participation in decision making and access to justice. Eucopro considers that most 
of these issues are already treated in many EU reg ulation and/or international Convention, such 
as Arhus Convention, and that additional burden and overlaps should be avoided.  
.  
6. In the spirit of better regulation principles, Eucopro asks to maintain the exclusion of CO2 
emissions from the s cope of IED , as mentioned in article 9 (1)  of the directive, to avoid overlap , 
with other s EU legisl ation such as EU ETS.  IED should be maintain ed as a tool dedicated to 
preventing local negative impact and should not become an additional tool to reduce CO2 
emissions in the industry. 
 
7.  Eucopro agrees on the need to improve coherence between IED with the E -PRTR Regulation  
(EC) 166/2006, as mentioned in the IIA document

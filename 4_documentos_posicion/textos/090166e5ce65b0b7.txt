 Inception Impact Assessment for Revision of the Industrial Emissions Directive  Ref. Ares (2020)1738021 - 24/03/2020   Thank you for this opportunity to give our comments to Commission’s plan in its early stage. FinnMin has reservations with the Commission suggestion that, as part of the planned revision of the IED, it might be advantageous to include mining sector in the IED. We do not share the view that IED framework and processes related would make any additional value if extractive industries would be included. Our understanding is based on: - Mining industry is experiencing each mineral deposit different and not as homogeneous  - Each mining operation is deeply connected to geological, geochemical, climatological and social conditions of the location; - We have around 40 mining operations in Finland with 40 different processes, technologies and challenges; - While remembering that each member state has an autonomy over its natural resources mining industry is at the same time fully regulated by EU and national laws and statutes; - The BAT Reference Document for the Management of Waste from Extractive Industries (2018) tells us that it is difficult if not impossible to compare the different situation in different Member States as every deposit is geologically quite unique.  - Together with our mining industry we have developed mining technology which is globally well-known and in use. It’s difficult to evaluate what would be the consequences for the innovation policy of this particular industries if IED would be in use. Finnish Mining Association  Pekka Suomela Executive Officer

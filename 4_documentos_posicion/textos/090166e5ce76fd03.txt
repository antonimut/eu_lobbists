About ETRMA 
The European Tyre & Rubber Manufacturers Association (ETRMA) represent nearly 4.400 companies in the EU, directly 
employing about 370.000 people. The global sales of ETRMA’s tyre corporate members represent 70% of total global 
sales and 7 out of 10 world leaders in the global tyre sector are ETRMA Members1. ETRMA members have a strong 
manufacturing, as well as Research & Development presence within the EU and candidate countries, with 93 tyre-
producing plants and 17 R&D centres. 
Feedback  
Sustainable and smart mobility requires a comprehensive and integrated approach. The tyre industry has 
invested in sustainable choices throughout the life cycle of the tyre; leading to reduce CO2 and noise 
emissions during the production and tyre use stage, as well as, tyre weight. The development of Tyres-as-a-
Service, which fuses innovative telematics solutions, has additional potential to improve air quality and traffic 
management.  
ETRMA welcomes the combined evaluation roadmap / Inception Impact Assessment towards the 
development of post-Euro 6/VI emission standards for cars, vans, lorries and buses. ETRMA supports the 
transition to a climate-neutral, resource-efficient and competitive European economy. The clean air in 
Europe is of utmost importance to ensure healthy living conditions for our citizens and enable the move 
towards an overall zero-polluted society.  
In this regard, it is beneficial for industry and policy makers to work together, in a timely manner, to meet 
the demand for unhampered move of people and goods in the European Green Deal context. This should be 
done in a mutual understanding of priorities under the unexpected and unprecedented challenge of 
coronavirus crisis.  
ETRMA stands ready to support the decarbonisation goal of 2050 and at the same time reinforce Europe’s 
competitive position in field of road transport. Safety should go hand in hand with the mitigation of climate 
change.  
Moving safely around in a clean environment will pave and safeguard the future of next generations.

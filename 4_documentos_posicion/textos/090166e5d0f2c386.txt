 
 
 
 
 
Proposed amendment to ECE R110 
 
UNECE R110 defines working pressure for an LNG tank to be the primary relief valve setting, 
without stating a minimum setting of said relief valve. UNECE R110 only states that the maximum 
allowable working pressure shall be less than 26 MPa. We believe that this stat ement is irrelevant 
and that it has been mixed up with the working pressure of a CNG tank. 26 MPa is not relevant for 
LNG tanks. Instead a minimum opening pressure of the primary relief valve should be stated.  
 
The Swedish Gas Association believe that this is a safety risk as well as a climate issue because of 
the probability that there will be vehicles that have safety valves who open at a lower pressure than 
the fueling station is set to stop the fuelling for. This is fundamental information for those who  build 
and operate the fueling stations and should be regulated in UNECE R110. We simply want to make 
sure that vehicles are not filled in a way that the primary safety valve opens during fuelling. To 
prevent this we need to know at what pressure the primary relief valve opens.  
 
The maximum allowable pressure for CNG tanks is stated in UNECE R110, so we see no problem 
stating a lowest opening pressure for the primary safety relief valve on LNG tanks. Also, the ECE 
R67 (concerning vehicles using liquefied petroleum gases in their propulsion system) are stating 
specific opening pressures of the relief valves. 
 
With the above in mind, we push the issue of a change in the regulations UNECE R110 where a 
minimum pressure for the primary safety relief valve's opening pressure is specified to 1.6 MPa. On 
the LNG trucks currently commercialized in Europe the opening pressure for the primary relief 
valve is around 1.6 MPa. Therefore, our proposal for amendment is as follows:  
 
Proposed amendment to ECE R110 
 
4.Definitions 
4.4."Working  pressure"  means  the  maximum  pressure  to  which  a  component  is designed  to  
be  subjected  to  and  which  is  the  basis  for  determining  the strength of the component under 
consideration. For CNG cylinder, the settled pressure  of  20  MPa  at  a  uniform  temperature  of  
15 °C.  For  LNG  tank,  the pressure of the LNG tank primary relief valve setting shall be at least 
1.6 MPa. 
 
Annex 3B 
2.2.Maximum pressure 
The  maximum allowable  working pressure  (MAWP) shall be  defined by the manufacturer and 
correspond to the nominal primary relief valve setting. The maximum allowable working pressure 
shall be less than 26 MPa at least 1.6 MPa.

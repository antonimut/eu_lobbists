Neste Reply to Waste Shipment Regulation – Public 
Consultation on the review of EU rules 
 
 
 
Neste Corp. (www.neste.com) is the world’s largest producer of renewable diesel refined from waste and 
residues, introducing renewable solutions also to the polymers and chemicals industries. Our business is 
focused on combating climate change and driving circular economy, and we are committed to becoming a 
solution provider in chemical recycling by providing cost-effective and sustainable innovations. To showcase 
this commitment, Neste has pledged to work towards chemically recycling more than 1 million tons of plastic 
waste annually from 2030 onwards. 
 
Neste supports the development of an EU legislative framework that enables the development of a recycling 
ecosystem and value chains through a safe and dynamic market for waste to secondary raw materials. The 
smooth shipment of plastic waste for recycling purposes within the EU is key to facilitate the successful 
transition to a circular economy. 
 
The Waste Shipment Regulation should promote a European network of innovative technologies for recycling 
valuable wastes within Europe, avoiding waste exports that result both in negative environmental and health 
impacts in the countries of destination. 
 
For the seamless functioning of the European recycling ecosystem, any cross -border administrative burden 
should be removed that limits the circulation of waste streams destined for recycling throughout Europe. 
Ensuring access to a stable, abundant and affordable feedstock is a prerequisite for industry, including Neste, 
to scale up innovative solutions, such as chemical recycling technologies, supporting the circular economy.

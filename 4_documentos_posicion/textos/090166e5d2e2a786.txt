LEAF (Linking Environment And Farming) Response to  
European Commission’s Inception Impact Assessment: 
“Empowering the consumer for the green transition” 
 
LEAF (Linking Environment And Farming) welcomes the Commission’s aim to support consumers 
choosing more environmentally responsible purchasing options based on reliable, robust 
information. This is increasingly critical with the impact of climate change and Covid-19. 
LEAFi is a leading global organisation delivering more sustainable food and farmingii. We work with 
farmers, the food industry, scientists and consumers, to inspire and enable more sustainable farming 
that is prosperous, enriches the environment and engages local communities.  
LEAF Marqueiii is an environmental assurance systemiv recognising more sustainably farmed 
products. It is based on LEAF’s Integrated Farm Management principles and applies across the whole 
farm business. The intended impacts of LEAF Marque are to improve: 
 Soil management to enhance soil quality and soil health 
 The resilience of cropping systems 
 Management of water use and water quality 
 Energy efficiency and energy use 
 Waste management 
 Management of livestock to enhance the environment 
 And enhance the management of native habitats and biodiversity. 
LEAF Marque certification is third party verified by LEAF Marque approved and accredited 
Certification Bodies (CBs). All LEAF Marque inspections are carried out independently, on an annual 
basis. Farm businesses are expected to comply with all relevant regulatory requirements, existing 
national and/or international laws and regulations as well as being a certified member of a LEAF 
Marque approved baseline certification system for each product (for example GlobalG.A.P.). 
LEAF Marque is a Full Member of the ISEAL Alliance; this shows our commitment to the Credibility 
Principles and demonstrates our compliance with Codes of Good Practice in setting standards, 
assuring compliance and monitoring impacts.  
Through our work with ISEAL, LEAF Marque helps to demonstrate its commitment to rigour, 
transparency, efficiency, robustness and credibility. Whilst this applies at all stages of LEAF Marque’s 
work, this is certainly true for our work in claims and labellingv. LEAF Marque logo and claims can be 
used on final product under Licence Agreement. LEAF Marque is also able to support businesses with 
their own claims as a ‘white label’ system, allowing brands to make claims which can be 
substantiated through LEAF Marque. 
LEAF Marque is recognised by a wide range of organisations for its ability to deliver. This includes the 
States of Jersey Government, England’s Environment Agency, Food for Life catering mark, retailers 
(e.g. Waitrose, Marks & Spencer, Tesco), and brands (e.g. Jordans Farm Partnership, Farrington’s 
Oils, Florette). 43% of UK fruit and vegetables are grown by LEAF Marque certified businesses.  
The collective environmental impactvi of LEAF Marque certified businesses is shown annually within 
LEAF’s Global Impacts Reportvii. Other research projects demonstrate specific impacts such as 
Independent Evaluationsviii.

As part of empowerment, LEAF also recognises the importance of increasing public awareness of 
food, farming and nature; LEAF Educationix works to inspire and educate future generations 
including LEAF Open Farm Sundayx. 
LEAF recognises that there is a proliferation of sustainability logos, however there is limited number 
of demonstrably credible ones such as those that are compliant with ISEAL Codes of Practice. Whilst 
a range of sustainability logos will continue to be helpful, excessive proliferation of non-credible 
ones will compromise the effectiveness. It is important that non-organic agricultural systems can be 
credibly recognised as sustainable, such as through LEAF Marque. 
Whilst LEAF Marque focuses on food and farming, it is important that sustainability can be applied to 
all products. To empower consumers for a green transition, it will be essential to consider all types 
of sustainability claims placed on products and services, applying equally to both commercial and 
voluntary standards.

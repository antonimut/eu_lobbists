CEEP INPUT TO THE ROADMAP WATER POLLUTION- EU RULES ON 
URBAN WASTE-WATER TREATMENT DIRECTIVE (UWWTD) 
 
CEEP, the European Centre of Employers and Enterprises providing Public Services and 
Services of general interest, welcomes the opportunity to submit its input to the revision of 
the Urban Waste-Water Treatment Directive (UWWTD).  
CEEP welcomes the Commiss ion’s intention to closely link the revision of the UWWTD with 
the Green Deal and the Zero Pollution Ambition. In general, the UWWTD has been successful 
in protecting the environment. Nevertheless, CEEP supports the Commission’s intention to 
adapt the Dire ctive to current developments, as the circumstances and environmental 
problems have significantly changed since its existence in 1991. This includes new challenges 
related to climate change, micropollutants, pharmaceuticals and other emerging pollutants. 
In this regard, CEEP highly welcomes the Commission’s intention to focus on measures aimed 
at reducing pollution at source. This requires a consequent implementation of the polluter 
pays principle. Therefore, CEEP welcomes the consideration of extended pro ducer 
responsibility (EPR), as proposed by the European Commission, since the current political and 
legal framework offers only limited and non -binding possibilities for reducing pollution  at 
source and implementing the precautionary and polluter pays principle. From CEEP’s point of 
view the evaluation of the directive should not lead to disproportionate costs for the waste -
water disposal companies. Because of its support of the re -use of sludge and treated waste -
water, CEEP calls for close links with the c urrent evaluation of the Sewage Sludge Directive, 
and asks for a coordination and harmonisation of both Directives whilst encouraging energy 
and resource efficiency opportunities , with the priori ty to ensure full environmental 
protection first, as it will play a central role in its modernisation progress and work towards 
the objective of the EU Green Deal. 
For this reason, CEEP would like to highlight the following recommendations when revising 
the UWWTD to bring it in line with the current challenges of c limate change and make this 
directive part of the solution to reach climate neutrality as set in the EU Green Deal: 
• Take into account the new challenges of climate change, micropollutants, 
pharmaceuticals and other emerging pollutants, by reducing pollution at source and 
consequently implementing the polluter pays principle, when evaluating this 
directive as well as other Green Deal instruments  to ensure it becomes part of the 
solution to reach the set goals of the EU Green Deal; 
• Support the collection and treatment of waste-water to become climate neutral, 
whilst taking the principle of environmental protection first, through the use of measures 
and the circular economy approach; 
• Coherence and combined approach with the Water Framework Directive  will be 
necessary to address problem of contaminants of emerging concerns in the waste -
water and treat it effectively and sustainably; 
• A holistic approach for CSO whilst taking local and regional conditions and priorities 
into account and the principle of  subsidiarity. CEEP does not support target 
performance values set at EU level to address overflows from collecting systems, yet

monitoring of CSO will be needed before actions can be considered; 
• Before expanding the obligations for waste-water disposal companies, consolidation

• Make better use of circular economy possibilities within the directive and facilitate

• Apply the principle of “extended producers responsibility” efficiently  to reduce

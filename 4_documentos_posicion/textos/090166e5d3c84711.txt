Additional information: 
 
We also recognize that certification by the two most rigorous certification schemes—Forest Stewardship 
Council and Roundtable for Sustainable Biomaterials–can help avoid harmful management practices, 
although these standards do not necessarily address carbon emissions. Only a fraction of the feedstocks 
imported from the USA have been certified by such schemes. A July 2020 report commissioned by one 
of the primary wood pellet exporters found that only 23% were certified by schemes other than the 
Sustainable Biomass Program (Boundless Impact Investing, 2020), a scheme which has been criticized for 
its failure to require third-party verification and for its permissive standards (e.g., NRDC, 2017). Existing 
sustainable forest management principles have not prevented the harvest of sensitive ecosystems in the 
Southeastern USA, including within the internationally-recognized Coastal Plain biodiversity hotspot.

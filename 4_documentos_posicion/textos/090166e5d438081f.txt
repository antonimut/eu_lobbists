 
Please share any additional statements, documents, position papers concerning eID under
the eIDAS framework and the future of digital identity.
We think that the introduction of new private sector digital identity trust services for identification,
authentication and provision of attributes must be carefully evaluated. In order to guarantee a level
playing  field,  we  would  suggest  to set  different  degrees of  trustworthiness  and requirements
depending on the robustness and reliability of the identity scheme and the level of access and the
importance of the information shared.
 
Additionally, DG FISMA has recently published its Digital Finance Strategy, where the Commission
is considering to build  on the eIDAS extended scope  to the private sector, by means of the
integration of further elements so that digital identities can be used, for example, for ‘on-boarding’
with another financial institution. We believe that it is necessary to ensure that the two initiatives
are developed in coordination 
Regarding the current European Action Plan for a Comprehensive EU policy on Preventing Money
Laundering and Terrorist Financing, due to the national divergent accepted methods for digital
onboarding  we also encourage the Commission to work in coherence with this eIDAS revision.

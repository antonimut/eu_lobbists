AMICE Detailed Answer to Q39 
The time horizon should be chosen so  that the climate change stress tests provide relevant 
information for the insurer’s business.  The time horizon should therefore be consistent with 
the one set out  for financial or strategic planning purposes. Against the background of 
increasing complexity and uncertainty, we would suggest to keep the long-term assessments 
of climate change on a qualitative level. Quantitative long- term stress tests are less relevant 
given the considerable business evolution / climate change adaption up to this point in time, 
which is impossible to be easily captured in a quantitative framework.  
We would like to question the significance of a stress test exercise that models the changes 
in an undertaking´s current portfolio over the medium to long term  horizon. The projection 
period should therefore be as short as possible if based on the current portfolio; otherwise too 
many assumptions would have to be made for the future, also including infrastructural 
mitigation measures. If a longer-term scenario is chosen, the nature of the stress test should 
be more explorative.

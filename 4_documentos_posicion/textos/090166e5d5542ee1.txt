 
 
EPPA, The European PVC Profiles and related Building Products Association represents the 
manufacturers of PVC window systems suppliers in Europe. Windows are long -life constructions 
products, thus EPPA is committed to ensure that the environment and the climate will benefit from 
windows placed on the market, bringing to the market products that are durable, recyclable and safe, 
while having a low environmental impact. It is one of the industry contributions to the objectives of 
the Circular Economy in the framework of the European Green Deal. 
Why windows are sustainable products? 
In sustainable procurement, EPPA pledges for the considerate use of raw materials and also stands for 
future-oriented product design PVC windows are considered to be durable and sustainable products. 
A window’s life counts at least 40 years during which product performance must remain constant.  
Recycling, Energy efficiency & Low emission 
Recycling PVC profiles is the most environmentally fr iendly, safe and circular end of life approach. 
From an overall environmental impact point of view, it is preferable over incineration or landfill 
because valuable resources are kept. If a PVC window is replaced, it is far too valuable for the landfill 
or incineration plant. Instead, the old window serves as a raw material supply for new profiles and 
thus must remain in a closed loop via recycling.  PVC can be recycled up to 7 times  (i.e. giving it an 
approximate lifespan of 280 years) while preserving its properties. By keeping PVC in a closed loop, 
industry can continue to use the material, which decreases the need to produce virgin plastics that 
ultimately requires new raw materials, and more energy to produce.  
Recycling should not be endangered by legal barriers, that include: 
• any potential restriction of lead use below 2%* or cadmium use below 0,1%  
• any potential restriction of other additives used in PVC profile production in the past 
• any potential required authorization on the use of substances present in recyclate 
• any classification of PVC waste as hazardous 
 
Sustainability requires resource-saving manufacturing process, in which energy is used sparingly and 
the emission of greenhouse gases and other pollutants is reduced.  A sensitivity analysis made for 
profile production shows that due to the reduction of raw material use the emission of CO2 and 
therefore the global warming potential (GWP) is reduced significantly by windows containing recyclate 
content. The inclusion of 40% of recycled PVC in new window frames can save emissions between 625 
CO2/kg to up to 1125 CO2/kg per window, in the production phase. In 2019, the profile industry used 
about 23% of rPVC in European Production. In the production of windows recycled material consumes 
90% less energy than using fresh material.    
Environmental Product Declarations  (EPDs), which EPPA published for double -glazed windows and 
triple-glazed PVC windows, analyse the environmental impact of PVC window profile systems 
throughout their entire life cycle from raw material to disposal. The dataset represents over 90% of 
the European market for PVC window profiles.  
Reusability & Reparability  
EPPA is not in favour of reusing windows. They must be controlled ( CE marked). Additionally, today, 
the secondary market is largely uncontrolled, thus EPPA is in favour of recycling. 
It is worth mentioning, that PVC windows are fully repairable, as they are modular products. Individual 
parts (sealants, glass, fittings) can be repaired and replaced.

Prevention of the greenwashing 
Independent product and quality marks such as the VinylPlus Product Label stand for windows are 
long-lasting and are produced from sustainable resources. 
Within the industry, we have already developed many examples of how the sustainability of products 
is being assessed and communicated over the value chain and depending on the product, directly to 
the consumers.  One of them is the VinylPlus® Product Label which is a labelling scheme which makes 
it easy for  customers and markets to identify the most sustainable and high -performance PVC 
products. 
Harmful substances 
EPPA is in favour of increased traceability of PVC materials & products throughout their lifetime with 
controls and verification that adhere to independent European standards. It is recommended by the 
industry to assess the sustainability of window profiles  with the help of independent, third party -
verified window sustainability assessments via Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) such as 
with the EPPA Life Cycle Assessment. 
 
To conclude, mentioned fundamental sustainability principles should consider all aspects of 
sustainability, i.e. ecological, economic and social responsibility, equally.

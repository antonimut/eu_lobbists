The issue 
Brake particle emissions are significant and increasingly relevant as we transition to stricter 
exhaust emission standards and electric vehicles, as documented including by the JRC1. 
Today, brake PM emissions represent 20% of particle emissions in cities such as Paris, and 
are the dominant source of all road transport PM emissions in Germany.  
The Euro 6 standard addresses only 20% of vehicle PM emissions. Typical brake PM 
emissions are about 6 times higher than allowed exhaust emissions (30 mg/km for brake 
emissions versus 5 mg/km for exhaust emissions). Brake emissions are the largest single 
source of vehicle PM emissions representing around 40% of vehicle PM emissions. Even for 
small electric vehicles, brake PM emissions are 2.5 times higher than allowed PM emissions 
of a thermal engine (11 mg/km versus 5 mg/km).   
PM emissions are problematic from both an environmental and health point of view. This is 
well documented in scientific literature. Recent evidence also points to a link between Covid-
19 death rate and PM exposure2.  
To effectively address PM emissions from vehicles, post Euro 6/VI standards should cover 
and set limits for brake emissions. Those same limits should also apply to electric vehicles. 
Such standards should be introduced swiftly and set the bar high for the EU to stay on track 
to meet the ambitious objectives set out in Green Deal.  
 
About Tallano 
Tallano technologie® is a French engineering company founded in 2012. We are pioneers in 
the field of particle emission reduction systems for vehicle braking. 
Determined to develop effective and modular solutions capable of being adapted to evolving 
market needs, we have established several technological and economic partnerships with 
major manufacturing groups and spare parts producers in the transport sector. 
Our tamic® technology, which combines redesigned brake pads connected to an 
electronically controlled vacuum and filtration system, traps brake particles emissions at 
source by suction. It is an adaptable system with no impact on vehicle performance, while 
capturing over 90% of brake PM emissions.

TAMIC - PRINCIPLES
Active vacuum system in connection with the friction  area
• Brake pads are modified to create a groove (prevent the
dissemination of particles in the air).
• Grooves are connected with the filtration and vacuum
units by depressurized lines made of flexible materials.
• Vacuum system which creates the depression is
managed by an electronic control unit (activated by the
brake pedal information)
• Dust is blocked and captured into the filtration system
efficient up to 0.03µm

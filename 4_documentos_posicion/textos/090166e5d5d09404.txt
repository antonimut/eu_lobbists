MCEC – Microbial Control Executive Council 
Sustainable Product Initiative Inception Impact Assessment (IIA) 
MCEC contribution to feedback mechanism (14/09-16/11/20) 
 
The Microbial Control Executive Council (MCEC) welcomes the opportunity provided by the European 
Commission to provide views about the forthcoming Sustainable Product Initiative (SPI).  
  
MCEC company members are committed to the betterment of public health and wellbeing through 
sustainable microbial control technologies. Microbial control active ingredients are the building blocks 
of a wide range of antimicrobial technologies, that are applied by thousands of companies, many being 
European SMEs. These products provide essential benefits to  homes, hospitals, food processes and 
industrial workplaces, and in applications as diverse as disinfectants or metalworking fluids. Lastly, 
microbial control technologies ensure the sustainability and durability of a wide range  of consumer 
goods and treated articles and processes such as paints on wall surfaces or ship hulls, plastics or wood 
for industrial and consumer use, and industrial cooling circuits.  
As far as the SPI is concerned, MCEC supports the policy goal to create a circular economy in Europe, 
focusing on enhanced reusability and recyclability of products. However, we would like to stress that 
references to hazardous chemicals should be better defined in the initiative. Many chemicals need to 
have hazardous properties to ensure  action efficacy. This is particularly relevant for biocides that are 
necessary to support the manufacturing processes of all products covered by this policy initiative. 
The published IIA reads as confusing all chemicals having hazardous properties, mixing those that can 
potentially prevent products recyclability and those that support in fact the sustainability aspects of 
consumption and professional use of these products.  
This could pose the microbial control industry issues for instance in the following areas: 
- Under the scope of this initiative, the European Commission announces  that textiles will be 
subject to new requirements for increased sustainability of these products, through facilitated 
recyclability. In that regard, biocidal substances may be used in textiles to prevent infection, 
to minimize incidents of biodeterioration  of the fibres  and to impart hygienic finishes for 
specific applications. These chemicals play a crucial role in enhancing textiles durability and 
reduce waste. 
- On the same note, t he initiative will also address the presence of hazardous chemicals in 
intermediate products. Microbial control technologies, such as  product preservation and 
coatings solutions, substantially contribute to improving safety at work, reducing construction 
waste and support a long usability  of materials : indeed, biocidal substances are used to 
reinforce the shell -life of essential intermediate materials from degradation caused by 
microbial action, such as paints & coatings, adhesives, sealants, plastic and wood.  
Based on the above, MCEC agrees with the European Commission that “i mproved durability and 
reparability of products is expected to boost jobs in the reuse and repair sectors, […]”. But to achieve 
this objective, existing solutions for intermediate materials  products durability should not be 
hindered, including during the manufacturing phase where microbial control technologies are 
essential for sustainable production.

Under the announced measures on production processes, for example to facilitate recycled content or 
remanufacturing and to track the use of hazardous substances in such processes , MCEC invites the 
European Commission to consider the extension of the scope of the Ecodesign directive only through 
relevant consideration of the existing regulatory framework. MCEC refers to the fact that human and 
environmental safety considerations of and from the use of biocidal substances are already addressed 
under the Biocidal Products Regulation (Regulation (EU) 528/2012). 
We hope our inputs are considered constructive by the European Commission. As far as microbial 
control technologies are concerned, MCEC remains at your disposal for any further information, and 
in the context of the follow-up consultation.  
 
Disclaimer 
While contributing to this consultation we remain very aware we are all still experiencing 
unprecedented times, with industry, governments and institutions  around the world taking major 
actions to address the Covid -19 crisis, and introducing large -scale policies which will have significant 
impacts for years to come. We will continue to support Europe’s Member State governments and 
institutions in their efforts to overcome the socio-economic impacts of the crisis. 
When investing for the future, industry, governments and institutions will have to ensure investments 
align with the policy targets of a climate neutral Europe by 2050. All this also means that the 
attractiveness of Europe as a re -investment destination, and re-shoring industry back to Europe, will 
depend more than ever on a favourable policy framework that manages ever -growing differences 
between the world’s regions. We look to the European Commissio n to undertake the appropriate 
assessments and to include these wider considerations in the future framework that will be developed.

About us 
The Microbial Control Executive Council or MCEC, is an initiative of the world’s leading  companies 
developing and supplying microbial control technology and solutions . It was established in 2012  in 
order to promote the safe and effective use of microbial control technologies. MCEC has six members: 
BASF, DuPont, ICL, Lanxess, Lonza and Troy.  
In creating MCEC, we envisioned creating a reference point for stakeholders and the public alike to 
understand the benefits of microbial control technologies and the steps being taken to ensure the use 
of more sustainable biocides across all applications. 
To learn more, follow us on Twitter, visit our website and our LinkedIn page. For more information, 
do not hesitate to contact us at info@microbial-control.com. 
MCEC is a sector group of the European Chemical Industry Council (Cefic).

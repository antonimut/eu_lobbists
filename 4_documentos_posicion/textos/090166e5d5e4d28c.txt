APPENDIX 1:
​
 Eco Design Definition
 
 
Our
products
account
for
the
largest
proportion
of
our
impact.
To
reduce
this,
we
are
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
developing
an
eco-design
strategy.
By
2026,
100%
of
our
products
will
be
eco-designed,
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
in line with the following definition:
 
-
10%
product
impact
for
at
least
2
indicators
(including
equivalent
CO2
Climate
Change)
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
compared
to
the
impact
of
the
previous
product
(before
eco-design)
and
without
significant
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
deterioration of other indicators.
 
 
OR,
in
the
event
where
environmental
assessments
are
not
possible,
a
few
specific
cases
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
(nutrition,
new
materials,
etc.).
For
recycled
polyester,
for
example,
a
product
is
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
considered
to
be
eco-designed
if
it
comprises
at
least
70%
recycled
polyester
materials.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The
use
of
recycled
cotton
is
the
best
solution,
as
it
does
not
require
additional
cotton
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
production.
However,
this
is
only
possible
up
to
a
maximum
of
30
to
40%,
as
recycled
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
fibres
are
shorter
and
must
be
blended
with
pure
cotton.

be

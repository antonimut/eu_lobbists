Inception Impact Assessment 
FOUR PAWS / VIER PFOTEN welcomes the Commission’s intention to further 
develop the sustainable products policy and the opportunity to provide 
feedback to the Inception Impact Assessment. In view of the European Green 
Deal objectives , we trust the Commission will be ambitious in its plans to 
provide a strong and coherent sustainable product policy framework. 
About FOUR PAWS / VIER PFOTEN 
FOUR PAWS is the global animal welfare organisation for animals under direct human 
influence, whic h reveals suffering, rescues animals in need and protects them. 
Founded in Vienna in 1988, the organisation focuses on companion animals including 
stray dogs and cats, farm animals and wild animals kept in inappropriate conditions as 
well as in disaster and conflict zones. With sustainable campaigns and projects, FOUR 
PAWS provides rapid help and long-term protection for suffering animals.  
Objectives and Policy options 
FOUR PAWS / VIER PFOTEN welcomes European Commission’s ambition to make 
products placed in the European Union market more sustainable.  To effectively 
address th is challenge, we support the development of a policy and regulation for 
sustainable products that is homogeneous  to all EU Member States , to achieve 
responsible consumption.  While the Eco-design Directive 2009/125/EC 
successfully regulates energy sufficiency  of energy related products , we 
believe its scope  should be extended to a wide range of products . In this 
respect, we would strongly encourage  the Commission to give priority to 
identified groups with a high environmental impact, such as textiles.  
It is also a further step in addressing  unsustainable production and consumption 
patterns. To that end, the production of fur for fashion has adverse impacts on animal 
welfare and both environment and human health . It is in tensively polluting, energy 
intensive and poses a risk to the biodiversity. The carbon footprint of fur production is 
particularly high due to the production of tons of manure 1 and the enormous amount 
of animal feed required 2. As an example, three tons of feed is normally needed to

Establishment of sustainability principles 
FOUR PAWS / VIER PFOTEN fully supports the European Commission in setting 
up sustainability principles and specific requirements linked to environment 
and social aspects along the value chain. We consider those principles should be 
incorporated into the production processes  and strictly defined , taking into account 
different conditions such as water consumption, environmental footprints, use of 
chemical and dangerous substances, recyclability and animal welfare. To ensure EU 
products are sustainable, we strongly encourage establishing a mandatory 
certified ecolabel with high environmental and  sustainable standards 
throughout product’s life cycle . The ecolabel must  establish ecological criteria

Lack of reliable information on sustainability  
Although the fur  industry claims to be  environmentally friendly and sustainable , it 
became apparent that the fur production is not green, intensively polluting and energy 
consumptive. Additionally, various hazardous and heavily polluting chemicals used in 
the process  to ensure the fur is not biodegradable . Therefore, we support the 
Commission to strengthen consumer protection against green washing. We 
believe consumers need clear and accurate information on the environmental 
footprint of their products. In this respect, it is essential that a  mandatory ecolabel 
provides clear and comprehensive information on the sustainability of products. From 
a consumer perspective , animal derived products should also include information 
about the keeping conditions. FOUR PAWS / VIER PFOTEN support the digitalisation 
of product information  and the creation of an App or digital passport  reporting the 
sustainable aspects of a product.

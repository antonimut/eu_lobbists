Feedback of the European Association of Hospital Pharmacists on the “Inception Impact 
Assessment for the Revision of the Union legislation on blood, tissues and cells” 
 
 
The European Association of Hospital Pharmacists (EAHP) welcomes the European Commission’s Inception 
Impact Assessment on the revision of the blood, tissues and cells legislation and its planned initiatives for 
the fourth quarter of 2021.  
 
The shortcomings identified in regards to the vulnerability of the supply chain are of particular importance 
to our Association and our members across Europe. Plasma -derived medicines are paramount for  the 
treatment of patients. In particular for those with rare, chronic or severe diseases, immunodeficiencies and 
bleeding disorders.  The COVID -19 pandemic saw a growing need for plasma -derived medicines whose 
demand cannot be met by the volumes of plasma currently collected in the EU. EAHP would consequently 
like to encourage the European Commission to put forward measures that optimize the access to and lower 
the risk of shortages for plasma-derived medicines. 
 
As key stakeholders in the discussion linked the shortage of plasma-derived medicines, EAHP would like to 
express its interest to be consulted via the targeted consultations mentioned in the Inception Impact 
Assessment which seek to collect input from healthcare professionals.

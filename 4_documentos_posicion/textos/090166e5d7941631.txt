Introduction to SWIPE
 
The
Successful
Wildlife
Crime
Prosecution
in
Europe
(SWIPE)
LIFE
project
intends
to
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
discourage
and
ultimately
reduce
Wildlife
Crime
by
improving
compliance
with
EU
Environmental
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Law,
increasing
the
number
of
successfully
prosecuted
offences.
The
implementation
period
is
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
between September 2020 and August 2023.
 
SWIPE
targets
11
countries
and
concentrates
its
efforts
on
cases
in
EU
Member
States
with
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
considerable
gaps
in
wildlife
crime
prosecution
(BG,
HR,
HU,
PL,
RO,
SK)
and
those
with
some
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
good
practice
to
share
(IT,
ES)
as
well
as
UA,
BiH
and
SR
-
countries
that
are
an
important
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
source
and
transit
points
for
trafficked
wildlife
and
host
populations
of
protected
species
that
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
expand across the EU borders.
 
Roadmap on the Environmental Crime Directive
 
 
SWIPE
LIFE
project
welcomes
the
commitment

the
European
Commission
to
revise
and
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
improve
the
Directive
2008/99/EC
on
the
protection

the
environment
through
criminal
law
and
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
contribute
to
the
implementation

the
goals

the
European
Green
Deal
and
the
EU
2030
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Biodiversity Strategy
​
.
 
 
 
Wildlife
crime
has
a
negative

biodiversity
across
the
world,
considering
that

are
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
currently
witnessing
a
​
catastrophic
average
68%

animal
population
sizes
tracked
over
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
46
years
(1970-2016)
​
1
​
,
and

to
the
environmental
destruction
that

to
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
virus
outbreaks
such

COVID-19.
​
The
illegal
killing,
poisoning,
trapping
or
trade
of
species
is
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
putting
the
European
Union’s
biodiversity
under
pressure
​
2
and

of
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
environmental
compliance

sanctioning,

Thus,

EMPACT

-

​
(more

There

for
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2022 onwards.

We

​
fourth

Roadmap:
​
amending
ECD
to
include
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
specific
provisions
to
improve
the
topics
1
to
5
​
,
​
combined

non-legislative
measures
to
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
provide more detailed guidance.
 
 
In

non-legislative
measures,

the
development
and
promotion
of
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
training
modules
​
because
increased
awareness,
knowledge
and
capacity
of
wildlife
crime
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
professionals
(prosecutors
and
experts
from
enforcement
agencies)

hand

hand
with
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
a
supportive
policy

and

can
result
in
better
national
and
cross-border
governance
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
in relation to wildlife crime investigation and prosecution.
 
 
 
Another
key
mention
under
the
fourth
option
is
the
collection,
processing
and
sharing
of
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
statistical
data

detection,
investigation,
prosecution
and

the
different

environmental

Member

We

reliable
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
evidence

European
Wildlife
Crime

to
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
information, enabling comparison of data across Europe.
 
 
Amending

Crime

​

Directive

The

​
harmonised

investigative

We

and

In

as deployed to fight other serious crimes.
 
 
 
We

where the public and the NGOs could bring in all the relevant know-how and expertise.

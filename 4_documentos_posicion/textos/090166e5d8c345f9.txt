Dear Sirs, 
 
We write to you regarding the European Commission’s public consultation on the review of the 
Alternative Investment Fund Managers Directive (‘AIFMD’). 
 
AFME represents a broad array of European and global participants in the wholesale financial 
markets. Its members comprise pan-EU and global banks as well as key regional banks, brokers, 
law firms,  investors and other financial market participants. We advocate stable, competitive, 
sustainable European financial markets that support economic growth and benefit society. 
 
We write specifically on behalf of members of AFME’s Prime Services Forum, who undertake 
prime brokerage activities, and thus are directly impacted by certain provisions within the 
AIFMD. Our members believe that other market participants are best placed to provide feedback 
on the specific questions raised within the consultation.  
 
However, we wish to emphasise that, in general, we believe that the current directive is efficient 
and robust, and that significant changes to the AIFMD are not necessary. In particular, we are not 
supportive of any proposal to merge the AIFMD and UCITS regulatory frameworks.  
 
In a challenging and uncertain economic environment, we believe that substantial alterations to 
existing, functioning regulation may be an inefficient use of the time and resources of regulators, 
policymakers and market participants. We recommend that, where changes are identified as 
necessary, these should be effected through “quick fixes” to supervisory guidance and/or changes 
to the relevant delegation regulation.  
 
We are grateful for the opportunity to provide this feedback, and welcome further discussion with 
you on this important topic. 
 
Yours sincerely,

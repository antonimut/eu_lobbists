Rare Cancers Europe 
Rare Cancers Europe (RCE)  was founded in 2008 and is a multi -stakeholder partnership representing 40 
stakeholders including scientific societies, patient and research organi zations, healthcare and 
educational providers, and  industry, un der the coordination of the European Society for Medical Oncology 
(ESMO). RCE is working on the implementation of  the recommendations of the Rare Cancer Agenda 2030 
produced by the EU Joint Action on Rare Cancers. With the same collaborative spirit enshrined in t he RCE 
governance we welcome th e EU efforts to assessing solutions to improve the legislative environment on 
medicines for rare diseases, including rare cancers.

5
Upstream Gas Supply Chain Methane Emissions
Without producer abatement response, the decline in total oil and gas methane emissions amounts to less than 1% at 25 €/tCO2eq and 1 - 2% at 100 
€/tCO2eq due to reshuffling of global gas trade flows. With producer abatement response, the 75% reduction in central baseline emission intensities 
results in a reduction in global oil and gas methane emissions of around 2 - 3% at both 25 €/tCO2eq and 100 €/tCO2eq.

Impacts on Average EU Wholesale Gas Prices
The impact of a methane price on EU natural gas wholesale prices is minimal except for the case of a methane price of 100 €/tCO2eq 
without a producer abatement response. At the end-use level, the impact of an EU methane price on gas prices is smaller due to the large 
share of national taxes, levies, distribution and other price components in end-use prices.

9
EU Natural Gas Import Supply Mix (I)
A methane price of 25 €/tCO2eq has a relatively small impact on the EU gas supply mix without a producer abatement response based on  
central baseline emission intensity assumptions. A methane price of 100 €/tCO2eq has a larger impact on the EU gas supply mix. The 
supply regions most affected are Russia, the US and the Middle East.

10
EU Natural Gas Import Supply Mix (II)
A methane price of 25 €/tCO2eq has a small impact on the EU gas supply mix with a producer abatement response based on central 
baseline emission intensity assumptions. Impacts of a methane price would be mitigated with a producer abatement response (because 
such an abatement response would proportionally reduce the methane price mark-up on EU gas import prices).

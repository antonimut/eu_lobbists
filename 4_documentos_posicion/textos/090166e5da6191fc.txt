Subject: Eneco response on: Hydrogen and Gas markets Decarbonisation Package 
Dear Madam / Sir, 
Eneco welcomes the opportunity to comment on the roadmap published by DG Energy on 10 February 
2021 concerning the Hydrogen and Gas Markets Decarbonisation Package.  
Response on the road map: 
1) Hydrogen infrastructure and hydrogen markets.  
Eneco is of the opinion that renewable hydrogen is promising as an energy carrier and feedstock to 
support the EU’s decarbonisation efforts. We support creating a regulatory frame work, in which the 
networks belong to the public domain and the network operators act on the principles of non-
discrimination, open access and cost-based tariffs and where the production, trade and supply is left 
to the market.  
2) Access of renewable and low-carbon gases to the infrastructure and the market: 
In the start-up phase of the consumer market for renewable hydrogen producers of CO2 free 
hydrogen should have a right of connection to the natural gas system in order to blend their 
hydrogen into the natural gas system, of course within technical and safety boundaries. 
3) Green Hydrogen 
For the development of the CO2 free hydrogen market it is important that the demand for CO2 free 
hydrogen is stimulated and made possible. Therefore a system of Hydrogen certificates has to be 
developed, that includes differentiation of the CO2 emission of the different sources of hydrogen. 
4) Consumer rights, competition and transparency:  
Eneco supports creating a regulatory framework for consumer rights for hydrogen. A framework for 
consumer rights on hydrogen products will enhance consumer confidence in hydrogen products.  
We thank you for the opportunity to comment on the roadmap. If you have any questions regarding

CO2 infrastructure for low-carbon hydrogen deployment
Inordertodecarboniseexistinghydrogenproductionassets,aswell asotherheavyCO2emittingindustrial processes, CO2 capture technologies can be deployed. An on-shore “midstream”infrastructurewill beneededinorder tocollect (andasthecasemaybetemporarilystore)CO2,before the CO2 will be shipped to off-shore sequestration sites. It is recommended that suchcollectioninfrastructure, tobedeployedmainlywithinindustrial basins, shall beunregulatedandbe open to private investment.
Access to CO2 storage should also be on a competitive basis, with aformof regulationput inplace to ensure that storage operators do not capture a “monopoly rent” on the storagemanagement, especially as responsibility for the core value of storage(longterm/permanence)will be borne by governments. For States where emitters are located, having this competitiveaccess to storage is critical to preserve the competitivenessof the industry in their geography.

 
 
AUTODESK’s Response to EPBD’s Inception Impact Assessment 
March 2021 
  
 
AUTODESK welcomes the opportunity to provide input regarding the revision of the Energy Performance 
Building Directive (EPBD) and shares the Commission’s view that d ecarbonization of the building sector is 
vital to deliver on the EU’s climate objectives. 
 
The global pandemic and today’s environmental and technology challenges have driven businesses and 
consumers alike to fundamentally rethink how we design, make and use buildings. It has created unprecedented 
opportunities, using the right tools and expertise to do more, better, with less: less resources, less energy, less 
carbon, less time, less money. 
 
We see the revision of the EPBD as a major vehicle to deliver on the new objectives set up by the Commission 
in the Renovation Wave and therefore to advance the digital transformation of construction, manufacturing 
and production, to enable cloud and data -driven business models  and to promote sust ainability in the built 
environment. 
 
Harnessing the benefits of digital solutions for sustainable buildings 
 
With the dramatic emission reduction targets in mind, professionals from the AEC (architecture, engineering 
and construction) sector  need more than ever to estimate the real impact of their energy consumption, to 
contextualize their operations, to better manage resource efficiency and project lifecycle as well as to consider 
climate resilience and health and environmental standards. Businesses and investors need tools to project 
carbon footprints and material impacts of the products/buildings.  
 
Digitalization is key for a sustainable buil t environment: it is crucial to optimize technical building systems 
and to incentivize the construction sector to deliver better built projects , whether it’s newbuild or retrofit. 
Accelerating the use of digital design solutions and operating tools (e.g., Building Information Modeling , 
Digital Twin) in digital construction and renovation work can  contribute to foster a data -centric approach 
helping to  analyze and track en ergy objectives/performance . They can support an environmental design 
approach for construction products, buildings, and infrastructure of the future.  
 
Digital solutions allow the design of high-performance buildings, improve structural material efficiency, and 
make safer and more inclusive buildings . Planning tools allow designers to conduct energy analysis from 
concept to complex modeling,  to optimize integration of renewable energy sour ces in a building,  to reduce 
material and resource waste in construction  but also to plan and design infrastructure for resilience and 
adaptation to climate change, to visualize projects in the context of the surrounding built and natural conditions. 
They help assess environmental and social impacts of designs and reduce embodied carbon through low-carbon 
material procurement. Those tools are collaborative and encourage a responsible value chain.  
 
Supporting the deployment of digital solutions in the building sector 
 
AUTODESK believes that the EPBD is an opportunity to spur the development o f innovative and digital 
solutions, in line with the objective s set in the Commission’s Communication on a Renovation Wave for 
Europe.  This is coherent the Commission’s approach to tackle the “twin challenges” (green and digital).  
 
The deployment of digital solutions in the building sector requires a  proactive and ambitious approach, 
combining regulatory requirements and financial incentives, in order to accompany and facilitate the transition 
needed for professionals and companies.  
 
The revision of the EPBD should therefore explore synergies with other EU initiatives such as the EU Skills 
Agenda, A Europe Fit for the Digital Ag e and the New European Bauhaus. Moreover, this is an opportunity

AUTODESK is looking forward to further contributing to this initiative , sharing best practices and ideas to 
make the built environment more digitized and sustainable.

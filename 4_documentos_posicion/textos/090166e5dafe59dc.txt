UNESID’S KEY MESSAGES FOR THE REVISION OF THE INDUSTRIAL 
EMISSIONS DIRECTIVE 
The following bullet points summarises the key aspects considered critical by UNESID 
when reviewing the Industrial Emissions Directive (IED): 
• The revie w of the IED shall consider the retention of the industry 
competitiveness. For that the output of this review shall focus on the permitting 
process itself, applicability time periods as well as new innovative emerging 
techniques’ application. 
• Related to the  previous bullet point, the IED revision shall focus on avoiding 
excessive burdens which will affect both the efficiency of the IED itself as well as 
the competitiveness of European industry. Double regulation shall be strictly 
avoided so that the efficiency of the environmental investment conducted by the 
European Commission and by the industry is optimized. 
• The scope of the updated IED shall consider the environmental management as 
a whole, not scaling some environmental aspects above others and consideri ng 
sufficiently potential cross-media effects. 
• The current definition of BAT and the current non-binding performance levels or 
BAT-AEPLS shall be respected in order to ensure smooth and sustainable 
innovation of the industry which will facilitate the industrial transition. 
• The Seville process shall be respected, which will ensure a collaborative, 
multidisciplinary and technical adoption of BREF guidance documents, which will 
be key for the continuous environmental improvement of sectors  under IPPC 
regulation.

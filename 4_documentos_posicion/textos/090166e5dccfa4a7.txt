Road haulage is growing even in countries with a high

The Union’s new transport initiatives should always 
include the social and environmental impact, so as not 
to jeopardise the core principle of the Commission’s

4. F airness between and within

In the present strategy, the European Union continues

 t

Further external costs (noise, pollutants, land usage

Instead, those costs are passed onto the public. In 
 a

As a result, it will not be possible to comply with the

The failure to take all external costs into

In AK’s view, true-cost pricing could be implemented

State aid in the rail freight sector must allow for more

Direct subsidies for shippers  should be included to 
encourage 
 c

Inspections of the few rules, for example in the field

 (

employees and consumers in Austria. It acts for the interests of its members in fields of social-, educational-, 
economical-, and consumer issues both on the national and on the EU-level in Brussels. Furthermore, the 
Austrian Federal Chamber of Labour is a part of the Austrian social partnership. The Austrian Federal Chamber

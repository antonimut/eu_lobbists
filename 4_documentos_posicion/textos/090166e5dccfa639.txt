Troms and Finnmark County Council input to the revision of guidelines for the 
Trans European Network – Transport (TEN-T). 
 
With reference to the EU Commission's open consultation on the revision of the guidelines for the trans-
European transport network, TEN-T, Troms, and Finnmark County Council would like to provide input as 
follow. 
 
Troms and Finnmark is the northernmost region in Europe that is affected by the development of TEN-T. 
Our cities, districts and local communities are crucial for maintaining value creation, development, and 
stability in the north. Value creation takes place largely along the coast and especially the seafood 
industry is an important export commodity to the EU market. The goods are transported through 
regional, national, and TEN-T transport networks, both road and rail. Significant growth is expected in 
the seafood industry in the north, which will also have an impact on transport to the EU market. 
 
Troms and Finnmark County Council welcome the fact that the revision will be future-oriented as well as 
safeguarding the ambitions outlined in the EU Green Deal. It is important that future measures also 
contribute to incentives and financing that ensures a competitive transport from the coast in the north 
to the EU market. 
Troms and Finnmark County Council therefore kindly request the EU Commission and national 
authorities to take into consideration the following aspects:  
 
1. Ambitions for the development of a comprehensive network should be intensified with a deadline 
set to 2035. It is also important to ensure a sustainable TEN-T development that safeguards 
accessibility, regularity and security. 
2. The network should be expanded to meet ambitions set out by the region itself and the Joint 
Barents Transport Plan. Prioritized corridors from Tromsoe and Hammerfest and southwards via the 
E8 / E-45 through Finland and Sweden to the Gulf of Bothnia should be given TEN-T status. 
3. Transport in the north must be ensured competitive conditions at the same time as these conditions 
contribute to achieving sustainability and climate goals by 2030. This by reducing CO2 emissions in 
TEN-T corridors. 
4. The EU and national authorities must facilitate incentives and funding opportunities that support 
the phasing in of low- and zero-emission solutions, especially for truck transports in order to reduce 
CO2 emissions in the TENT-T network.

5. Climate objectives and transport security must be seen in the context of a better facilitation of 
digital services throughout the TEN-T network and the transport chain. This includes investment in 
5G mobile networks, access to electricity along the road and improved information services. 
6. Climate objectives also require increased facilitation of commercial transports through the removal 
of bottlenecks to facilitate increased use of environmentally friendly trucks. This includes 
development of 24-hour rest areas with real-time information services and fuel / charging stations 
that facilitate the phasing in of zero and low emission transports in the TEN-T network. 
7. Improved conditions for increased use of passenger cars that use electricity through a strengthening 
of the charging infrastructure in the TEN-T network. 
8. Improved interaction between modes of transport through a more efficient reloading from road to 
rail. In the north, this presupposes a strengthening of the current regularity and capacity of the TEN-
T railway network to Narvik, and a need for increased use of railway. 
9. A more efficient transhipment and strengthening of the railway will also have an impact on costs 
associated with transport in and out of the region, and thus also the price and access to goods 
within the region with further ensuing effects on business and society. 
10. An extension of the TEN-T railway north to Troms and Finnmark should be considered with a view to 
future investment in a more climate-friendly transport solution. 
11. The revised guidelines should consider climate change which increases the strain on TEN-T 
infrastructure. The focus should especially be put on measures that safeguard winter regularity, 
tunnel safety, robustness in new infrastructure and increased transport safety with satisfactory 
landslide protection. 
12. An important prerequisite for achieving the goals for TEN-T's comprehensive network and core 
network in the north is to ensure the completion of the measures contained in the White Paper for 
the National Transport Plan 2022-33 by 2027. 
13. The development of the TEN-T network should also bear in mind an Arctic perspective.

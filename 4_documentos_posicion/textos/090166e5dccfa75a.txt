Additional comments Bellona Europa: Public Consultation Trans-European transport network (TEN-T) 
– revised guidelines 
The Commission has rightfully acknowledged through its questionnaire as part of the Public Consultation 
Process on the TEN-T Regulation the need to strengthen synergies between the TEN-T and TEN-E to 
enable future decarbonization and further reductions of air pollutant emissions of transport.  
However, the questionnaire does not provide ample room to identify in which areas such synergies can 
play an important role. Bellona Europa therefore provide this attached document to outline the 
importance for industrial decarbonization and climate change mitigation of including transport of CO2  – 
through all modalities including ship, rail, barge and truck – in the TEN-T. Inclusion of all CO2 transport 
modalities would be in line with, and offer important support to, the Sustainable Finance Taxonomy 
Delegated Acts for Climate change mitigation and adoption published on the 21st of April. 
Appropriate CO2 storage sites across Europe are unevenly distributed amongst Member States., 
Ensuring accessibility to CO2 storage sites and facilities for industrial operators in countries with little or 
no developed, or possibility to develop, CO2 storage will be a prerequisite to incentivize the installation 
of CO2 capture technology intended for permanent storage. Investments in CO2 transport thus enable 
industrial high-emitters to capture CO2 while reducing costs.  
As part of an ongoing campaign on the parallel revision of the TEN-E Regulation, Bellona Europa has 
highlighted the necessity of including CO2 storage to the category of carbon dioxide networks in the 
current proposal from the Commission. Although the inclusion of CO2 storage in the TEN-E would be a 
game-changer for industrial decarbonisation efforts across Europe – accessibility is key. In the current 
European Commission proposal for the revised TEN-E Regulation, only CO2 transport via pipeline is 
eligible for PCI status. But, CO2 transport via pipeline is not always the most cost-effective option, or 
even a possibility.  
The TEN-T is appropriately placed to support transport of CO2 for permanent storage by different 
modalities than pipeline, in synergy with the TEN-E. Bellona Europa have over the last few months 
spoken to several market participants in energy intensive and harder-to-abate industry sectors, who 
confirm that both CO2 storage and flexible transport solutions are their greatest challenges when trying 
to realize their plans to decarbonize – calling for European Action. We therefore urge the European 
Commission to incorporate in the revised TEN-T the possibility of CO2 transport by all transport 
modalities, especially ship, truck, rail and barge, to be eligible for PCI status and the benefits it entails.

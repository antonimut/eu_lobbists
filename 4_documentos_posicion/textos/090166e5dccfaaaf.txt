Oulu is a strategic multi-modal node of the Euro-Arctic traffic area, which combines all modes of transport  
and international traffic corridors. Oulu belongs to the Bothnian arc that unites Finland and Sweden, which  
has the most significant concentration of industry, investigation and skills in Northern Europe.     
Oulu’s central location in relation to growing industries and international transport corridors and its nota- 
ble logistics service provisions strengthen its international position as a logistical centre. Investment in  the 
most s ignificant new fo rest-based, bio -based a nd mining i ndustries, as well as t ourism, inc rease t he  
volume of transport and passengers considerably. Oulu’s node holds a central position with regard to  
increased transport and journeys.     
The Oulu Region is the largest centre in the northern areas of Nordic Countries. It has 250,000 inhabitants  
and the number is estimated to grow by about 20% by 2040. In total there are about 96,000 jobs in the  
area, of which 5% a re in t he tra nsport and warehousing in dustry. Oulu is an inter nationally significant  
centre for technology and skills, and it has a high concentration of forest-based, chemical and metal  
industry.

The port of Oulu and the Oulu Airport as part of the TEN-T core  network    
 
The current TEN-T core network corridors and their nodes are all located in the southern parts of Finland. This 
leaves the bulk of Finland’s traffic network in a secondary strategic position. The proposal to extend the North 
Sea–Baltic Core Network Corridor to Northern Finland implies linking the most important na- tional-level south-
northward connections with the most important European strategic transport network. By contrast, Finland’s 
most important ports and airports, which are in a key position for the Finnish export industry and foreign trade, 
will be left without a core network status and related development possibilities after the proposal has been put 
into practice. The closest airports and ports that are part of the core network are located in Southern Finland, 
over 600 km from Oulu. 
 
Oulu is a strategic multi-modal node for the whole of Northern Finland. The different transport modes of the 
proposed Northern dimension of North Sea – Baltic core network corridor join together at the Oulu urban node. 
Transport and trip chains starting from or passing through the region are also well connected to Europe’s 
northernmost parts and to Asia through Eastern Finland via TEN-T core and comprehensive network roads and 
railways and motorway of seas. Interconnections between the major routes of different transport modes and 
logistics hubs have been determinedly developed within the last few years. 
 
The port of Oulu and the Oulu Airport are part of the comprehensive network. In Oulu, different modes of 
transport are joined together for the transportation of both people and goods. There are good connections from 
the railway station, port and airport to both the comprehensive and core network nodes. Local and suburban 
traffic and goods traffic connections are efficient. Central passenger and goods traffic terminals have been 
connected to a multi-modal traffic network. In Oulu, there are several logistics centres that include terminals of 
the most important national and international logistics companies. 
 
Oulu Urban Node is well connected to the TEN-T ports of the Bothnian Arc, which serve the growing export 
industy sector of the northern parts of Scandinavia. The Port of Oulu is important particularly for transportation 
linked to the forest-based, sawmilling and chemical industries. It is the largest container port and import port 
for pulp and chemicals in Northern Finland. Near future targets within Oulu Urban Node is to commence 
regional railway passenger transport and further develop the multimodal logistics hubs within the region. Oulu 
airport is the second is the second busiest in Finland with over million passengers (2019). Oulu should be 
included in the TEN-T core network as an urban node including Oulu Airport as core network airport and Port of 
Oulu as core network port.  
 
 
FACTS   
  The port of Oulu ist the largest general port in the Bothnian Bay. The volume of goods traffic via Port of     
Oulu totals some 3,6M tonnes per year.   Port Oulu Smarten, a digitalization project of the Port of Oulu, has 
won the 5 G innovation competion arranged by 5G Momentum.  
  There are optional clean fuels on o ffer at the port of Oulu.  Cur rently, LNG is trans- ported from 
Tornio’s LNG terminal on rubber wheels, but both ports are also planning to build their own  LNG 
terminals.   
  The Oulu Ai rport meets the requirements of the comprehensive network with regard to its an nual  
overall p assenger volume (over 0.1 % o f the annual overall p assenger volume o f all of EU’s air - 
ports, based on a three-year average calculated by Eurostat 0.980 million passengers).    .    
  For the time being, there are no clean fuels on offer at the Oulu Airport.    
 
 
 
 
 MOST IMPORTANT MEASURES   
  The port of Oulu to become TEN-T core network port.     
  Oulu Airport to become a TEN-T core network airport.     
  Oulu TEN-T to become an Urban Node in the core network.

Our views on Instant Payments 
 
Payments have become a strategic asset for retailers and their digital transformation, 
impacting customer experience , customer and co -worker safety  and business 
resilience. Customer habits are evolving at an unprecedented pace as customers rely more 
on apps, from in-store scanning of products to online and mobile buying. Payment solutions 
such as  Instant Payments  are key enablers of services  like click-and-collect and in -store 
payment through apps while supporting business conti nuity and social distancing under 
COVID restrictions. Innovative payment systems of tomorrow will drive conversion rates 
online, contribute customer loyalty and secure direct access to customer feedback. 
 
Instant Payments are paramount to the competitiveness of omnichannel retailers and 
will benefit European consumers. Features such as certainty of payment, 24/7 availability 
and increased security through Strong Customer Authentication will help consumers 
control their spending better. Instant availability of funds  through Instant Payments will 
improve how retailers forecast cash flow and improve cash pooling management , further 
reducing costs and improving affordability for customers . Because they accept a wide  
variety of payment means,  retailers have in -depth insights on the functioning of the 
payment market and can assist European Institutions in developing a future-proof 
European Instant Payment system.

We welcome the commitment of the European Commission to the development of 
Instant Payments  in Europe  through market-led initiatives. An open and  market-led 
European Scheme for Retail Instant Payments will support the uptake of EU-wide payment 
solutions able to compete with and reduce dependencies on international incumbents.  We 
call upon European Institutions to work with stakeholders towards a level-playing field for 
Instant Payments that will promote end-user solutions addressing the needs of consumers 
and merchants. To that end, w e have the following recommendations to create a future-
proof European Scheme for Retail Instant Payments: 
 Future EU Retail Instant Payment Scheme s should promote consumer-centric 
solutions. 
 Future EU Retail Instant Payment Schemes  should create a fair, transparent, and 
open ecosystem for all parties. 
 Future EU Retail Instant Payment Schemes  should enable SEPA-wide preferred 
operational settlement.  
 
 
 An E U Retail Instant Payments Scheme should promote consumer-centric 
solutions. 
 
Future European Schemes for Retail Instant Payments should enable a seamless, fast 
and secure customer payment experience across all channels. Customer awareness and 
trust in convenient and secure payment experience will be a key driver of the uptake of 
Instant Payments. Relying on an omnichannel approach that encompasses all retail uses 
such as e-commerce, mobile and in-store payments will address new consumer habits and 
support greater use of Instant Payments.  
 
EU Instant Payments solutions should offer key features and services that customers 
expect, from providing certainty of payment execution and settlement, instant repayment 
(refunds) and instalment to deferred payment and pre-authorisation of payments where 
the final amount is unknown.  Open and accessible proximity technology (e.g., QR code, 
Near Field Communication – NFC, Bluetooth Low Energy – BLE, geolocation) should be fully 
integrated into future EU solutions as customers have embraced new ways to pay in shops 
following the COVID -19 pandemic.  Additional services such as  Request To Pay (RTP) , e-
invoicing and the integration of value -added merchant services and loyalty offerings (e.g., 
redemption points for the use of specific payment means)  will be essential to make the EU 
Retail Instant Payments Scheme a future-proof set-up for customers and retailers alike.  
 
EU-wide availability, reachability and continuity of Instant Payments throughout the 
payment ecosystem should be a priority so that no one is left behind.  Merchants are 
currently not able to  offer Instant Payments to customers and suppliers whose banks 
remain rel uctant to provide such solutions. Creating incentives for banks to join  future 
European Schemes for Retail Instant Payments  will enable customers and suppliers to 
benefit from innovative payment solutions and improve cash flow management. Similarly, 
implementing offline services in case of system unavailability or disruption will be 
paramount to service continuity.  A European regulatory approach should be put forward 
should market forces fail to secure availability, reachability and continuity  of Instant 
Payments in a timely manner.

 Future EU Retail Instant Payment Schemes should create a fair, transparent, 
and open ecosystem for all parties. 
 
The business model and governance processes of future European Schemes for Retail 
Instant Payments  should prevent gatekeeping behaviours , supporting competitive 
payment services and innovative value -added services for consumers and merchants alike  
across the EU. Effective competition between incumbent players will enable banks, Payment 
Initiation Service Providers (P ISPs) and merchants to equally contribute to the future EU 
Instant Payments framework.  
 
Securing a fair, reasonable and transparent cost structure for merchants throughout 
the future European Retail Instant Payments  Scheme should be a priority . Instant 
Payment and settlement are becoming the rule and will be soon foreseen as a commodity 
service. Instant Payments should be free of charge for consumers considering that banks 
will soon operate on a 24/7 basis and rely on fully automated processes. Similarly, 
introducing a premium for corporate users would not be proportional. 
 
Future EU Retail Instant Payment Schemes  should operate under a common EU-wide 
standard and specifications that are easy to implement and fully interoperable. 
Market participants , from banks to P ISPs and merchants, and EU standardisation  and 
specifications organisations should co-lead on pan- EU standard setting processes, notably 
regarding a single SEPA-wide QR code standard for Instant Payments. Only such an 
approach will enable the European Union to scale up innovation, become a world leader in 
the field of Instant Payments  and contribute to EU sovereignty in the field of Instant 
Payments. 
 
 
 Future EU Retail Instant Payment Schemes should enable SEPA-wide preferred 
operational settlement. 
 
European Schemes for Retail Instant Payments should implement common caps with 
the existing  Single Euro Payments Area  (SEPA) Instant Credit Transfer Scheme (SCT 
Inst). To that end, a €100,000 cap  aligned with the current SCT Scheme  cap should be 
implemented across t he future SCT Inst Scheme . Such an approach will support greater 
transparency for customers and secure legal certainty for merchants. 
 
Transaction settlement should align  with merchant preferences, either at transaction 
level or in larger batches on predefined schedules upon merchants’ request. The wider 
uptake of Instant Payments will lead to the reception of thousands of incoming individual 
credits requiring new systems and capacity in place . Batch processing and settlement will 
prove useful  until the adequate infrastructure is in place.  Similarly, b espoke batch 
processing such as more regular batch processing (e.g., more than once a day) could help 
merchants optimise the time at which they receive funds.

Conclusion 
 
Instant payments are driving the ability of European retailers to grow and compete now and 
in future. A fair, reliable and harmonised EU Scheme for Retail Instant Payments will help 
the SEPA payment eco system innovate, scale and develop new services across the EU  in 
order to  provide consumers with more and better choices. We stand ready to support 
leveraging the European scheme for Instant Payments that will  ensure a future for Europe 
that is fair and equal for all, climate neutral, and digital.

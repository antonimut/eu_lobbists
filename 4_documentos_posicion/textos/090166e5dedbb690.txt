Public Consultation regarding the  mechanism to deter & counteract coercive action by non -EU 
countries – Commentary by economiesuisse  
 
The European Commission has invited interested parties to express their views on the mechanism to 
deter and counteract coercive action by non -EU countries . 
 
As a national federation, economiesuisse represents the competition- oriented, internationally 
networked, and responsible- minded Swiss business sector. Switzerland is a highly export -oriented 
country and economically more intertwined with the EU than the majority of the EU -member states  
themselves. We are convinced that these strong economic ties are mutually beneficial.  Regarding the 
present consultation, we would like to present  the following remarks from a Swiss perspective.  
 
In general, coercive practices and measures of all types can have a fragmenting effect on the 
application and use of multilaterally agreed rules. We consider multilateral solutions and strategies  at 
an international level  to be the first -best solution to solve such issues . However, w e acknowledge that 
in the current political environment , reaching global solutions might be difficult.  
 
Even though tackling coercive practices by third countries might be necessary for the EU to defend its 
interests under certain circumstances, it is crucial to ensure that this is exclusively a measure of last 
resort, used only after all other means of addressing the problem are exhausted and after careful 
political calibration. In this context we underline the existing dispute settlement mechanisms on the 
international level.  
 
The anti -coercion instrument should also be designed in such a way that it simplifies and does not 
entail additional burden on economic operators  and limit trade/investment disruption to cases where it 
is unavoidable.  Regarding the economic effects  on business  such measures  need a careful qualitative 
and quantitative evaluation  before and during implementation.  
 
In our view,  it is important that the anti -coercion instrument s hall not be used in cases where other 
types of instruments / policies are in place that allow the EU to safeguard its interests. In addition, such 
an instrument should be limited in time and scope.  The definition "until the coercive act is removed"

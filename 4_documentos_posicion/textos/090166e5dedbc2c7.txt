Additional comments by TenneT Holding B.V. to the Open Public Consultation on 
the Hydrogen and Gas Market Decarbonisation Package (deadline 18 June 2021) 
 
Due to the limited maximum amount of characters TenneT would like to take the 
opportunity and provide additional comments to questions 30 and 91 of the Open Public 
Consultation on the Hydrogen and Gas Market Decarbonisation Package.  
 
Question 30 
Not only the deployment of electrolysers and scale-up of hydrogen is essential for a 
success of the energy transition and reaching climate neutrality, the right choice of the 
location of electrolysers and its impact on the energy system should not to be 
underestimated. It is more cost efficient to build electrolysers close to the generation of 
renewable electricity and transporting the hydrogen via repurposed gas pipelines to the 
demand centres for hydrogen than fulfilling the transport of RES needs to the demand 
centre, to be convert RES electricity to hydrogen via electrolysers, with much more costly 
additional electricity grids. In order to guarantee that the production of hydrogen does not 
result in in additional build-out of transmission grids or bottlenecks amendments to the 
national and European regulatory and framework are necessary. The following regulatory 
options can contribute to symbiosis between the generation of RES and production of 
hydrogen, while taking into consideration the whole energy system (hydrogen and 
electricity grids) while not putting additional pressure on the transmission infrastructure, 
that might eventually lead to a delay of developing a hydrogen economy in Europe:  
 Combining offshore windfarms tenders with electrolysers as close as possible to 
the generated RES and as best economical suitable to make use of the surplus of 
RES for the production of hydrogen; 
 Subsidised electrolysers being placed at locations that do not result in extra build 
out of the transmission grid or curtailment might be subject to additional financial 
incentives, for instance additional full load hours being subject to national subsidy 
schemes; 
 Barriers for the transportation of hydrogen via retrofitted gas pipelines and for 
storing hydrogen in at storage locations should be removed by European 
legislation in order to financially stimulate the transport via the retrofitted gas 
infrastructure in order to prevail an economically not-feasible build out transmission 
grids; 
 If operation of the electrolysers are able to circumvent curtailment of excess 
renewable electricity without increased re-dispatch needs for congestion 
management they might be subject to temporarily lower network tariffs; 
 The option to redispatch of electrolysers should be considered especially if they 
are placed at location leading to possible bottlenecks; 
 Connection charges may need to be adjusted in terms of locational signals to 
provide incentives that contribute to the efficiency of the overall system. 
Question 91 
As provided in Directive 2019/944 for storage, P2X should be used by market operators. 
However, in case of market failure, TSOs should be allowed to develop and own the asset 
with a fully regulated investment. The operations would be assigned to market operators 
through competitive procedures. Therefore, the operation of such electrolysers would be 
comparable to the current operations of interconnectors where TSOs own they asset and 
provide these assets in a transparent and non-discriminatory manner to market parties. 
The assets are regulated, whereas the associated revenues are covered by specific 
regulations comparable to congestion income on cross border capacity.

SMEunited input to the  EC Roadmap on  
the Data Act 
 
SMEunited welcomes the opportunity to comment on the European Commission’s Roadmap on 
Data Act (including the review of the Directive 96/9/EC on the legal protection of databases), 
published on 28 May 2021.  
SMEunited agrees with the objectives of the initiative. We  recognise the essential role of data 
use for SMEs and the economy overall. The digital transformation , which was accelerated 
during the pandemic,  is changing the way SMEs do their business , showing that d ata is 
becoming increasingly important in this process.  
As, outlined in our position paper on Access to data, those who have access to data possess a 
strong economic asset in the digital economy. Data is a pre -condition to compete, empowers 
companies to innovate and to bring new products and services onto the market, enjoying a 
competitive advantage. For SMEs, in their daily activities, access to data often means the ability 
to compete and continue their business, otherwise they risk to be pushed out of the market. 
Access to data is an issue for SMEs from all sectors, however in some it has already been more 
advanced/visible than in others (e.g. automotive value chain as well as smart home). 
We welcome the Commission attempts to create a European Single Market for data . We ask 
for the creation of a regulatory framework based on fairness in the data economy and on the 
principles outlined in our position paper .  Difficulties of access to and use of data by SMEs in 
the B2B context should be removed by creating the right conditions for companies to compete 
on fair grounds. 
New rules on B2B access to and sharing of data should be clear and coherent with existing 
legislation on the sharing of non-personal and personal data. They should create a regulatory 
framework where all actors feel empowered to share their data in a safe manner.  
Concerning the topic of Business-to-government data sharing for the public interest, we  agree 
with the initiative that it is in the public interest to improve governments’ access to some data 
sources held by private companies. Similarly, it is even more so in the public interest to furth er 
enhance the availability and re-use of publicly-held data for companies, especially SMEs, while 
ensuring compliance with GDPR and other relevant rules. However, in SMEunited view before 
any further initiative is taken in the B2G field, public authoritie s of all levels should firstly fully 
implement and apply the “once only” principle.

Comments from the Danish Chamber of Commerce on the Commission’s 
roadmap/IIA on the Data Act 
 
The Danish Chamber of Commerce is pleased to provide feedback on the European Commission’s 
IIA for the Data Act.  
 
Generally, we believe that use of data will be fundamental for the digital transformation of the Eu-
ropean Union, and that use of data has a great potential to enable us to overcome many of the 
greatest challenges faced by our society e.g. the climate crisis.  
 
We welcome the effort of the European Commission to establish legal certainty and to promote 
more data sharing in the B2B and B2G context. It is important to ensure that any future (legislative) 
framework encourages competition and the development of new business models. 
 
We encourage the aim to increase B2B and B2G data sharing but stress that sharing data must 
always be voluntary. Therefore, legislators should seek to develop incentives, partnerships between 
private and public sector actors and other measures that would encourage voluntary data sharing.  
 
Ensuring legal certainty and transparency for businesses is key to long term success and achieving 
desired outcomes. As a rule, we favor an approach that, 1) takes into account newly implemented 
regulation and/or other initiatives, whose effect is still not fully understood (e.g. the codes of con-
duct developed by SWIPO) and 2) does not create regulatory overlap with other pieces of EU regu-
lation and/or other international frameworks (e.g. OECD, WTO etc.).  
 
The Danish Chamber of Commerce looks forward to following this proposal closely and is available 
for further comments at any time.

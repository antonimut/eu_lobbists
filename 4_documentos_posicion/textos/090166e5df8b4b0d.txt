Urban district with natural watercycle - Offenbach
• Through storm water management with green roofs, 
we are able to rebuild natural water cycles in dense 
urban areas. The green roof systems lead also to a 
high flood prevention. 
• Even severe storm events, with frequencies of 1 in 
100 years, are retained and decentralized in the 
green roof structure and no connection to the sewer 
system is needed.
• All stormwater of the urban district can be handled 
at the district: excess water from the roofs can be 
completely infiltrated into the ground (22%), for 
groundwater rebuilding, the rest of the stormwater 
(78%) can be evaporated. 
• The evaporation cools the surrounding and increases 
biodiversity.

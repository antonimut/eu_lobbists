CONFEDERATION OF INDUSTRY OF THE CZECH REPUBLIC RESPONSE TO 
PUBLIC CONSULTATION - INCEPTION IMPACT ASSESSMENT ON THE 
REVISION OF THE URBAN WASTE WATER TREATMENT DIRECTIVE 
 
The Confederation of Industry of the Czech Republic welcomes the opportunity to comment on the water 
pollution initiative - EU Rules on Urban Wastewater Treatment.  
We fully understand the objectives of strengthening EU legislation to reduce the negative e ffects of 
wastewater (especially urban wastewater) and achieving the zero pollution ambition of the EGD.  
However, in some passages the economic impacts are insufficiently written. To implement the measures you 
mention in the initiative will require a cons iderable amount of money and we are not sure that "energy 
savings" (which is the only one mentioned here) is a factor that can at least partially recover such an 
investment in the short/long term.  
Investment in new technologies, new technology development  and monitoring will be very costly. Perhaps 
for sludge recovery this could theoretically work.  
The Confederation of Industry of the Czech Republic does not like the assessment of the impact on 
administrative burden. It only adds more and more to the administrative burden, which is already high.  
We see another risk in relation to rainwater. Stormwater is drained from the site/area (where e.g. some 
potentially unsuitable substance is stored) and treated before being discharged into the watercourse. We 
would hate to see the plan turn out to require companies to monitor every outfall.

 
 
 
 
Public Affairs LKQ Europe: Position Paper Access to Data & functions 
Free access to in-vehicle data represents an essential issue in LKQ’s agenda on fair competition in Europe.  
Topic/Background:  
• Telematics and digitalization are rapidly advancing and revolutionizing the automotive industry. Vehicles 
generate, collect and exchange data to operate and monitor systems. Data is essential to drive 
innovation and offer better services for drivers, workshops and other stakeholders (e.g. road assistance 
services, fleets management, insurances).  
• Currently, car manufacturers control the access to all communication devices and enjoy a 
privileged position with exclusive access to vehicle information (due to their concern about customer’s data 
security).  
• Some car manufacturers have provided a data access model, but still third-party only receives a 
limited part of in-vehicle data. This further supports the monopoly of OEMs. 
• Vehicle manufacturers are also introducing restrictions to the regulated OBD in-vehicle data access 
& functions. 
Our Target:  
• Direct, independent, unmonitored and real-time & secure access to the vehicle, its data and 
resources via all available and enabled communications channels (e.g. OBD, telematics, USB, HMI)  
• The scope and quality of the data/functionalities shall be at least the same as those available to 
vehicle manufacturers.  
• Equal abilities for all automotive service providers to use bi-directional secure communication via the 
in-vehicle display and/or HMI functions  
• Ability to install securely independent business models (applications) in the vehicle for optimized data 
processing and innovation. 
• A harmonized neutral framework to provide a single point of access to cybersecurity certificates, 
supported by legislative requirements for both this independent access/trust center and for the use of the 
certificates. 
LKQ’s Statements and Demands: 
Fair competition: 
• The monopoly of car manufacturers on any in-vehicle data access channel prevents a fair 
competition throughout the European market and excludes other market players as data is key for future 
competitiveness. Exclusive in-vehicle data access channels or not sharing technical data gives OEMs a game-
changing advantage on the market.  
 LKQ asks to support a fair market and oblige car manufacturers to disclose access to data and 
functions 
Open access to data for all stakeholders at the same time: 
• Car manufacturers should provide full transparency on technically available data, regardless of 
whether they use the data themselves or not. Data should be accessible with the same quality and at the 
latency time for all stakeholders.  
 LKQ supports the access to in-vehicle generated data to allow vehicle diagnostics and predictive 
maintenance for the Independent Aftermarket to ensure competition, innovation and 
entrepreneurship in European mobility services. 
Customer decides him/herself: 
• Car drivers/Consumers should be in full control to decide which service providers can access 
their data, without interference from vehicle manufacturers. If the car driver chooses an independent player 
in the automotive aftermarket, the player needs to get the ability to safely, securely and independently 
interact with the vehicle and the driver remotely also using the in-vehicle Human-Machine-Interface (HMI) 
functions (e.g. via the dashboard or voice commands).

 Basis to all discussion should always be the safety and 
security for car drivers and responsible handling of data (incl. data protection, privacy and 
security). 
Contracts as a basis:  
• Data should not be stored on car manufacturer’s servers but be open accessible for all economic 
stakeholders and not dependent on contractual agreements.  
 LKQ asks for bi-directional communication without the prior permission of the OEM, including no 
monitoring of the data stream by the OEM. 
State-of-the-art parts identification: 
• Electronic parts identification, software and repair information should be accessible for every stakeholder by 
vehicle identification number (VIN) as those are relevant for repair and maintenance. 
 LKQ asks for access to parts identification, software and repair information for independent 
workshops to ensure also free competition and enable customer choice.  
Safety and affordable mobility for everyone: 
• The overall road safety and affordable mobility should be improved throughout Europe and contribute to a 
more sustainable and circular economy. Compliance with data protection rules needs to be 
guaranteed through a direct relationship between the service provider and the client.  
• The pan-European network infrastructure for eCall must be established independently of 
manufacturers. Otherwise, there is a risk that the infrastructure will be used by vehicle manufacturers for 
proprietary applications that go beyond emergency call functions in a way that restricts competition.  
 LKQ supports Europe’s fair competitive automotive industry and therefore, affordable mobility 
for everyone. With telematics, competition is increasing. 
 LKQ believes that Cybersecurity can be and has to be guaranteed, even with the access to all data 
points. 
 LKQ asks to enhance legal guidance on that topic.

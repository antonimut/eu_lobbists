Public Consultation on Data Act 
Bonn, 3rd of September 2021 
ZDK welcomes the European Commission Initiative to create a General Data Act as `lex generalis` to 
regulate data sharing. Please find additional comments on certain topics mentioned in the 
consultation: 
 
I. `Data sharing´  
Throughout the consultations questionnaire and especially in Section II. data sharing is understood as 
more of an exchange of data between businesses.  
Data sharing is in most cases given in the automotive aftermarket mandatory, no act of free choice. 
In certain sectors it is less data sharing than data provision, required by law and necessary to all users 
of data. In the automotive aftermarket sector, the provision of certain data is mandated by the 
vehicle type approval regulation.  
 
II. Fairness Tests, Model Contract Terms and Smart Contracts 
Only a strong sector specific regulation can ensure a level playing field between stakeholders in the 
automotive aftermarket no matter their size (please see below). The instruments mentioned above 
are certainly helpful to create better contractual agreements, but are not able to fix disadvantages in 
competition. 
 
III. Sector specific Rules are necessary 
ZDK calls for a sector specific regulation concerning the access to vehicle data and functions. Given 
the complex structures and business relations in the automotive sector, especially the automotive 
aftermarket, only sector specific legislations can adress the subject appropriately. ZDK supports the 
Secure Onboard-Telematics Platform, a sector specific approach, promoted by the AFCAR Alliance.   
As ZDK we recognize and highly value the efforts of the European commission to regulate the access 
to vehicle data as just presented by the upcoming consultation „In-vehicle generated data – EU rules 
for services based on access to car data“ or the upcoming Motor Vehicle Block Exemption Regulation.

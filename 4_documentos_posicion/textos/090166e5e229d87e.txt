SMEunited input to the EC Roadmap on 
the Revision of the Package Travel 
Directive (PTD) 
SMEunited welcomes the opportunity to comment on the European Commission’s Roadmap on 
the Revision of the Directive (EU) 2015/2302 on package travel and linked travel arrangements, 
published on 6 August 2021. 
SMEunited agrees with the objectives of the initiative. We recognise the essential revision of 
the PTD, specially in lights with major crisis such as the COVID-19 pandemic and the Thomas 
Cook insolvency in 2019. As a result of the European rules, companies are bearing the full risks 
and burdens in crisis times, with disastrous consequences for the sector. 
According to Art 3(2)( b), packages are combinations of travel services put together by one 
trader " irrespective of whether separate contracts are concluded with individual travel service 
providers, those services are  purchased from a single point of sale and those services have 
been selected before the traveller agrees to pay ". This current definition of Packages is too 
broad and force mere travel agents artificially into the role of a tour organiser which is 
absolutely disproportionate. Furthermore (very) small businesses like hotels can easily turn into 
“tour organisers” just by off ering combined offers costumers usually expect and have to cope 
with a regulatory regime that is tailor-made for major tour operators. A more targeted definition 
of Packages is needed. 
The Covid -19 crisis had and still has severe consequences for the touri sm and hospitality 
sector. Package travel providers (hereafter: travel organisations) currently bear the full risk of 
unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances. Small and large providers cannot or hardly bear 
the high risks and costs arising from circums tances that are completely beyond the control of 
travel organisations. Travel companies experience severe financial problems and large scale 
forced redundancy of employees due to the disproportionate risk distribution resulting from the 
obligations of the PTD. 
 
In addition, there is a clear u nbalanced distribution of risks in the chain . Travel organisations 
bear the full burden and risks of extreme and unavoidable circumstances. SMEunited advocates 
for a limitation of liability in exceptional situations, su ch as a pandemic.  In the event of 
additional waves of the Covid-19 virus, or the outbreak of a future pandemic, there is a good 
chance that travel companies will not be able to meet the obligations under the PTD.  
The distinction between Linked Travel Arra ngements (LTA) and packages requires 
simplification. Especially for the stationary distribution of travel agents the difference between

COMMENTS OF TELESAT 
 
1 Telesat welcomes the opportunity to provide these Comments in response to the Inception 
Impact Assessment on the Establishment of a European Union (EU) Space-based Global Secure 
Connectivity System issued by the Directorate-General for Defence Industry and Space (DEFIS), 
and applauds the EU’s initiative to consult on the optimal pathway to do so. Access to a secure 
and resilient global connectivity network that addresses the security, sovereignty, and  strategic 
connectivity needs of the entire EU as well as the growing demand for high-speed broadband and 
its industrial and social applications, is a highly strategic and major undertaking that will benefit 
EU citizens for decades to come.

digital requirements but also harness the immense economic, technological and strategic benefits 
that stem from building these constellations.

II. TELESAT LIGHTSPEED IS THE BEST SOLUTION FOR EUROPE 
Key objectives 
9 Telesat Lightspeed will not only be the fastest and lowest risk solution to establish a highly 
secure, redundant, end-to-end connectivity network for Europe but will also achieve every one of 
the EU’s objectives for a LEO constellation.  
General Objective 1: Support implementation of the EU policies through ubiquitous, secure, 
reliable and affordable connectivity 
10 Telesat Lightspeed will directly enable the  implementation of EU policies through the 
delivery of  a ubiquitous, secure, reliable and affordable high-speed connectivity network, that 
tailors to the needs of every EU citizen, government and institution. With its next generation 
characteristics, world leading capacity  and through a potential EU -Telesat partnership, Telesat 
Lightspeed can ensure that the EU has sovereign access to a robust, highly redundant and self -
sustaining, high-performance network that will enable every connectivity use case possible for the 
EU’s governments and citizens, ranging from growing complex government functions and digital 
needs, to emerging cybersecurity and political threats worldwide.  Having access to this digital 
network will enable the EU to implement and innovate a wide range of strategic digital policies, 
projects, and solutions, today and in the future.  
 
 - 4 - 
General Objective 2: Safeguard EU sovereignty and security and avoid excessive reliance on 
non-EU based solutions, in particular for security-sensitive functions 
11 Due to its extensive capabilities and highly robust design, Telesat Lightspeed is the optimal 
solution for the transmission of secure data across the EU and will enable multiple use cases 
including the opportunity to establish  a global EU-based quantum enabled communications 
network, a communications relay network  for European spacecraft, and significantly upgrade 
National Security Space Communications . Telesat Lightspeed will also enable a wide range of 
government use cases that include security -sensitive government and military functions whether 
in the air, at sea, on land, or on remote bases. Furthermore, Telesat Lightspeed’s space relay 
abilities will allow for  the development of w arning and g lobal detection systems, n avigation, 
climate monitoring, radio frequency collection, radar capacity, military communications, and 
scientific missions. Through a potential EU-Telesat partnership, the extent and variety of such use 
cases will help the EU rely considerably less on solutions and external connectivity infrastructure 
that will be a key strategic foundation for the future of connectivity in the EU. 
Specific Objective 1: Ensure resilient and secure connections with advanced technological 
features meeting EU needs on a globally competitive basis;  
12 The Telesat Lightspeed network will offer the EU the widest possible range of use cases 
and applications than any other LEO constellation, due to its world-leading technological features 
paired with affordable access to terabits of global dedicated high-speed broadband capacity. Not 
only will Lightspeed’s high throughput and low latency meet the growing connectivity demands 
everywhere in the EU, but it will also ensure that demand hotspots are never underserved by 
dynamically allocating capacity to where it is most needed. This makes for the most resilient,  
redundant, and highly secure network that is globally competitive and that will ensure the EU ’s 
connectivity infrastructure is in line  with the future of broadband infrastructure and technology  
globally. 
Specific Objective 2: Provide high-speed broadband availability across the EU and worldwide 
to overcome existing gaps and addressing growing data and service demands in support of the 
inclusive and sustainable development of EU regions

Specific Objective 3: Ensure that the considered solutions provide the EU with an appropriate 
level of strategic autonomy in terms of technologies, assets, operations and services 
14 Through a potential EU-Telesat partnership and by working hand-in-hand with the EU and

tomorrow’s strategic functions and growing complex needs.

Key problem 1: Lack of resilient, secure telecommunication infrastructures and rising threats 
16 In today’s growing digital economy and rapid technolog ical changes, hybrid threats and 
cyberattacks appear every day in new, evolving forms. This has reshaped the global political scene 
and has made way for new threats and power dynamics. It is imperative for every government and 
institution to have access to the most cutting -edge, advanced cybersecurity systems and digital 
lines of defence to ensure that highly sensitive data is protected. Telesat’s LEO ne twork has an 
unprecedented ability to transmit  secure data with its highly redundant space relay abilities , 
minimizing the risk of hybrid attacks and data leaks more than any other LEO network. Lightspeed 
will support the transmission of multiple terabits  of secure data across the EU and the world, 
supporting key highly sensitive functions in addition to day-to-day use.  
Key problem 2: Growing data and service demands , which EU global digital competitiveness 
requires to be met 
17 Telesat Lightspeed is scalable and will support government and enterprise use case s by 
providing substantial amounts of data capacity, low latency, and a highly secure network. It will 
also enable a secure, EU -centric 5G network that will boost the development of industry 4. 0 
applications in addition to ensuring affordable high-speed Internet and 5G connectivity 
everywhere, driving economic growth and social prosperity. 
Key problem 3: Insufficient connectivity infrastructure within the EU and in the regions of 
strategic interest 
18 Telesat Lightspeed – with its state -of-the-art optical inter -satellite links – will directly 
address this problem by ensuring a truly global high-speed broadband network that connects the 
EU to every corner of the world, including the Arctic and Antarctic, the Atlantic, every continent 
and every ocean globally. The robustness and reliability of Lightspeed will support the 
development of policies, missions, and overall demand from government al and institutional 
European users, dramatically lowering costs and eliminating the need to incur high, non-recurring 
costs and the acquisition of project-specific capabilities.

Key problem 4: Risk of strategic dependency & loss of digital sovereignty 
19 Today’s global space race  poses many challenges for countries worldwide wishing to 
maintain a competitive level of sovereignty and technological advancement given the speed and 
scale at which LEO constellations are being developed globally. This sets the stage for present and 
future risks of digital dependency, exposure to external risks, and loss of strategic sovereignty and 
competitiveness. Through Telesat’s extensive investments in its European supply chain, including 
the selection of Thales Alenia Space as the prime manufacturer  of Telesat Lightspeed, coupled 
with a further EU-Telesat partnership, the EU could ensure that it remains at the forefront of space 
communications technology and has a long-term, scalable, strategic, highly capable and sovereign 
global connectivity network.  
Key problem 5: Digital di vide, which challenges socio -economic development and a fully 
functioning single market

IV. CONCLUSION 
26 As the world emerges from the global COVID-19 pandemic, now more than ever, industry, 
governments, and countries need to come together to stimulate the econo my, create jobs, and 
deliver essential services to their citizens. The importance of reliable high -speed broadband has 
never been more important and with the inevitable growth of teleworking, 5G, and the Internet of 
Everything, a European Space-based Global Secure Connectivity System, sensibly and responsibly 
pursued, has never been more critical. In addition, the New Space economy is widely recognized 
as one of the most promising, high growth industries in the coming decades and Europe must take 
affirmative and thoughtful steps to ensure it is well positioned to compete.  
27 World-leading European companies , including Thales Alenia Space , are strongly 
positioned to be at the forefront of this growing industry, as a direct result of their participation in 
the L ightspeed program . Telesat L ightspeed is the single largest investment ever made by a 
Canadian space firm, injecting billions of dollars into the European Union . Telesat Lightspeed 
represents a tremendous opportunity for the European space industry , while also representing a 
unique opportunity for Europe to secure its own Space-based Global Secure Connectivity System 
to advance important public policy objectives, including bridging the digital divide throughout 
Europe. As noted above, the Telesat Lightspeed constellation will support 1 ,500 jobs across 
Europe at a time when investment and job creation have never been more critical, and position the 
European space sector to thrive in the New Space economy.  
28 Telesat and the EU partnering on Telesat Lightspeed  to create a European Space-based 
Global Secure Connectivity System is an example of industry, governments and countries coming 
together to embrace new, innovative and disruptive projects to deliver benefits – both near term 
and long term - for their citizens. A project of this nature would be a priority in the best of times. 
In light of the economic wreckage brought by COVID-19, it must now be a top priority. Telesat 
would welcome the chance to discuss this important opportunity at your earliest convenience.

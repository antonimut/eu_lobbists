CITA input for the review of the ELV Directive 
 
 
Background 
 
CITA is the worldwide not-for-profit association of authorities and authorised companies active 
in the field of vehi cle compliance www.citainsp.org. We are one of the partners of the UNEP 
project “Safer and Cleaners Used Vehicles for Africa”. 
 
Scope 
 
Our field of expertise is and reason to give our input for this public consultation is the export of 
used vehicles out of the EU. 
 
Inputs 
 
Definition of a  usable vehicle: the European Union cons iders the ve hicle r oadworthiness 
certificate (Dir 2014/45/EU) as the proof that a vehicle can be used on the road. It is logical to 
apply the same criteria for exporting: an acceptable vehicle for the EU is an acceptable vehicle 
to export. 
 
Sovereignty of recipient co untries: countries recipient of used vehicles shall definer their 
acceptance criteria. 
 
The role of exporting countries: exporting countries, like the EU Member States shall: 
• Support recipient countries to set up their vehicle compliance schemes. 
• Facilitate the control of exported used vehicles, accor ding to the criteria of importing 
countries. 
• Provide reliable informat ion about each used exported vehicle  – IT communication 
platform between registration authorities in a model like EUCARIS-EREG. 
Additional aspects to take into account 
• The enforcement of importing countries is essential. If only the EU controls the fitness 
of used exported vehicles, recipient countries will keep receiving them from somewhere 
else. That is happening today with substandard new motorcycles in West Africa. 
• This document only considers the control of design and fitness of used vehicles leaving 
the Union. Further considerations, like those related to circular economy, are out of the 
scope.

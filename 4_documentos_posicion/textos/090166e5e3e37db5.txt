ACE reply to the European Commission inception impact assessment 
(IIA) consultation on food waste – reduction targets 
 
The Alliance for Beverage Cartons and the Environment (ACE) and its members BillerudKorsnäs, Elopak, 
SIG Combibloc, Stora Enso and Tetra Pak, welcome the European Commission initiative to tackle food waste 
via regulatory action. We support a robust legislative initiative which will tackle food waste at its core and 
contribute to a carbon-neutral and circular economy, as mandated by the European Green Deal.  This impact 
assessment of this proposal should be coherent with other relevant policy initiatives, and take into account 
the measures proposed via the revision of the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Directive, the Food 
Contact Materials Regulation and Farm to Fork initiatives.  
 
We recommend taking the following elements into consideration when designing  policies 
targeting food waste:   
• The positive contribution of food packaging and processing to prevent food waste and extend shelf-
life must be fully recognised in future policies.  
• The food packaging and processing sector must be included in discussions to support food loss and 
waste reduction goals 
 
Food packaging is instrumental to minimise food waste 
EU regulators, in line with FAO recommendations1, should promote appropriate measures for 
increasing shelf-life and improving packaging and storage to extend consumption time and to 
reduce food loss. Food packaging plays a vital role in the delivery of essential goods by enabling the 
safety, accessibility, affordability and availability of food supplies across the EU. Increasing the shelf life of 
a product has a direct impact on food waste, helping to reduce it from 8% to 3% (documented cases).2  
• Beverage cartons protect food from spoiling, reduce food waste3, and can help reduce the EU’s GHG 
emissions. Beverage cartons are an indispensable part of life today, keeping food and beverages 
safe, accessible, and fresh for consumers, while protecting beverages and food products against 
shelf life degradation (spoiling) caused by light, air, and contaminants.  Products packaged in 
beverage cartons can be stored up to 12 months without refrigeration, removing the need for added 
preservatives, saving additional energy, and eliminating carbon emissions associated with powering 
the refrigeration process.4 
 
Collaboration is key to tackling food waste 
We encourage the EU policy makers to continue an open dialogue with stakeholders across the 
value chain to ensure food loss is minimised. Packaging should be considered an ally in the fight against 
food waste given its capacity to keep food fresh for longer and protect it at transport and retail stages.   
 
ACE is looking forward to contributing with its knowledge, experience, and expertise in this process.

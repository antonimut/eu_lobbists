Subject: EUROPUR comments to the public consultation on End-of-life vehicles - evaluating the EU rules 
 
 
In addition to th e an swers and comments added where possible , EUROPUR would like to provide further 
information to some of the answers provided. 
 
Question 7: Do you agree that more recycled plastics should be used in the manufacturing of new vehicles? 
Answer: EUROPUR agrees on this regard  and adds the need of enabling conditions for more recycl ed 
plastics to be used in manufacturing of new vehicles.  
 
Question 41: Please indicate whether you agree with the following statement: 
To ensure that a high quality of recycling is achieved, it is necessary to compensate the authorised treat ment 
facilities (ATFs) for their dismantling efforts that, under the current conditions, are not economically viable. 
Answer: EUROPUR agrees and recommends to consider phase-in time and standards. 
 
Question 43. The following options were  recommended in the  study mentioned above. Which of them do you 
think should be considered in the proposal for the revision of the ELV Directive? 
Answer: In addition to the options available, EUROPUR would like to add the recommendation to install 
sorting technologies for chemical recycling.

About EUROPUR: 
 
EUROPUR is the European Association of Flexible PU Foam Blocks Producers. Its members operate PU 
foam production plants in 28 countries in geographical Europe.  Together, EUROPUR members operate 
>60% of the PU foam plants in the European Economic Area and produce around 700,000 tonnes of PU 
foam (+/- 70% of the total production in the region). Some of them also produce mattresses and furniture 
directly.

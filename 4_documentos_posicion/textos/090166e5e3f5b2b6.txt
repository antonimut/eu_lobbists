 
 
 
 
About LKQ Europe and Atracco Reuse and 
Recycling Operations 
LKQ Europe, a subsidiary of LKQ Corporation, with its head office in Zug, Switzerland, is the 
leading distributor of automotive aftermarket parts for cars, commercial vans and industrial 
vehicles in Europe. It currently employs approximately 26,000 people with a network of 
1,000 branches and approximately USD 5.5 billion in revenue in 2020. The organization 
supplies around 100,000 independent workshops in over 20 European countries. 
The group includes Euro Car Parts, Fource, RHIAG Group, Elit, Auto Kelly, and 
STAHLGRUBER Group, as well as recycling specialist, Atracco. LKQ also holds a minority 
interest in Mekonomen Group. 
Atracco is a fast growing company and a leader in the Nordic countries in environmentally 
friendly car dismantling and sales of car parts over the internet. The business consists of 
recycling and sales of used car parts as well as materials such as metal, rubber, glass etc. 
Atracco works with a clear environmental and sustainable focus and is working for more 
efficient car dismantling with increased quantity and improved quality of dismantled and 
recycled car parts in damage repairs. No parts are wasted - Atracco dismantles everything 
directly and assure the quality of parts that are put into storage. 
"We are convinced that environmental and efficient clean-up/dismantling, as well as closer customer partnerships 
and e-commerce, are key words for the future of car dismantling. By reusing car parts, consumers, garages and 
insurance companies save the environment and the economic benefits are significant."

Dismantling Process Flow at Atracco 
 
Atracco Norrköping 
 
 
Dismantle 
Atracco uses an industry-developed computer system, which helps the company to work in a 
structured and efficient way with everything from registration and stock management to sales 
and delivery. When the car enters the workshop, the car is registered. Some information 
about the car is taken from the vehicle register and others are entered manually into the 
computer system.  
 
The "dismantling card" includes: 
 Model Designation - The unique code of the car model. 
 Color body / code - The unique code of the body. 
 Engine size - The displacement of the engine. 
 Body type - The body of the car. For example, sedan or station wagon. 

 
 
 
 
 Color interior / code - What code the color interior has. 
 Gearbox type / code - Gearbox type or gearbox code. 
 Mile - How far the car has been driven. 
 Engine type / code - Engine type or engine code. 
 Rear axle gear ratio - How many turns the engine must rotate for the rear axle to 
rotate one turn. 
 
Statistics, methods & long-standing routines 
Before the car is dismantled, it is also checked that both the registration number and chassis 
number match the information on the dismantling card. The methods and procedures used 
at Atracco to determine which parts to stock are based on extensive knowledge and 
statistics. 
 
Quality assessment before dismantling 
Before disassembly, parts should be checked for visible damage. It is a natural part of the 
work to assess, test and check all components. This is an important part of the competence 
for dismantling. 
Once the correct component has been disassembled, it is marked with a unique reference 
number. The part is now ready to be placed in our warehouse, and the customer can find it 
by searching for it using your registration number or the part's part number on our website. 
All vehicles are environmentally decontaminated during dismantling.

LKQ Corporation 
LKQ Corporation, the parent company of LKQ Europe,  is the leading provider of alternative 
and specialty parts to repair and accessorize automobiles and other vehicles. LKQ continues 
to be a thought leader in driving the industry forward, in everything from efficiency and 
sustainability to product quality to expansion into other related businesses, like 
services. LKQ has been an environmental leader since the day then company was founded 
in 1998 as a salvage dismantler and recycler of passenger vehicles. Today LKQ is the 
largest recycler of vehicles in the world, with its North America operations recycling more 
than 90% of the materials from the vehicles we procure that would otherwise go into landfills.

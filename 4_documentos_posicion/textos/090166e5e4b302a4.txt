 
 
FinCo Fuel Group and GoodFuels Position on amended Renewable Energy Directive 
 
Fit for 55 package and global outreach 
FinCo Fuel Group and GoodFuels welcome the proposal for an amended Renewable Energy Directive, which in combination 
with the other proposals provide a sound mix of regulatory measures on the path to climate neutrality by 2050. The 
inclusion of dedicated targets for the shipping and aviation sector is received with enthusiasm. We believe that these 
proposals serve as buildings blocks for the goal – a global framework of legislation and regulation to decarbonize the 
transport sector.  
 
We moreover believe in a consistent package to reach the overall Fit for 55 targets, in which technology neutrality must be at 
the forefront and renewable and sustainable fuels must be able to contribute based on an objective well-to-wheel 
measurement.  
 
Ambition and target 
We are positive about the shift to a GHG intensity reduction goal instead of a minimum renewable energy share. The 
suggested target of 13% GHG intensity reduction for the transport sector is a first start, nevertheless the market can already 
do more, also taking into consideration that the FQD already sets a target of 6% CO2 reduction in 2020.  
 
As a result, we are in favour of a more ambitious and market stimulating target, increasingly pushing the industry to invest 
and scale up. Certain elements of the proposal allow for softening of the target by allowing the target to be met despite 
lower actual GHG reductions:  
- The current 13% target includes green RFNBO’s used as intermediate product for conventional fuel production, 
whereby these feedstocks cannot be fully allocated to transport.    
- Member States have the option to decrease their GHG reduction target for transport if they lower the cap on 
conventional feedstocks. This does not support the overall ambition to lower CO2-emissions in transport.  
- Renewable Electricity for EV’s is already considered in the overall Fit for 55 GHG reduction target and does not add 
additional renewables into transport. 
 
Sustainable biofuels 
The European Commission foresees that electrification is an important pillar for the road sector to decarbonise. And the EC 
recognises the shipping sector being a hard to abate sector. Our mission is to propel decarbonization in those transport 
sectors that cannot rely on electrification, in specific the heavy-duty road and shipping sector. We are already doing this in 
the most sustainable and cost-effective way possible, which also for decades to come is realized through the introduction of 
sustainable biofuels. We therefore urge the Commission to take note of the fact that an increasing amount of annex IX A and 
B feedstocks are required to realize these targets. 
 
Double counting and multiplier 
We encourage the removal of the existing double counting mechanism. In short-term this will lead to more Annex IX A fuels 
on the market, which is crucial for a further scale up of these types of fuels. This way progress reporting towards reaching 
national renewable energy targets will also be based on real uptake, and not be artificially inflated.  
 
Closing statement 
FinCo Fuel Group and GoodFuels, frontrunners in decarbonizing the transport sector, are of the opinion that to maximize 
GHG emission reductions, targets need to be higher, and all parties need to act now. We invite the members of the 
Commission for a dialogue to further discuss our viewpoints and solutions to increase the share of renewable energy in 
different transports modes and hereby propelling decarbonization.  
 
Thank you for your consideration.

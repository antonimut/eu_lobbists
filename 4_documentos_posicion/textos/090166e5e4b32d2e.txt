Position and suggestions on the proposed “Fit for 55” Package
Terégawelcomes the"Fit for 55" packagepresentedbytheEuropeanCommission(EC), whichconstitutes theappropriate legislative toolbox toachievethenewclimateobjectives set bytheEuropeanclimatelaw: a55%reduction of GHG emissions by 2030 compared to 1990, as well as carbon neutrality by 2050.
Terégawelcomes theambitious renewableenergytargets for 2030,whichwillcertainlymaketheEUapioneerindecarbonisationandamodel for thirdcountries' industrial strategies. Renewableandlow-carbongases,aswell as the existing gas infrastructure, will be neededtoachievethesetargets inacost-effectiveway. Theambitious reform of the ETS mechanism also enables setting clear market signals for renewable gases,especially for hydrogen and biomethane.
Althoughmassiveelectrificationwill bekeyfor achievingclimatetargets andisadriveroftheEuropeanGreenDeal, Terégavoices concerns regardingthehighlevelofelectrificationplannedbytheEuropeanCommissioninthe latter, which risks replacing the existing monopoly of oil by another: electricity. Teréga sees bothelectricity and renewable and low-carbon gas as being complementary and would like to stress theimportance of relying on different types of energies to guarantee the flexibility of the energy systemandsecurity of supply, in particular when relyingonthestorages providedbythegas system, andtoensuretheadoption of the most cost-efficient decarbonisation option, relating to different cases and uses.
Thus, Teréga provides several recommendations and areas for improvement to make the Fit for 55packageevenmoreeffectiveandinparticulartofurtherclarifytheroleofallrenewableenergysourcesby2030,andseta frameworkfor upcomingtexts, especiallyregardinglow-carbonsolutions. Furthermore, Terégasupports theneed to ensure coherence between the Fit For 55 package and other European legislation, especially thefutureHydrogen&Gas decarbonisationpackagetobeunveiledonthe14thofDecember2021,thetaxonomyand the currently under revision TEN-E regulation, as well as the RFNBO delegated act.
Please note that this paper shortly highlights Teréga’s main attention points in order to make surethat thenet-zero objective set by the EC is reached as swiftly as possible and at the lowest-possible cost for thecommunity, based on a sector integration approach.
Teréga’s positionis instrongsynergywiththepolicyresponsepublishedbytheconsortiumGasforClimate:TheFit for 55 package and Gas for Climate – Making 2030 EU climate targets achievable with a clear role forrenewable and low-carbon gases.

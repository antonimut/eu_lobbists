 
Date: 18 November 2021 
 
 
 
 
Liquid Wind’s consultation response regarding the re-vision of 
the Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Directive (AFID).   
 
 
Liquid Wind welcomes the European Commission’s initiative to revise the Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Directive as 
a part of the Fit-for-55 legislative package.  
Liquid Wind is a Swedish Power-to-Fuel Development Company committed to reducing carbon emissions. We 
develop, finance, build and manage replicable facilities for production of the renewable electro-fuel eMethanol, to 
accelerate the transition to carbon neutral shipping and transportation.  
Liquid Wind believes that infrastructure will be the backbone for using renewable and carbon neutral fuels paving the 
way for climate neutrality by 2050. Therefore, we have strong concerns regarding the proposal’s focus on LNG. LNG is 
a fossil-based fuel and do not deliver the GHG benefits required for meeting the target of climate neutrality. Further, it 
risks diverting important investments in infrastructure that instead should be used for renewable fuels. Liquid Wind 
would instead suggest similar support to increase the development of infrastructure for renewable fuels, such as 
eMethanol.  
As an easy-to-handle, liquid fuel eMethanol is well suited to take a prominent role in the energy transition of maritime 
transport. With regards to infrastructure, renewable liquid fuels require only minor, low-cost, modifications to existing 
bunkering infrastructure and fuel supply systems, such as storage tanks, pipelines, fuel pumps, and bunkering barges, 
unlike fuels which may require refrigeration and high-pressure storage.  
As large volumes of methanol intended for chemical applications are already stored in multiple ports, mandated 
deployment of fuel infrastructure would facilitate methanol integration into maritime transport. By setting a target for 
deployment of infrastructure for renewable fuels in ports, the Commission would stimulate adaptation of existing 
infrastructure to the handling of fuels which may deliver immediate climate and local air quality benefits.

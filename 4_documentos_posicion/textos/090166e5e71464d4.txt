Proposal for a regulation on shipments of waste 
 
and amending Regulation n°1257/2013 and n°2020/1056 
 
COM (2021) 709 final of 17th November 2021 
 
EUCOPRO comments 
 
 
 
EUCOPRO (European Association for Co-processing) is an association composed of waste pre-treatment 
companies that prepare alternative fuels and raw materials mainly from hazardous wastes, with the aim of 
recovering them in co-processing processes, such as cement kilns. Members of EUCOPRO operate plants 
located in several EU countries including Belgium, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, 
Poland, and Romania.  
 
 
 
EUCOPRO welcomes the proposal for a regulation on shipments of waste, adopted by the Commission, on 
17th November 2021, as this draft: 
 
o Remains a procedure regulation, 
o Maintains the structure of the current waste shipment regulation and the strict notification procedure for 
hazardous waste shipment, 
o Brings clarification, introduces numerous procedure improvements, and asks for implementing an 
electronic procedure. All these evolutions will reduce delays for the notification procedure and more 
generally will facilitate the use of waste in a circular economy, the processing of notification requests and 
waste transfers. 
 
In addition, Eucopro finds very positive the following points: 
 
o The proposal for a harmonized calculation method for the financial guarantee (article 7 – financial 
guarantee), 
o The tacit consent possibility from the dispatch authorities, which will help to reduce delay (article 9 – 
consents by the competent authorities and time periods for transport, recovery, or disposal), 
o A better regulatory framework for the Pre-consent facilities, and their new related provisions, which will 
reach to develop the use of such installations for waste shipment for recovery (article 14 – Pre-consent 
facilities). 
 
However, the draft text calls for some comments, objections and/or suggestions for improvement, which are 
explained below. Many of these comments come from feedback from our members. Proposals for 
amendments (*) are suggested in the annex.

Eucopro comments –14th January 2022 - Proposal for a regulation on shipments of waste –2 
 
 
o Article 4 – Overall procedure framework & Article 18 – General information requirements  
 
 Article 4 - point 4. The procedure for shipment of waste sample for analysis has been strengthened 
with an extension to shipment of waste for experimental treatment trials. Eucopro understands the 
reasons for this reinforcement, in particular for the shipment of waste for experimental treatment 
trials. 
However, for shipment of waste samples for analysis, the proposed procedure seems to be a heavy 
administrative work without real environmental added value, as it concerns very limited quantity. In 
addition, there is no added value in requesting a contract, and certain provisions of article 18 are not 
applicable to a shipment of waste sample for analysis.  
We suggest adding a point 12 to article 18 indicating that “For shipment of waste explicitly destined 
for laboratory analysis, points 4 to 11 of article 18 are not applicable”.* 
 
o Article 8 – Requests for information and documentation by the competent authorities 
 
 Eucopro welcomes the addition of a delay of 7 days to frame the response of the notifier.  
Such a delay will be enough when few additional documents would be asked. But some national 
authorities could ask additional waste analysis or numerous additional documents, with need of 
translation. 
In such cases, Eucopro is wondering on how the 7 days delay could be ensured. Would it be possible 
to give recommendations to limit request for additional documents and to introduce possibility of 
adaptation for the deadline in case of translation and additional analysis? 
 
 Eucopro asks for equal treatment between the Member States and the notifiers on the deadlines  
framework specified in Article 8: for the Member States, it is mentioned that these are working days 
and not for the notifiers. As result, we ask for the introduction of “working days” in points 8.2, 8.3 
(second paragraph), and 8.5 (first sentence).* 
 
 Improve the article 8 structure. 
Points 1 to 4 (first paragraph) concern the request for additional documents by the authority of 
dispatch when the notification received is not properly carried out. 
Point 4 (second paragraph) and Point 5 (first two paragraphs) concern the request for additional 
documents by the other competent authorities when the notification is not considered as completed. 
In order to clarify the reading, Eucopro asks that the different purposes of this article would be 
precised in separate paragraphs. 
 
 Article 26.1 of the draft regulation provides for requests and sending of additional documents to be 
made by electronic means. Eucopro asks that these provisions be compulsory as soon as the 
provisions of this new regulation are applied.

o Article 9 – Consents by competent authorities (feedback) 
 
 Eucopro welcomes the clarification introduced with the following sentence “a shipment shall have left 
the country of dispatch by the end of the period of validity of the tacit or written consents of the 
competent authorities concerned” (article 9.4).  
 
In addition: 
 
 to improve more the administrative follow-up of the notification procedure, specifically when transit 
countries are included, we suggest adding a requirement about a summary document in which all 
the received agreements would be listed *. 
 
 To clarify the dates of the notification validity, we suggest that the beginning of the one year’s 
notification should be based on the date of the last agreement received*. 
 
o Article 11 – Prohibition of shipments of waste destined for disposal – Point 3 
In case where the competent authorities concerned have not authorised a planned shipment, it shall 
provide the notifier by an electronic mean a motivated explanation.* 
 
o Article 13 – General notification -– point a) 
“the waste contained in the different shipment has essentially similar physical and chemical 
characteristics”. 
Eucopro considers that the interpretation of this item must be identical by all the Member States. We 
suggest that it can be clarified by the Committee of Experts of the Member States. 
 
o Article 13 - points 1c & 2 - General notification – Same routing (feedback) 
 
In many cases, modification of the route indicated in the notification can happen (closed tunnel, restriction 
of circulation…). Procedure related to modification of the route indicated in the notification file are very 
heavy in terms of administrative management, both for the notifier and the competent authorities.  In order 
to decrease the administrative burden, we suggest including possibility of alternative routes, in the original 
notification *. See also our comments on article 17. 
 
 
o Article 13 (and article 5) – General notification - Renewal of a notification (feedback) 
 
Eucopro would like to include a procedure to frame the renewal of a notification, as long as the information 
contained therein remain unchanged (composition of the waste, producer / notifier, destination facility, 
route, ...). This would reduce the administrative burden of the competent authorities and of the notifier. 
This procedure would not be applicable when the transfers, made under the corresponding previous 
notification, have been subject to sanctions.

o Article 16.3  (and article 15.3) – Requirements following consent to a shipment -  “The facility shall, within 
one day of receipt of the waste, provide confirmation to the notifier and the relevant authorities that the 
waste has been received”. 
 
 Eucopro wishes to ensure that the term "receipt" relates to the date of the waste arrival at the site 
and not the date on which it was accepted. Indeed, in certain cases (for example, packaged waste), 
the date of arrival on the site may differ from the date of acceptance. Eucopro suggests defining this 
notion of “receipt” as the date of the shipment arrival on the installation.* 
 
 Another proposal could be to introduce, in the block 18 of the movement document, the two dates : 
the date of arrival and the date of acceptance. 
 
 The WSR new proposal decrease from 3 days to one day the time given to the facility to provide 
confirmation to the notifier and the relevant authorities that the waste has been received. In some 
cases, for example when receipt takes place just before the weekend and or during holidays, 
flexibility could be needed. As a result, Eucopro would prefer to keep the 3 days delay.   
 
o Article 17 – Changes in the shipment after consent – First paragraph – last sentence. 
“Changes in the intended quantity, route, routing, date of shipment or carrier shall constitute essential 
changes”. 
 
 Eucopro considers that changes in routing, date of shipment, carrier, or intended quantity (in case of 
limited modification) are not to be considered as essential changes. However, there is a need to 
frame such modifications, either : 
­ as proposed in our previous comments on article 13, points 1c & 2, to introduce in the general 
notification proposals of alternatives (routing, carriers) and, 
­ to define a dedicated procedure to inform in time the competent authorities of these changes. 
This procedure may however include for the competent authority the possibility of requesting a 
new notification if it considers that the proposed modifications are essential changes compared 
to the initial notification file. 
The purpose of these suggestions is to decrease administrative burden for both the notifiers and the 
authorities compared to the introduction of a new notification. 
 
 For Eucopro, essential changes are mainly modifications of the waste code, of the type of treatment 
and of the intermediary or final treatment installations. In these cases, a new notification shall be 
submitted. 
 
 As a conclusion, Eucopro asks to include in this article two types of procedures : one for essential 
changes (new notification) and a second for less essential changes such as listed in our first bullet.

o Article 22. 4 – Take-back when a shipment cannot be completed or intended (feedback) 
 
We suggest in order to facilitate the implementation of this article and to decrease the administrative 
burden both for the competent authorities and the notifiers: 
 
• to consider as a first step the sending by the initial competent authority of dispatch, to the other 
competent authorities, of a duly motivated request, 
 
• to ask to these competent authorities to give an answer under 24 hours if they accept or not to use 
the original notification, and only in case of disagreement to submit a new notification. 
 
o Article 28 – Disagreement on classification issues 
 
Eucopro suggests adding a point in this article (or in article 9) in order to protect the notifier when 
authorities of dispatch and destination agree on the content of the notification: the authorities of transit 
should not ask for substantial changes, such as Basel code, EWC code…. 
 
Proposal of addition : “where the competent authorities of the countries of dispatch and destination agree 
on the content of the notification, the transit countries competent authorities cannot request essential 
changes to the notification». 
 
o Annex I – B - Movement document (and corresponding points in Annex I-C) (Feedback) 
 
 Block 1 - In order to improve the administrative follow-up for the notifier and to avoid unwanted “illegal 
shipment”, we suggest including, in the movement document, the dates of the beginning and of the 
end of the notification (proposal to add a block 1a)*, 
 
 Block 18 – Quantity received. This quantity must be stated at the time of receipt of the waste. It is 
specified that the actual weight should be considered. However, at time of receipt, it is not possible 
to say if the waste will be accepted or rejected until tests are done, as well as the quantities which 
will be accepted. An alternative should be to indicate an estimate weight based on the formula 
“estimated weight deducted from the theoretical tare weight”, under the reserve that justification 
document will be kept by the treatment site if difference of weight would exist*.

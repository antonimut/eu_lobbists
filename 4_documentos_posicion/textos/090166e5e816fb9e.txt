Subject: Environmental impact of waste management – revision of EU waste 
framework 
 
Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE), the association representing the plastics recycling industry, 
welcomes the initiative’s objective to harmonise separate waste collection rules at EU level. 
 
Our association supports the revision of the Waste Framework Directive (WFD)1 as an 
opportunity to introduce measures that lead to a clear harmonised distinction between waste 
as a resource from waste to be disposed of. In this sense, waste reduction measures should 
be directed towards maximizing re-usability and recyclability while reducing the amount of 
waste destined to disposal. 
 
PRE agrees on the necessity to prioritize harmonised separate collections by “clarifying and/or 
restricting the scope of derogations provided for in Article 10(3) WFD, introducing minimum 
requirements for source segregation and separate collection of waste that facilitate separate 
collection of used items for re-use and preparation for re-use and high-quality recycling of 
waste”. 
 
Minimum requirements for source segregation should be inspired by the proposed essential 
requirements for packaging annexed to the Packaging and Packaging Waste Directive 
(PPWD),2 i.e., design for recycling, a harmonized definition for recyclability, recycled content 
targets, and material specific rules and targets on waste to disposal generation.  
 
Further, the revision of the WFD should harmonize the way EU Member States (MS) interpret 
the provision of the Directive with the definitions used by standards, with a particular regard to 
pre- and post- consumer waste and by-products materials. 
 
By insisting on the uptake of a market for plastic recyclates in the EU, PRE believes that 
residual (non-recycled) municipal waste generation will decrease consistently by 2030.

Finally, PRE calls on the EC to pursue a revision of the WFD that provides for a clear set of 
definitions for concepts often used in the EU legal framework on waste and plastics, such as 
“high-quality recycling”. 
 
 
 
PRE remains available to discuss the elements tackled in this position paper with the relevant 
policy offers at DG ENV Unit B.3. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
About Plastics Recyclers Europe 
Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE) is an organization representing the voice of the European plastics recyclers who reprocess 
plastic waste into high-quality material destined for production of new articles. Recyclers are important facilitators of the circularity 
of plastics and the transition towards the circular economy. Plastics recycling in Europe is a rapidly growing sector representing 
€3bn in turnover.

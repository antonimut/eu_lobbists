THE OBJECTIVE OF THIS DOCUMENT 
 
 
In occasion of the revision of the EU Waste Framework Directive  (WFD), the 
present document is intended to submit to the European Commission the case 
of R-PMIX-SRA, a recycled plastic product to be used in metal- and steel-making 
processes for the specific purposes foreseen by the technical standard  UNI 
10667-17:2021.  
 
While the European Green Deal sets ambitious decarbonisation objectives for 
2030 and 2050, the Circular Economy Action Plan aims at halving the amount 
of non-recycled municipal waste by 2030. I.Blu, a company of the Iren Group, 
has developed a recycled plastic product which can contribute on both fronts: 
increasing the material recovery of hard-to-recycle plastics, while significantly 
contributing to the decarbonisation and emission reduction of the  steel and 
metal industries, notoriously considered “hard-to-abate sectors”.  
 
One of the main objectives of the WFD revision initiative is to reduce the 
quantities of mixed waste destined to disposal and incineration and to increase 
recycling rates. To do this, the separate waste collection needs to improve in 
quality and quantity , to increase the percentage of waste sent to re cycle. 
However, this is not enough: the recyclability rate of the collected plastics also 
must improve. To date , about h alf of the plastic packaging streams resulting 
from selection and sorting processes are still classified as non -recyclable and 
must be disposed of through incineration or landfill. It is for example the case 
of mixed polyolefin-based plastics (e.g., multilayers), which is expected to grow 
alongside the collection rate.  
 
R-PMIX-SRA, also referred to as SRA or BLUAIR ®, is a plastic  Secondary Raw 
Material (EoW) deriving from the Mechanical Recycling of these plastic streams 
in compliance with the technical standard UNI 10667 -17:2021 and  used in 
metal- and steel -making processes for the specific purposes foreseen by the 
same. This recycled plastic  product can significantly contribute to the 
attainment of the European Recycling targets for plastic packaging waste, 
currently set for 50% by 2025 and 55% by 2030.  
 
The present doc ument provides an overview of the product and presents a 
proposal for the revision of the WFD foreseen in 2023, in order to unequivocally 
clarify the inclusion of this Secondary Raw Material  in the recycling rate 
calculation, in that it derives from a recycling operation and complies with the 
relevant End-of-Waste requirements, as well as with the European definition of 
“recycling”.

The SRA is a plastic end-product complying with its relevant technical standard, 
and as such an End -of-Waste, as defined in  article 184 -ter of the Italian  
Legislative Decree n. 152/2006 as amended by D.L. 116/2020 , which 
implements the Waste  Framework Directive with regard to the End -of-Waste 
criteria. The specific purposes of R-PMIX-SRA listed in the standard UNI 10667-
17 include its use as secondary  reducing agent, one of its main functions , in 
replacement of traditional fossil resources such as coal and anthracite . The 
employment as fuel and/or energy recovery is excluded.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The production of R-PMIX-SRA is authorised by the relevant public Authorities 
in I.Blu’s recycling plants, in accordance with current applicable legislations. In 
the permits it was established that: 
 
a) the production activity of the reducing agent SRA is classified as Recycling 
Operation (R3) in accordance with the current legislations; 
b) the SRA complying with the technical standard UNI 10667-17 is no longer 
classified as waste, according to article 184 -ter of the Italian Legislative 
Decree n. 152/2006 as amended by D.L. 116/2020, and can be used for its 
specific purposes. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The feedstock used for the SRA production is utterly complementary and 
compatible with the co nventional recycling system and does not compete for 
resources in any way. After a previous high -performing separation process of 
the most commercially valuable polymers (such as PET, PE and PP) destined to 
traditional recycling, the remaining streams are further cleaned and separated 
to become a suitable feedstock for the production of BLUAIR ®. These streams 
are mainly composed of polyolefin -based mixed plastic, such as multilayers 
packaging, which are otherwise non -recyclable and as such incinerated or 
landfilled. All sorting and recycling activities carried out by I.Blu are framed 
within the relevant EPR system (e.g. COREPLA), and material streams comply 
with the PRO requirements.

In order to achieve EoW compliance and being  considered a product, the SRA 
must meet the following criteria  set by both the permits’ provisions and the 
technical standard: 
 
1. process: a combination of operations and treatments in  the realm of 
mechanical recycling (e.g., shredding/grinding,  cleaning/washing, 
densification/extrusion, etc.). 
2. quality requirements: including both physical and chemical parameters. 
3. purposes defining the final applications and functions of the product. 
 
The above provisions and the technical requirements are  consistent with the 
consolidated definition of “recycling” as specified in European legislations. 
 
Apart from being  certified within the QEHS Management System , I.Blu has 
obtained the Second Life Plastic  and EuCertPlast recycling certifications. The  
latter is released in compliance with the European sta ndard EN15434:2007, 
which defines the necessary traceability procedures  for recycled plastic 
materials and establishes the basic rules for  the calculation of the recycled 
content of a product. The quality and EoW compliance of the product are subject 
to constant control through a structured monitoring plan.

CIRCULAR ECONOMY AND 
CARBON FOOTPRINT REDUCTION 
 
The SRA product represents an example of industrial symbiosis, since it allows 
to increase the recycling rate of the collected plastic quantities, while also 
cutting down the consumption of coal, which is traditionally used in metal and 
steel production  as a reducing/foaming agent . In doing so, it contributes 
significantly to the decarbonisation and GHG emission reduction of a hard-to-
abate sectors like the metal- and steel-making industries. 
 
BLUAIR® fulfils the pivotal principles of the Circular Economy: 
 
o Reducing waste 
at the end of the chain , where waste has been reduced, reused,  repaired, 
refurbished and recycled conventionally, there  are still streams that can be 
recovered, which would otherwise  be incinerated or landfilled. By recycling 
mixed polyolefin -based and multilayer plastics, I.Blu extends their life -cycle 
and maintains their value within the economy. 
 
o Replacing virgin raw materials 
the recycled product BLUAIR® plays its useful role in replacing traditional and 
polluting secondary reducing agents used in metal production, such as coal and 
its derivatives, with  a substitution rate of 1:1. This proves the high quality of 
this recycling operation, since “a high quality secondary raw material is one that 
can be used in subsequent manufacturing processes in place of high-quality virgin 
material.”1 BLUAIR®  also plays a particularly important role when considering 
the environmental impact assessment of recycling practices, which carry with 
them the environmental benefits that derive from substituting virgin raw 
materials. This is especially important in the case of R-PMIX-SRA, which 
efficiently substitutes critical fossil raw materials like coal and anthracite.  
 
o Cutting down CO2 emissions 
A major aim of the circular economy is to reduce the GHG  emissions deriving 
from the extraction and processing of raw  materials to produce goods. In the 
case of R -PMIX-SRA, cutting  down CO 2 is possible not only by avoiding the 
extraction, long -distance transportation and processing of coal, but also 
following the recycling process, when the carbon contained in the polymers  is 
recycled into steel , in replacement of the one traditionally provided by coal. 
Apart from supplying the recycled carbon  necessary for the steel -making 
process,  BLUAIR® also allows for a CO 2 cut down of about 30% compared to 
virgin coal.

LEGAL FRAMEWORK 
 
After being recycled in compliance with the permits and the technical standard, 
BLUAIR® officially becomes a Secondary Raw Material, used for the purposes 
foreseen by UNI 10667-17:2021. The SRA product represents the final product 
deriving from the recycling ope ration, in compliance with the End -of-Waste 
criteria defined in article 6 of Directive 2008/98/EC  (WFD), as amended and 
supplemented. Therefore, it should be  accountable for the attainment of the 
recycling targets, as specified in Article 11a: 
 
(c) the weight of the municipal waste recycled shall be calculated as the weight of waste 
which, having undergone all necessary checking, sorting and other preliminary operations 
to remove waste materials that are not targeted by the subsequent reprocessin g and to 
ensure high-quality recycling, enters the recycling operation whereby waste materials are 
actually reprocessed into products, materials or substances. 
 
This concept is further stressed by Article 6a of Directive 94/62/EC on Packaging 
and Packaging Waste, as amended by Directive (EU) 2018/852: 
 
b) the weight of packaging waste recycled shall be calculated as the  weight of packaging 
that has become waste which, having undergone all necessary checking, sorting and other 
preliminary operations to remove waste materials that are not targeted by the subsequent 
reprocessing and to ensure high-quality recycling, enters the recycling operation whereby 
waste materials are actually reprocessed into products, materials or substances. 
 
Apart from excluding certain applications such as energy  recovery and 
backfilling, which do not apply to the SRA product,  the consolidated European 
definition of recycling comprises  the reprocessing of waste materials into 
products, materials or substances whether they are intended for the original or 
for other purposes: 
 
‘recycling’ means any recovery operation by which waste materials are  reprocessed into 
products, materials or subs tances whether for the original or  other purposes. It includes 
the reprocessing of organic material but does not  include energy recovery and the 
reprocessing into materials that are to be used  as fuels or for backfilling operations 
[Directive 2008/98/EC]; 
 
Until 2018, according to article 6 of Directive 94/62/EC on Packaging  and 
Packaging Waste as amended in 2004, the first recycling  target for plastic 
packaging amounted at 22.5%, to be attained by 2008, and it was specified that 
the calculation would include “exclusively material that is recycled back into plastics”. 
Subsequently, this limitation was consistently removed by Directive 2018/852, 
which also  raised the target to 50% for 2025 and 55% for 2030. This 
modification opened the scope to new purposes and applications, by no longer 
limiting to the recycling of plastic packaging to the plastic-to-plastic concept, in 
accordance with the definition of recycling introduced in 2008.

WFD REVISION PROPOSAL 
 
In view of the upcoming Waste Framework Directive revision, and based on the 
arguments presented in this document, I.Blu and the Iren Group would like to 
propose two revisions to unequivocally confirm  the accountability in the 
recycling rate calculation of Secondary Raw Materials that are  recycled in 
accordance with national and/or European technical standards, provided these 
ensure compliance with the current definition of “recycling” and the End-of-
Waste criteria set by European and national legislations.  
 
More specifically, the suggested revisions are the following: 
 
 
Point 17, Article 3  
“Definitions” 
‘recycling’ means any recovery operation by which waste materials are 
reprocessed into products, materials or substances whether for the original or 
other purposes. It includes the reprocessing of organic material and recycling 
operations that comply with national and/or European technical standards that 
ensure end -of-waste conformity, but does not include energy recovery and the 
reprocessing into materials that are to be used as fuels or for backfilling 
operations [Directive 2008/98/EC]. 
 
 
 
Point 1, Article 11a  
“Rules on the calculation of the attainment of the targets” 
(c) the weight of the municipal wast e, including packaging waste, recycled shall 
be calculated as the weight of waste which, having undergone all necessary 
checking, sorting and other  preliminary operations to remove waste materials 
that are not targeted by the subsequent reprocessing and to ensure high -quality 
recycling (e.g. recycling operations compliant with national and/or European 
technical standards that ensure end-of-waste conformity), enters the recycling 
operation whereby waste materials  are actually reprocessed into products, 
materials or substances.

CONCLUSIONS 
 
The present document’s objec tive is to bring to the attention of the European 
Commission the case of the recycled product R -PMIX-SRA, and to present a 
proposal for the revision of the Waste Framework Directive that unequivocally 
confirms its inclusion in the recycling rate calculation. A major objective of the 
WFD revision is to increase  recycling rates, in accordance with the Circular 
Economy Action Plan, which aims to halve the amount of non -recycled 
municipal waste by 2030. T he recycled product BLUAIR® has a number of 
environmental benefits , in line with  the Recycling Targets set by the Waste 
Framework Directive  and the decarbonization objectives  established by the 
European Green Deal.  
 
One of the SRA’s major benefits is the opportunity to increase the recyclability 
of collected plastics by recovering hard-to-recycle streams and divert them 
from other undesirable disposal  practices, such as incineration and landfill.  
BLUAIR® therefore contributes to achieving European targets, according to 
which 55% of plastic packaging waste will have to be recycled by 2030. Other 
environmental benefits are linked to the final application of R-PMIX-SRA, which 
substitutes the virgin raw materials traditionally used as reducing and foaming 
agents in metal production, such as coal and its derivatives. BLUAIR® provides 
the recycled carbon  needed in the process , while contributing to the 
decarbonisation and CO2 emission reduction of the receiving industries, which 
are notoriously considered hard-to-abate sectors.  
 
In essence, the SRA product represents a r eal example of industrial symbiosis, 
which can both boost circularity and concretely tackle climate change.

IRU’s preferences
Specific DRT rules – occasional 
Ref. Ares(2022)1350994 - 22/02/2022
Proposed amendments
• Improved 12-day derogation
• The derogation fits into the core specificity of the EU coach tourism model developed over the years. This derogation should be 
applicable for national transport as well and making the compensation for reduced weekly rest more flexible, to fit the extre mely 
high seasonality of the coach tourism business. 
• Possibility to make use of a 16-hour daily duty time (instead of 15 hours currently) twice per week
• To facilitate late evening tours, opera and theatre visits, restaurant meals etc.
• Possibility to split the 45-minute break into maximum of 3 breaks of minimum 15 minutes each
• To fit the flexible tourists’ requirements, particularly, city sightseeing and places of interest visits
• Amending Article 12 to facilitate reaching hotel in case of exceptional circumstances
• To allow drivers to make use of this possibility not just for safety reasons (as is the rule currently) but also for reasons related to 
customers’ wellbeing (esp., after day tours returning to hotel in late evenings). 
• Taking the daily rest into three periods consisting of at least an uninterrupted period of 1 hour, an 
uninterrupted period of 2 hours, and an uninterrupted period of 9 hours
• To fit the extremely diverse pattern of tourism drivers’ work during a typical daily travel pattern, without jeopardising roa d safety
• Proposing a longer reference period of 4 or 6 weeks (or even longer to fit the tourist season), beyond the 
current 3-week reference period (i.e. similar to the current derogation for intl. freight transport for two 
consecutive reduced weekly rests)
• Especially adjust to high seasonality of tourism business, where drivers are in demand during tourist season.

Neste, together with a large and varied group of stakeholders, mapped out how a crediting 
system can work, how It can be woven into the existing policy framework and what its benefits 
are for OEMs, vehicle users, other affected stakeholders, society at large and the climate. All 
information is available here: https://www.crediting-system-for-renewable-fuels.eu/ 
- In a joint letter more than 135 scientists are supporting the fuel crediting 
- A study about the impact assessment of the CO2 emissions standards for passenger cars 
and vans showed lack of solid evidence for the critics of the fuel crediting system like 
increase of administrative burden, blur of responsibilities and decrease of the efficiency 
and effectiveness  
- In another study it was demonstrated that a fuel crediting system can avoid excess 
premiums and could save up to 215 million tons of CO2 emissions 
- A fuel crediting system leads to more solutions for the customer and deals as a risk 
management tool if electric or fuel cell mobility occurs challenges 
- The methodology of a fuel crediting system is well-developed and legally checked on behalf 
of the German federal ministry of energy and economics

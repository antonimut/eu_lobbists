Building sustainability considerations into product design is key to extending a product’s life cycle thereby minimizing 
its environmental and social impact together with the associated costs - from developing and manufacturing to use 
and disposal. We welc ome, therefore, the European Commission’s initiative on a new ‘Right to Repair’ which aims 
to encourage producers to design easily reparable goods that last longer  and enhance consumers’ rights to high -
quality repaired products. PMI supports policies that facilitate circular business models and sustainability by design, 
fostering the internal market in the EU, based on a more restorative economic model.  
PMI is a tobacco and nicotine product company. In 2016, it announced its new vision to deliver a smoke-free future 
by focusing its resources on developing, scientifically substantiating and responsibly commercializing smoke -free 
products that are less harmful than smoking, with the aim of completely replacing cigarettes as soon as possible. 
These products ar e based on different tobacco or liquid heating technologies that rely on electronic, battery -
operated devices, and are currently commercialized under the brand name IQOS, IQOS VEEV or VEEV.  
For PMI, contributing to the circular economy means developing pro ducts with potentially less impact on the 
environment by improving their recyclability, efficiency, and reparability. In that regard, in 2020, we established, a 
cross-functional global program, taking a holistic approach to address product returns and opti mize the lifetime of 
our devices. This effort focused on reducing unnecessary device replacement and limiting e -waste generation. This 
program was the first step in our journey to improve the lifetime of our products, primarily through optimization 
with th e outlook toward refurbishment and repair. Our smoke -free, heat -not-burn IQOS devices are subject to 
standard product warranties, generally for 12 months from the date of purchase or such other periods as required 
by law. In 2020, we also launched or expan ded several service-based solutions to minimize environmental impacts 
aimed at extending product lifetime through device optimization and maintenance.  
Furthermore, we trained our retail and call center teams on how to assess potential product issues faced  by the 
consumer to ensure a diagnosis is made before replacing a device, for instance by deep cleaning the device or 
ensuring the software is up to date directly at our brand retail store. We also launched a mobile application for IQOS 
users in certain ma rkets, which provides tips on how to use and clean the device as well as basic troubleshooting 
instructions.  
We strive to establish commercially viable repair -and-refurbish programs to offer a second life to our devices and 
cycle them back into the economy. With this in mind, in  2020, we worked with our reverse logistics partners to 
leverage and expand our existing CIRCLE ( Central Inspection and Recycling  for a Closed Loop Economy ) Hub 
infrastructure for the future possibility of repair and refurbishment and continue to expand these initiatives beyond 
2021. Committed to embedding sustainable design into our smoke-free electronic devices, which, as a relatively new 
product category, are not typically covered by existing eco-standards, we work to ensure these are designed per our 
sustainable design criteria and we seek to  develop and implement new category standards  as necessary to achieve 
our aspirational target to have all our new smoke-free electronic devices commercialized as of end 2025 eco-certified 
through identified relevant certification schemes . To that end, we are  currently pursuing improvements in areas 
such as durability, design for repair and r ecycling, and energy efficiency . Further information on our work on eco -
design and circularity of our products is available in our latest Integrated Report 2020. 
Overall, we believe consumers should be entitled to high -quality repair of their products provided that the 
functionality, performance and safety of the repaired product is preserved, consumer safety during  its post-repair 
use is not compromised, and data integrity, confidential proprietary knowledge and IP rights are protected and 
secured. In this respect, we trust the established manufacturer-associated repair networks are best placed to provide 
consumers with convenient access to repair/refurbish options and services. Further, these networks are the source 
of jobs and high -quality service in a circular economy. Most important ly, they are optimized for efficiency as they 
maintain product repair and production data and thus reduce resource and material consumption.

As the Commission works on the upcoming proposal, PMI stresses that the legislation should not dismiss this 
approach and factor in these benefits, and takes the opportunity to call for a balanced approach for all the on-going 
and upcoming EU initiatives; one that will allow for both ambitious goal-setting and practical implementation of the 
enforceable policy measures in order to achieve a real and even transformational positive impact on businesses, 
society and the environment in the transition towards a circular economy.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
About Philip Morris International 
Philip Morris International (PMI) is leading a  transformation in the tobacco industry to create a  smoke-free future 
and ultimately replace cigarettes  with smoke-free products to the benefit of adults  who would otherwise continue 
to smoke, society,  the company, and its shareholders. PMI is a leading  international tobacco company engaged in 
the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, as well as  smoke-free products, associated electronic devices  and 
accessories, and other nicotine-containing products in markets outside the U.S. In addition, PMI ships versions of its 
IQOS Platform 1 device and consumables to Altria Group, Inc. for sale under license in the U.S., where these products 
have received marketing authorizations from  the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)  under the premarket 
tobacco product application (PMTA) pathway; the FDA has also authorized the  marketing of a version of IQOS and 
its consumables as a Modified Risk Tobacco Product (MRTP),  finding that an exposure modification order for  these 
products is appropriate to promote the  public health. P MI is building a future on a new  category of smoke -free 
products that, while not  risk-free, are a much better choice than continuing  to smoke. Through multidisciplinary 
capabilities in product development, state-of-the-art facilities and scientific substantiation, PMI aims to ensure that 
its smoke-free products meet adult consumer preferences and rigorous regulatory requirements. PMI’s smoke-free 
product portfolio includes  heat-not-burn and nicotine -containing vapor products. As of December 31, 2020, IQOS 
was available for sale in 64 markets in key cities or  nationwide, and PMI estimates that approximately  12.7 million 
adults around the world have already switched to IQOS and stopped smoking.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
CALL FOR EVIDENCE FOR AN IMPACT ASSESSMENT 
Soil health – protecting, sustainably managing and restoring EU soils  
(Ref. Ares(2022)1132884) 
 
The Euro pean Fertilizers industry w elcomes and fully supports the EU ambition to pr otect, restore 
and promote a sus tainable use o f soils and to tac kle the issue of soil degradation.  Soils’ health and 
fertility represent crucial elements and ensuring no deterioration in soil fertility is vital in order to 
achieve the EU’s ambitious climate objectives while guaranteeing global food security  at the same 
time.  
Fertilizers play a vital role as they r epresent the basis for sustainable food supply, enabling 50% of 
food production. The sector is committed to work together with the other actors of the food su pply 
chain in order to advance a productive, resilient and sustainable use of soils. 
To this end, an enabling EU policy framework is needed.  
• The EU soil health law should be based on a harmonized soil health analysis me thod and on 
the development of standardized official Eu ropean indicators. The scientific assessment of 
current soil degradation, that the EU Commission is currently developing/using, needs to be 
thoroughly addressed and improved and one-size-fit-all approaches should be avoided. 
• The COVID-19 outbreak, followed by the recent geopolitical developments, have shown how 
food security is and must remain a top pr iority and has  highlighted the importance of a 
resilient farming and food industry in Europe. To achieve this objective, the production losses 
that could aris e from the setting of arbitrary  reduction targets have to be taken seriously . 
Such reduction would result in an increased import d ependency and higher food prices, as 
opposed to  the direction undertaken by the EU.  A right legislative framework based on 
science, coupled with a financial support and incentives for farmers will be the way forward 
to enhance more sustainable agricultural practices.   
• Healthy soils and sustainable farming practi ces have a big potential to contribut e to carbon 
sequestration and s torage. In order to unlock this potenti al, the use of precision a nd digital 
tools should be promoted to foster sustainable soil  management. The EU carbon farming 
initiative could contribute to enhance synergies between soil and  nutrient management. To 
maximise the use of nutrient resources available in t he EU is crucial ; to this end we support 
all initiatives that promote and encourage the nutrient use efficiency (NUE) and the use of 
nutrient efficiency indicators such as the NUE indicator developed by the EU Nitrogen Expert 
Panel. Knowledge gathering and demonstration projects  around soil f ertility and health  
should also be boosted.

About Fertilizers Europe  
Fertilizers Europe represents the interests of the majority of mineral fertilizer manufacturers i n the 
European Union. The association’s membership comprises 16 fertilizer manufacturers from countries 
across the Union and eight national fertilizer associations. The association communicates with a wide 
variety of stakeholders, institutions, European an d national policy -makers and members of the 
general public who seek information on fertilizer products and application technology, and topics 
relating to today’s agricultural, environmental and economic challenges.

 
 
 
Brussels, 21 March 2022 
EUMEPS feedback to the proposal for a Directive of the European Commission: 
Energy efficiency – Revision of the Energy Performance of Buildings Directive 
The association for European Manufacturers of Expanded Polystyrene (EUMEPS) is the voice 
of the Expanded Polystyrene (EPS) industry in Europe. Its members cover the entire EPS value 
chain from raw material suppliers and converters to recyclers, as well as supporting industries 
such as machinery and additive suppliers. They include individual companies as well as 22 
national associations. This ensures that EUMEPS represents both large suppliers and small- 
and medium-sized converters and recyclers (SMEs) – altogether more than 1,000 companies, 
which employ more than 80,000 people.  
 
EUMEPS as a representative of expanded polystyrene manufacturers welcomes the recast of 
the Energy Performance of Buildings Directive ( EPBD) opened on the 15 th of December. We 
are very happy to see that the proposal contains important goals aiming at creating climate 
friendly and energy efficient buildings in the EU. European Commission can count on our 
support to this directive proposal. 
EUMEPS has always support ed energy efficiency in buildings. Expanded Polystyrene (EPS) is 
in fact an excellent insulation material for constructions. Due to its lightweight, durable, and 
cost-effective properties, EPS is already helping  European buildings to achieve the 
environmental targets set by th e EU Green Deal. Indeed, buildings insulated with EPS prove 
to be uniquely efficient against greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions  helping companies and 
citizens saving money and resources. 
 
For EUMEPS the introduction of the renovation plans for buildings is a warmly welcome idea. 
This is an important step to turn Europe into a climate friendly continent. We find it as the 
main way to cut the emissions and support “Fit for 55” package. Our industry members are 
ready for the challenges and opportunities brought by this new proposal.  
Effectively, an important goal to reach zero-emission buildings is a proper insulation. This way 
the massive emissions coming from heating can be significantly reduced.  In this regard, i t is 
noticeable that the directive focuses on operational carbon as the main source of emissions, 
and only partially covers the whole-life cycle greenhouse gas emissions. In our opinion, the 
way how these emissions are described by the legislation does not provide as clear guidance 
as for operational carbon emissions. We understand that the legislation is just the first step 
towards the full introduction of whole-life cycle GHG emissions but at this moment this might 
result in discrepancies between the manufacturers, materials, and member states.

Moreover, the calculation method of the whole-life cycle GHG emissions only considers the 
gas emissions while there are also many other aspects covered by life cycle assessments 
which indicate a negative impact on the environment. These aspects are not considered in 
the proposal yet.  
We want to underline that the proposal should be related to energy efficiency of buildings, 
durability and long-term stability of the whole insulation system. We need to ensure that 
recommended insulation systems will be long-lasting (meaning for example, a constant 
thermal stability and mechanical properties in time, resistivity to moisture and water) and 
relevant to the EU Green Deal by addressing circular economy aspects. 
In regard to other parts of the EPBD, we oppose the inclusion of specific fire safety proposals 
into the EPBD. We note that there are other legislations focusing on fire safety  where these 
aspects are properly covered.  What is more, each member state has their own fire safety 
regulations and the best knowledge of how to address local exceptions and requirements in 
providing fire safety . The addition of  fire safety aspects to the EPBD might result in 
discrepancies with other legislations (not least at the member state level) and create 
confusions for industries. 
We hope that all aspects covered in EPBD will be reflected in renovation wave where energy 
efficiency will be core fundament as well.

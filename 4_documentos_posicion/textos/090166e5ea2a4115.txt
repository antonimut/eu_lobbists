Austria Österreichische Webanalyse (ÖWA) measures Austrian websites 
advertising since 1998 based on Internet traffic information. Each month, 
data about visits, page impressions and unique clients of each 
participating website are published. It is organised as an association with 
about 100 members from all sectors of the economy. The measurement 
also includes mobile media measurement.

Germany German Audit Bureau of Circulation (IVW), is one of the main Joint 
Industry Committees in Germany  that records and audits the distribution

INFOnline is a digital audience measurement  for the measurement of 
user data for digital sites and thus enable a reliable comparison of the 
digital market. It provides technology for many major digital Joint 
Industry Committees: AGOF, netmetrix, agma, mmc, OWA, AGF 
videoforschung and Wissen Was Zahlt.

Greece Nielsen Television Audience Measurement: Nielsen is the primary

Hungary Nielsen Audience Measurement is the only company in Hungary that is

• Right to amend the contract: In the case of continuous digital services, 
the entrepreneur can contractually grant himself a right to amend the 
contract for good cause, whereby the amendment and the reason must 
already be specifically stated in the clause. The consumer must not incur 
any additional costs as a result of the amendment. The consumer may 
terminate the contract free of charge within 30 days of the change or 
any subsequent information by the trader if his access to the digital 
service or its use is more than slightly impaired by the change and he is 
not offered a continuation of the status quo under the same conditions 
by the trader. 
• An update obligation is introduced for digital services and goods with 
digital elements (e.g. smartpho ne, smart TV, smartwatch): The trader 
must provide the updates necessary to prevent defects. This obligation 
can only be excluded under qualified conditions. 
The duration of the update obligation must be differentiated: 
-If the digital service is to be pro vided continuously according to the 
contract (e.g. access to a streaming portal), the period corresponds to 
the duration of the respective obligation to provide, but amounts to at 
least two years after handover for goods with digital elements. 
-If the digi tal service is to be provided individually (e.g. e -book with 
unlimited right of use), updates are owed for a reasonably expectable 
period of time.

Malta 
When a good develops a fault, the trader has the right to first choose to repair 
the product free of charge. The repair should be carried out within a reasonable 
period and without causing any significant inconvenience to the consumer. 
Replacement of defective or non-conforming goods can be chosen if the 
product cannot be repaired or if the repair would cause significant 
inconvenience to the consumer. When a product is replaced, the two-year legal 
guarantee does not start over again with the replaced product but continues 
from the original date of purchase. 
A part / full refund of the money paid for the product may be claimed when 
repair or replacement of the product are either not possible or if opted for may 
cause a significant inconvenience to consumers. 
Commercial warranty is not obligatory. It is given to the consumer voluntarily 
by the trader. This does not replace the legal warranty. Terms and conditions 
chosen by the trader. If the product is returned to the trader to be repaired or 
replaced as per the commercial guarantee’s terms and conditions, the guarantee 
is automatically extended by the period during which the guarantor had the 
goods in his possession while executing the repairs.

Spain 
We have a right to repair: 
When the product shows defects, the consumer can ask the remedy these 
defects (repair or replacement), the reduction of the price or the termination of 
the contract. 
In particular, regarding the repair or replacement, consumer can choose 
between them unless one of these two options proves impossible or, in 
comparison with the other corrective measure, involves disproportionate costs 
for the trader. 
Manufacturers are obliged to have parts to 
repair their products for 10 years after the 
product is no longer manufactured. They 
also have to provide a proper technical 
service during 10 years.

FUTURE EURO VII EMISSION LIMITS 
 
I. PRELIMINARY IRU OBSERVATIONS 
1. Postpone lower limits only after a complete assessment of the impacts of Euro 6 and Euro VI   
2. Perform a single regulation stage and not various steps as for Euro 6 and Euro VI  
3. Maintain separate regulations between LD and HD  
4. Implement a monitoring phase for limits on new pollutants and adoption of fuel and technology 
neutral metrics  
5. Replace limit on CO2 with limit on GHG (CO2, CH4 and any N2O) and remove separate limit on 
CH4  
6. Do not implement additional IUC checks on top of those in EURO 6 and Euro VI  
7. Ensure appropriate time is available for the development and implementation of the new EURO 
7 and Euro VII regulations.

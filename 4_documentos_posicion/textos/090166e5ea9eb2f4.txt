 
 
 
Answer to question 13: Indicate to what extent the following actions are 
important to improve the effects of emission limits; 
Introducing ‘geo -fencing’ that puts a vehicle automatically into zero 
emission mode depending on its geolocation 
 
CLEPA supports the sensible further development of the EURO 7/VII pollutant emission 
standards. As providers of sustainable technology solutions for road transport, we advocate 
for a regulatory framework that facilitates clean and affordable mobility. 
 
Based on robust scientific analysis, potential for further improvement of air quality should be 
determined and measures designed accordingly. We underline that latest generation vehicles’ 
impact on air quality has decreased  substantially and to the extent that non-tailpipe related 
emissions have risen in relative importance.  
 
CLEPA supports a technology and fuel neutral regulatory framework and underlines the need 
for in intact single market for mobility and transport: EURO standards ought to set out the 
rules for the type approval for vehicles which can be moved freely in the EU single market.  
 
We acknowledge that there are stakeholders, who wish to go further than that by regulating 
the use of vehicles depending on specific local circumstances, e.g. access limitation within cities 
depending on powertrain technology or EURO norm. Such access limitations disagree with the 
principle of free movement of vehicles in the EU single market.  If not avoidable, such  
limitations r equire sound scientific justification and cost/benefit ana lysis to ensure the 
regulation is proportionate and effective. 
 
We are  of the  understanding that geofencing technology regulations, switch ing between 
propulsion based upon geolocation , goes beyond the development of EURO 7/VII standards 
and will affect many  other regulations both from EU and UN ECE, We therefore suggest the 
development of geofencing regulation outside of Euro 7/VII regulation. 
 
We welcome further dialogue with the legislators and stakeholders on geofencing.

About CLEPA

associations and European sector associations. CLEPA is the voice of the EU automotive supplier industry linking the sector 
to policy makers.

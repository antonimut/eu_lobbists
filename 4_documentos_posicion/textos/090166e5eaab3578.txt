Feedback from Autoalan Keskusliitto Ry on Commissions plan regarding Sustainable 
consumption of goods – promoting repair and reuse 
 
 
Autoalan Keskusliitto welcomes and appreciate the Commissions plan regarding 
sustainable consumption of goods and promoting repair and reuse. 
 
We advocate policy option 3B to extend the legal guarantee period beyond the current 
minimum period of 2 years specified in the principles below.  
 
It is impossible for seller to improve the quality of the product. Manufacturer is the only 
party which is able to improve the quality of the product. Therefore, manufacturers 
should be required to produce more sustainable products and manufacturers must also 
be liable for the defective products. Nowadays vehicle manufacturers don´t have a 
interests to product sustainable products but only to minimize production costs at the 
expense of the sustainability of the product. 
 
Nowadays vehicle manufacturers and importers are not liable for the defective 
products that have sold to another consumer. So manufacturers and importers are not 
liable for the defective secondhand cars at all although the secondhand car might be 
only for example 3 years old. It is something that in no way encourages manufacturers 
to make sustainable products. Manufacturers and also importers must, along with the 
seller, be liable for the defective secondhand products in certain period after 
manufacturing the product. 
 
In durable goods such as vehicles, the product will only last if it is serviced in 
accordance with the maintenance and repair instructions provided by the product 
manufacturer in the country of destination. Manufacturers, importers or sellers can 
only be held liable for a product if it has been serviced in accordance with the 
manufacturer's maintenance instructions. 
 
Repairing, instead of replacing, must every time be the preferred way for 
seller/importer and manufacturer to remedy the defectives of product. The seller, 
importer or manufacturer must also every time enable to replace defective products 
with refurbished goods and not new ones.

About Autoalan Keskusliitto Ry 
 
The Finnish Central Organisation for Motor Trades and Repairs (AKL) is an association 
that provides services to, and represents the financial and labour-market 
interests of automobile, truck and machinery sales companies, repair, painting, anti-
rust and inspection companies. 
 
Over 95 percent of new car dealers in Finland are our members.

2. Hydrogen should be distinguished according to its carbon content based on its complete Life Cycle
How to maximize decarbonization?● A level playing ﬁeld in terms of methodology to calculate the CO2 footprint of low-carbon ANDrenewable hydrogen: the full LCAshouldbetakenintoaccount. Indeed, for exampletheCO2footprintfor imported hydrogen should take into account the carbon emitted during transport (and sameLCAmethod)● Co-production of hydrogen and carbon monoxide should be recognized● Having hydrogen produced through electrolysis based on low-carbon electricity recognized aslow-carbon
Thedevelopment of asoundcommondeﬁnitionandcertiﬁcationsystemforhydrogenisofparamountimportancein order to fully beneﬁt from the immediate decarbonization potential of clean hydrogen. In terms ofdeﬁnition/threshold, Air Liquide would support that low-carbon hydrogen is “hydrogen produced fromnon-renewable energy sources and meeting a 70% emission reduction criterion”. However, we believe that theproposed delegated act ‘specifying the methodology for assessing greenhouse gas emissions savings fromlowcarbonfuels’ needstobeadoptedearlier than2024. It isimportanttoensureclarityondeﬁnitionsandcertiﬁcationof low-carbon(andrenewable) hydrogenassoonaspossible.Itwill beimportantthatthis70%emissionsreductioncriterion will be based on workable and realistic comparators.
The Commission should take intoaccount thedecarbonizationpotential of all hydrogenproductiontechnologies.There should be a level playing ﬁeld in terms of methodology to calculate the CO2footprint betweenrenewablehydrogen, asdeﬁnedintheRenewableEnergyDirective(wheretheCO2footprintofrenewableelectricityproductionis zero) and low-carbon hydrogen. This is particularly important against the background of potential hydrogenimports. Thedeﬁnitionof thecarbonfootprint of hydrogenproductionshouldbesimilar, regardlessofthetypeofhydrogen, andshouldasaconsequencetakeintoaccount thecompleteLifeCycleAssessmentoftheproductionand, if applicable, transportation and conversion, until the point of entry in the network.

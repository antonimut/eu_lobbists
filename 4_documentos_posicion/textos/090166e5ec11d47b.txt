Summary of possible YUTA contribution for revue of PTD
YUTA as national association of travel agencies in one side, and - as involved subject in the
international tourism and traveling ecosystem (within administration of UNWTO and WTN) in a
purpose for realization of the fundamental aims of the YUTA organization and establishing the
resilient and sustainable solutions for the fragile tourism and traveling ecosystem after COVID -19
crises,  is  very  happy  to  give  a  contribution  to  all  efforts  of  the  European  Commission  for
establishing  all  elements  that  sustainable  solutions  which  can  be  resilient  on  the  future  crises
scenario can be placed in the legislation procedure.
YUTA, within the formed working group – MonTiN Safe, recognized the models and potential
solutions which can contribute to the strengthening of the everyday business cycles and possible
models for placing the central digital guarantee platform, linked with the paymennt process
and penalty obligation, and which platform is centrally monitored by the Standard setting
bodies in the space of the payment systems, like the World Bank, International Monetary
Fund  and  the  bank  of  international  settlement.  In  that  manner if  payment  for package
arrangement is centrally locked and used as a guarantee for the realization of the businesses
processes  of  everybody  in  the  business  chain,  and  where,  additionally,  with  the  penalty
segment  is  placed  intervention  for  preventing  that  established  rights  can  be  abused.
Therefore, economy effect on the complete ecosystem can be encouraged  – and this is reflection
of the three reasons:
1)  preventing abuse of business realization
2) preventing insolvency issue
3) unloading unnecessary bank guarantee( or insurancensurance policy ) for the business
realization for the subject who need guarantee for the business activity, and according
to that unload those subjects now can have better bank rating which can use for
improving their services
YUTA will be very glad that this proposed model presents to the European Commission and other
sectors or subjects who are willing to participate in this kind of public discussion. 
YUTA within the internal activities of the process for placing the resilient and sustainable everyday
business processes in tourism ecosystem already have established:
1) Interactions towards West Balkan government sector  in ministries which jurisdiction
cover structure of processes contained in MonTiN activities in EU Agenda as initiative that
West Balkan Balkan sector can be efficient synchronized and coordinated with EU and

The complete team from YUTA and MonTiN Safe environment is very glad and available in any
moment to give contribution for the processes which will result in creating stable and resilient
ecosystem, even on the future crises scenario, and in a manner that transport and tourism contribute
with 15 % EU GDP, it is very clear how all the procedure which the European Commission process
within the revision of the EU legislation is important and delicate.
We are looking forward to all future interactions.

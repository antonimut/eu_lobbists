*Ahead of the Christmas season, the gov. has adopted a two-tiered 
covid Green Pass system, which has come into force on December 6th 
and restricts the movements of those who are unvaccinated: 
- “basic” Green Pass: is a certificate proving that the holder has been 
vaccinated against covid or has recovered within the last six months or 
has been tested negative in the last 48 hours. From December 6th, basic 
green pass becomes mandatory for hotels, regional rail transport and 
local public transport network; 
- “super” Green Pass: it cannot be obtained via a negative covid test 
result. From December 6th, to access indoor restaurants, bars, cinemas, 
theatres, concerts, night clubs or sports stadiums, the super green pass 
is required. 
*Colour-coded system - Currently all of Italy is in the lowest-risk white 
zone with the exception of the northern Friuli Venezia Giulia and Alto 
Adige regions which have entered the low-moderate risk yellow zone. 
The Green Pass is not required for shopping centers however if the 
region passes into the orange zone then the Super Green Pass is required 
at weekends and public holidays 
*Unvaccinated workers can still access their workplace using the current 
system of getting tested every 48 hours. 
 
*As of 15 Dec., mandatory vaccination, already in force for healthcare 
workers, to all school staff, police and the military; nationwide booster 
campaign for all starting

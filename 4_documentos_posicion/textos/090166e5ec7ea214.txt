  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
ESMIG strongly supports including numerous contributors in public consultations on 
upcoming EU regulatory and legislative policies, acts, standards or similar documents.  
Additionally, ESMIG members support the implementation of regulatory security 
requirements (both horizontal and voluntary) which would increase general cybersecurity 
levels for digital products and lower risks of critical, major or cross -border security 
incidents and their impacts. 
 
ESMIG has shared its feedback on the “Call for evidence for an impact assessment  – Cyber 
Resilience Act . However, we  were not able to prepare a unified response for the Public 
Consultation on the cybersecurity of digital products and ancillary services.  
 
In particular, we were not able to provide unified answers on 25 “user” related qu estions, 
while we were able to provide unified answers for 2 “vendor” related questions . As per 
explanation, the term “users” encompasses both consumers using digital products as well 
as busines ses, public authorities and other types of organi sations deploying digital 
products. 
 
ESMIG is the European association of smart energy solution providers and during the 
analysis of the Public Consultation we have concluded that the  majority of questions are 
perceived as individual and therefore cannot be assessed at association level. Due to the 
varied cybersecurity maturity levels of members, varied cybersecurity risk appetites, 
cybersecurity approaches and perception of cybersecurity etc. members cannot provide 
relevant unified answers as an organisation. ESMIG could share their answers as either  a 
costumer (physical person) or as an individual business deploying digital products.

About ESMIG  
ESMIG is the European voice of the providers of smart energy solutions. Our members provide 
products, information technology and services for multi -commodity metering, display and 
management of energy consumption and production at consumer premises.  
Our activities are focused on systems for smart metering, consumer energy management and 
safe and secure data transfer.  
We work closely with EU policy makers and other EU associations to make Europe’s energy and 
water systems cleaner, reliable, more efficient and the European consumer informed, 
empowered and engaged.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY  
The Austrian Federal Economic Chamber (WKÖ) supports the Commission´s intention to carry out a revision 
of the current Directive on driving licences adopted in 2006, with the aim of improving road safety and 
facilitating free movement. In this context, we recommend various changes to the Directive.  
WKÖ looks forward to continuing the dialogue with the European Commission on how to  improve and update 
the existing rules. 
CURRENT SITUATION  
The Austrian Federal Economic Chamber (WKÖ) welcomes the initiative of the European Commission as we 
are of the opinion that the current rules need an update especially to consider new technologies and address 
the problem of shortage of drivers.  
RECOMMENDATIONS  
WKÖ calls upon the Commission to consider the recommendations of this position paper. 
Please find below the key WKÖ recommendations: 
MAIN PROPOSALS  
We call for: 
• the promotion of alternative drive systems 
• the implementation of the Austrian L 17 model for driving licenses category B to the truck 
sector 
• fostering e-mobility in driving schools 
• the possibility of carrying out control or appraisal trips with buses and coaches with a driving 
license category C 
IN DETAIL  
1. To meet the climate goals, it  is important to  promote alternative drive systems , namely by considering 
the higher own weights of gas, hydrogen, and electric vehicles. Currently - according to Art 6 par 4 lit c - the 
Member States can allow (after consultation of the Commission and only on its own territory) the operation 
of vehicles with a maximum authorized mass above 3 500 kg but not exceeding 4 250 kg for the transport of 
goods operating without a trailer for holders of a category B driving license. We think that this should be the 
rule among EU Member States, not the exemption and call for union-wide implementation and recognition of 
this possibility. 
 
2. To counter the shortage of drivers in the freight transport sector and - at the same time - foster the 
profession's attractiveness, Member States should have the option of lowering the minimum age  for driving 
licenses of category C to 17 years (extension of the" Austrian L17 model" to the C driving license): 
This model sh ould be available as part of the vocational training to become a truck driver. After the 
theoretical driving school training and practical driving lessons, the apprentice driver should gain practical 
experience in driving accompanied by an instructor. The driving instructors should be specially trained for

this task. After practice and completion of the practical driving test the driver is then allowed to drive trucks 
on his own from the age of 17. To implement this model nationally and unionwide, the Directive needs to be 
adapted. 
3. The current automatic transmission regulation (Code 78) puts the brakes on the use of alternative drive 
concepts in driver education. The easier it is to delete Code 78 from the driver’s license without exam, the 
more attractive it will be for driving schools to change their fleet. Furthermore, the number of vehicles with 
automatic transmission is increasing and e-mobility is key to meet our climate goals.  
To this end, we propose: After the completion of the driving test on a vehicle with automatic transmission, 
code 78 will be entered. After 4 driving lessons in a manual transmission vehicle, code 78 can be deleted.  At 
least 2.5 of the driving lessons  in a driving school vehicle should be held in public traf fic and one practice 
unit should be  held at the training area  (low-speed range). The required driving skills shall be taught 
exclusively while driving on the road. The driving instructor confirms the completion of the training units . 
There shall also exist a "small test" (0.5 units, skills test held by the driving instructor). The skills test is 
possible from the day after the official driving test (first granting of the driving license class B).  
 
4. Solving the problems of carrying out inspection or appraisal journeys with buses 
According to Directive 2006/126/EC, the category C driver's license can no longer be used to drive vehicles 
in categories D1 or D if - provided no passengers are transported - the journey is for inspection or assessment 
purposes to determine the technical condition of the vehicles or the journey is to move a bus or a coach out 
of a danger zone. In practice, this rule leads to the current situation where only a few companies can offer 
and perform recurr ing inspections for buses. Employees in motor vehicle workshops  do not usually have a 
driver's license for class D. This means that only a few workshops can offer and carry out those tasks. This 
results in an increase of inspection costs and long journeys for bus operators. 
Hence the  restoration of the legal situation before the Directive 2006/126/EC on driving licenses is 
paramount. 
 
5. Further remarks: 
- For class D/D1, the extension of the validity and lowering of the minimum age to 21 or 18 years with code 
95 is important for securing the next generation. 
- C1 vehicles are more like B vehicles than C vehicles, so the extension of validity to 10 years or 5 years from 
the age of 60 should be considered. 
- It will have to be considered that the different levels of automation require different knowledge and driving 
skills and that this aspect is currently not reflected in driving licenses (driving licenses according to 
automation levels, L3, L4).  
- The driver's license number is indicated on the driver cards for digital EU  control devices. Driver cards are 
valid for 5 years. If the card is lost or defective, a new card will be issued again for 5 years. Driving licenses 
for C, CE, D, DE are valid for 5 years and are to be renewed again for 5 years from the date of the medica l 
examination. From the age of 60, a new driver's license with a new driver's license number is issued every 2 
years. A new driver's card cannot be applied for. Therefore, it happens that the driver's license number on 
the driver's card and the driver's li cense do not match for a certain period of time ( which causes problems 
during controls). 
We therefore propose to integrate the driver card into the driver's license. 
 
4 
 
We welcome the increase in the maximum mass of Class B vehicles to 4.25 t. A change can be observed above 
all concerning technical conditions . Therefore, it is desirable to adapt the requirements to the changing 
conditions. Alternative drive technologies, such as electric mobility, cannot be implemented within the 3.5 
t limit from today's perspective.  
Motorhomes and minibuses also have a high unladen weight. Various additional equipment, passenger weight 
and luggage increase the weight. These vehicles are generally heavier due to electric motors, batteries, and 
wiring, which is why an increase in the maximum mass will also be necessary here.

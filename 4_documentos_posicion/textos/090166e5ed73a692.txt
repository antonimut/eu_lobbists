 
 
Position of the Fédération de la Haute Couture et de la Mode  
on the Proposal for Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation 
 
 
Founded in 1973, the Fédération de la Haute Couture et de la Mode (“the Federation”) 
represents over a hundred French and international brands and is the voice of the creative and 
high-end fashion industry. The Federation manages commissions aimed at raisi ng active 
awareness in relation to emerging issues in the legal, social, sustainable development and 
innovation fields. This is notably the case of the Sustainability & Innovation Commission , 
consisting of sustainability managers and policy advisors from the Federation’s members. 
 
Together with  this expert task force, the Federation and its members fully adhere to the 
European Commission’s ambition for a more circular economy  and the objectives set by the  
Sustainable Products Initiative.  
 
At national level, the Federation has taken an active part in the debates regarding the “Anti-
Waste and Circular Economy ”1 (Loi AGEC ) and “ Climate & Resilience ”2 (Loi Climat & 
Résilience) laws. These texts aim to push for a  change in production and consumption patterns 
to limit waste , preserve natural resources  and accelerate the ecological transition of French 
society and economy.  
The Federation also supports  industry  initiatives such as the Fashion Pact and UNFCCC 
Fashion Charter, which strive to tackle climate change, biodiversity loss, and negative 
environmental impacts more broadly. Through these initiatives, the Federation and its members 
are already active enablers in the transition to a more circular economy. 
 
The Federation would like to provide comments on the following aspects of the Proposal:  
 
- Ecodesign requirements – Article 5  
 
The Federation believes that, in line with circularity principles, enhancing durability is one of 
the key elements necessary to eco-designing high-end fashion products.  
 
High-end clothing and accessories are designed to be durable by nature.  They embrace core 
notions of quality and long product lifetime as they are made from high-quality and innovative 
materials with outstanding craftsmanship techniques and savoir -faire. High-end products can

The Federation members have always aimed at producing the most durable products, while 
respecting freedom of creation. One key aspect of eco -design is to encourage the use of low 
impact fibres. However, incorporating recycled content into creative fashion collections is  
challenging today as available recycled fibres are mostly below satisfactory levels of quality, 
durability, and recyclability.  At the current level of technological advances, implementing 
minimal requirements would be inadequate to the market of recycled fibres as it would only be 
applicable to plastics or synthetic fibres, while the  high-end sector favours the use of natural 
fibres in its creations.  
 
Another aspect of circularity models is to promote design for disassembly or recyclability. 
Efficient textile recycling relies on the implementation of collection, sorting and recycling 
systems at a local level – making its applicability complex for international brands.   All 
measures in this regard should therefore take into account local systems, product sp ecificities 
and technical challenges. Allowing sufficient time for research to develop recycling solutions 
for the variety and complexity of the materials used in many high-end products is essential. 
The Federation and its members are continually working to implement solutions in this regard. 
 
Finally, the Federation welcomes the initiative towards a toxic -free environment, including 
tackling the issue of microplastic pollution. Its members have committed to strong internal 
product safety practices by using safer and more sustainable chemicals, and increasing the 
protection of human health, from production to end of life.  
The Federation would therefore like to draw the Commission's attention on these factors and 
issues and believes that the Delegated Act implementing eco-design requirements as set out in 
Article 5 of the Proposal should take into account the specificities of each considered industry 
and product group so that each stakeholder can best implement eco-design objectives according 
to the specificities of its activities. 
 
- Performance requirements – Article 6 
 
At national level, the Federation is developing, within the framework of the experimentation 
for an environmental display of textile clothing products implemented at the request of the 
French Ministry of Ecological Transition, a method for evaluating the environmental 
performance of these products. One of the particularities of the experimentation conducted by 
the Federation is that it aims to evaluate not only the environmental performance of products 
but also their social performance, and therefore anticipating future requirements.  
 
At European level, the Federation is a voting member of the Apparel & Footwear PEF working 
group. 
 
As it is actively involved in both these experimentations aimed at providing consumers with 
reliable information on the performance of products, and since it recognizes the need for 
common sustainability standards, the Federation calls for the application at European level of 
performance evaluation methodologies combining both an environmental and social scope and 
aiming at taking into account the specificities of each considered industry and product group.

- Product passport – Article 8 
The Federation acknowledges the need for improved information regarding the environmental 
and, where appropriate, social aspects of products. 
 
The Federation therefore welcomes the digitalisation of such information, in the form of a 
digital product passport, as a practical solution which allows for more information to be 
communicated given certain space constraints on product labels and/ or packaging. Such a 
system would also enable stakeholders to adapt the information disclosed to the concrete needs 
of each type of consumer, depend ing on the sector and the type of products involved, and to 
their locations. 
 
The Federation would like to stress that, under article 13 of the French Loi AGEC, operators 
are already subject to the disclosure of numerous information regarding the environmental 
characteristics and qualities of the products placed on the French market, such as the 
incorporation of recycled material, recyclability, presence of substances of concern, 
traceability, presence of microplastics.  
 
In order to avoid any unne cessary and disproportionate administrative burden for economic 
operators or any distortion within the internal market, the Federation therefore calls for an 
harmonization of the information requirements to be implemented at national and European 
level. Besides, the Federation would like to stress that the information to be published should 
focus on  those that are relevant for the interested stakeholders, without leading to the disclosure 
of any sensitive data for companies.  
 
- Destruction of unsold goods – Article 20 
 
The Federation and its members  support the initiative of establishing measures banning the 
destruction of unsold goods, a measure that has already been implemented in the French Loi 
AGEC adopted in February 2020 and effective since January 2022.   
 
However, the Federation would like to draw the Commission’s attention on the information to 
be disclosed, considered under Article 20, paragraphs 1 et 5.  
 
The Federation believes that some information to be disclosed would not necessarily fulfil the 
important objective of prohibiting the destruction of unsold good set out by the Proposal, nor 
be relevant to the consumer. Instead of detailing inventory information, which could prove to 
be extremely complex and in certain cases subject to confidentiality or business secrecy, the  
Federation therefore suggests including a description of the end-of-life product management 
policy in the annual reports of the companies concerned. 
The Federation will follow the evolution of the Proposal and elaboration of the Delegated Acts 
with great interest and will remain available to provide any necessary contribution in this 
regard.

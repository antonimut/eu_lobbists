We would, however, like to draw the Commission's attention to the ESPR text's current wording,which, contrary to its objectives, threatens the viability of the second-hand market and that ofrefurbished products in particular :● The current text does not correctly define refurbishment.● The proposed rules do not differentiate a new product from a second-hand one that hasalready been manufactured.

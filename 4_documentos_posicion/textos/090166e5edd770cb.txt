1.4 Recyclable products
We would ask the European Commission to incorporate theconcept of sustainabilityduringtheseongoingdiscussionsaimedat devisingthedeﬁnition. A“singlematerial”productis, infact,sometimes considered to be “less sustainable” in terms of the elements outlined below. Asmanufacturers and retailers, we’re inviting public authorities to familiarise themselves with thischallenge of upgrading our eco-design strategies.
We would like the following deﬁnitions to be included in future delegated acts:
• Recycling: Total transformation of product waste into its raw material state,harnessing the material’s intrinsic properties. The recycled material can be used tomanufacture new products.• Downcycling: Converting used materials into materials with a lower value. We losethe properties of this material.• Design for Recycling: An eco-design strategy designed to look at the limitations ofrecycling from the design stage, in order to facilitate end-of-life product processing.• Incorporation of recycled materials: Eco-design strategy that helps to reduce aproduct’s environmental impact by using less virgin material. Choosing recycledmaterial to incorporate should be done in line with the material’s technicalspeciﬁcations. It is not always necessary or appropriate to conduct closed circuitrecycling (i.e. recycling a product just to make another identical one).

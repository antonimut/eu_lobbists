Sources of air pollution in Poland
Every fourth Pole who heats their home with coal is convinced that the main source of air pollution in Poland is the burning of 
solid fuels. This opinion is expressed significantly more often by people for whom the problem of air pollution in their plac e of 
residence is significant (36%), while it is indicated much less often by less educated people (11%).

Opinion on the environmental impact of coal quality
Over 60% of people heating their homes with coal are convinced that burning coal can be environmentally friendly/neutral if 
high quality coal is used. This opinion is particularly popular among people considering industry as the main source of air 
pollution in Poland (75%).

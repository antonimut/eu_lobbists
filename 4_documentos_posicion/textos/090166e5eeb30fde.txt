Subject: Revision of the Construction Products Regulation 
Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE), the association representing the plastics recycling industry, 
welcomes the proposal for a Regulation laying down harmonised conditions for the marketing 
of construction products (CPR), amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 and repealing 
Regulation (EU) 305/2011. 
PRE sees the revision of the CPR as an opportunity to improve the harmonisation of rules 
applicable to construction products in the EU while increasing their sustainability through 
specific requirements, in line with the Circular Economy Action Plan and the EU Green Deal. 
Please find below our comments, which focus on recyclability and the uptake of recycled 
content. 
1. Product requirements 
PRE supports the inclusion of recyclability and recycled content as part of the inherent product 
environmental requirements for construction products.1 Combined, these two elements are 
essential to increase the sustainability of construction products by promoting the uptake of 
recycled materials to be used in the same or similar applications, thereby decreasing the use 
of virgin plastics and associated GHG emissions. 
2. Harmonisation 
PRE welcomes the focus on harmonisation through industry standards and legislatively 
mandated delegated acts on the requirements applicable to construction products. It is highly 
relevant to ensure effective implementation of product and labelling requirements for the 
construction sector in the EU MS and enable a harmonised legislative framework to assess 
construction products’ environmental performance. 
3. Article 3 ‘Definitions’ 
PRE agrees with the inclusion of packaging within the scope of the definition of ‘construction 
product’ in paragraph 1, as it will allow for product requirements such as recyclability and the 
use of recycled content to also apply. It is however important that such requirements are in 
line with the Packaging and Packaging Waste Directive.

Further, in paragraph 24, the definition of ‘used product’ should be better defined to clarify its 
scope and its relation between the notions of ‘preparing for re-use’ and ‘recycling’. 
4. Article 22 ‘Additional environmental obligations to manufacturers’ 
The obligations listed in paragraphs (b), (c), and (h) are key to fostering product recyclability 
to make products easily recyclable through design for recycling and promotion of the use of 
recycled content in products through minimum targets. 
5. Annex I Part B  
Paragraph 2(a) states that product requirements should be specified where necessary 
through “the use of specific materials which can be specified also in terms of their chemical 
composition”.2 PRE stresses that given the comprehensive rules under the EU chemical 
legislative framework (e.g., REACH), requirements under the CPG should be aligned with the 
latter to avoid conflicting rules related to substances’ use in products, which would lead to 
increasing complexity and confusion for the recycling value chain actors, thereby undermining 
the harmonisation efforts of the new proposal. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
About Plastics Recyclers Europe 
Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE) is an organization representing the voice of the European plastics recyclers who reprocess 
plastic waste into high-quality material destined for the production of new articles. Recyclers are important facilitators of the 
circularity of plastics and the transition towards the circular economy.  
Plastics recycling in Europe is a rapidly growing sector representing over €7.7 billion in turnover, 9.6 million tonnes of in stalled 
recycling capacity, more than 650 recycling facilities and over 20.000 employees.

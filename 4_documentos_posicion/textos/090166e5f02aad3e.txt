Waste Framework Directive 
 
The Bioenergy Association of Finland supports the objective to improve waste 
management by reducing waste generation including through re-use of products and 
mixed waste. Energy recovery must be conducted at the highest efficiency from 
sorted side streams not suitable for reuse and recycling (solid recovered fuels) i.e. 
what cannot be reused or recycled, should be incinerated at high efficiency waste-to-
energy plants. 
 
A proper waste incineration is an essential solution of waste management for the 
environment and human health. It is also contributing to circular economy and can 
play a significant role also in future hybrid energy networks. It is important, though, to 
follow the waste hierarchy and aim at more sustainable incineration of waste. To 
improve the efficiency, the R1 definition should be more ambitious. Requirement for 
higher efficiency should be inserted. 
 
However, high efficiency energy recovery is not enough to ensure sustainability in 
waste incineration. Only sorted waste, deemed not suitable for reuse, preparation for 
reuse, or efficient material recycling, should be burnt. High efficiency energy recovery 
from appropriate waste streams that are not suitable for re-use, preparation for re-use 
or efficient material recycling should be considered as the preferred form of energy 
recovery in the waste hierarchy. Sustainable energy recovery from solid recovered 
fuels (SRF) must be recognized in the revision of the WFD.

Feedback regarding proposal for a regulation of the European Parliament and of the council on 
standards of quality and safety for substances of human origin intended for human application  
This position paper is made by Cryos International Sperm & Egg Bank.  
Cryos agrees on the identified shortcomings and inadequacies in the current European Tissues and Cells 
Directive and implementing Directives and welcomes update of the current legislation to allow for more 
flexible alignment to scientific and technological developments.  
Cryos appreciates that policy option 2 is chosen to solve the current challenges. This policy ensures the 
possibility of rapid updating of safety and quality requirements defined by European Expert Bodies. 
Cryos finds the submitted proposal for a Regulation positive and appreciates being heard and involved in 
the process. We take this opportunity to express our views on some identified shortcomings in the current 
proposal and to put forward recommendations for consideration in the further revision process. 
 
GENERAL REMARKS: 
Optimize access to SoHOs intended for human application  
The overall objective of the update of the legislation is to ensure a high level of health protection for EU 
citizens. This goal is not only achieved by enforcement of safety and quality requirements, but also by 
securing an optimized access to secure and safe SoHOs for everybody, especially within medically assisted 
reproduction. 
Cryos believes that much greater focus should be placed on the security of supply of SoHOs for medically 
assisted reproduction for clinical- or self-treatment, both in cases where the substance is imported from 
countries outside the EU, and in cases where the substance is exchanged between EU Member States. 
The Regulation does not prevent Member States from maintaining or introducing more stringent protective 
measures that are compatible with Union law. Cryos believes that this results in preventing all patients 
from actual access to safe treatment with SoHOs. This problem is already well known today, where 
Member States restrict some people from access to SoHOs and medically assisted reproduction only 
because of their sexual orientation, cohabitation etc.  
Cryos therefore believes that the proposal should define a right for everyone, regardless of gender, 
cohabitation, sexuality etc. having access to and treatment with SoHOs.  
 
Donor Registry 
The demand for medically assisted reproduction is increasing, and it is expected that the increase will 
continue in the future. Therefore, it is necessary to ensure that all patients who need medically assisted 
reproduction treatment have access to safe donor eggs and safe donor sperm/ SoHOs. 
Cryos suggests ensuring further protection of donor conceived children and women in fertility treatment by 
establishing a common Donor Register in EU. A common EU register for sperm and egg donors, wherein all

GENERAL PROVISIONS: 
Definitions 
Exchange of non-released SoHOs  
The Regulation needs to be clarified so that it clearly states that exchange of non-released SoHOs between 
entities in a SoHO organization is not distribution. The exchange might occur in the same member state or 
between two different member states.  
New definition of SoHO entity and SoHO establishment 
SoHO organizations with legal entities and activities in several member states is not covered by current 
definitions (“SoHO entity” or “SoHO establishment”), and it is therefore unclear how the regulation is to be 
implemented in practice, such as how exchange of SoHOs is ensured. A new definition of a SoHO 
organization covering larger organizations / legal entities with locations in several member states must be 
implemented.  
 
SUPPLY CONTINUITY: 
Supply alerts for all SoHOs, not only critical SoHOs 
SoHO supply alert should be send to competent authorities in case of a significant interruption, not only for 
critical SoHOs, but generally for all SoHOs within medically assisted reproduction. A supply alert should for 
example be send in the case that a member state introduces national regulations which prevents all 
patients from actually having access to safe treatment with SoHOs. 
 
GENERAL OBLIGATIONS ON SoHO ESTABLISHMENTS: 
Quality management systems and physician  
In larger organizations, quality management systems as well as other tasks will typically be centralized, 
which strengthens the quality and alignment of SoHO activities. Since it is directly mentioned in the

preamble that both SoHO entities and SoHO establishments can consist of several locations, it should be 
made clear that a centralization of resources is permitted, and that it is not the intention of the regulation 
that a SoHO organization with legal entities in several Member States are expected to operate independent 
quality management systems, and have doctors employed in all member states where SoHO activity is 
carried out. 
Physician 
Clarification is desired of the possibility of conducting cross-border SoHO activities within the same 
organization in several member states. It will lead to greater patient safety if the same doctor is responsible 
in the same organization regardless of national borders. 
 
SoHO DONOR PROTECTION:   
Standards concerning SoHO donor protection 
Consent 
It should be clearly specified that only national requirements regarding consent in the Member State, 
where the donor is donating, is applicable to the donation. Other Member States’ legislation regarding 
consent is not applicable. This will ensure that the donor does not need to fill in several consent forms, and 
it will eliminate the current challenge of which data must be disclosed.  
Further, it should not be possible to withdraw consent within medically assisted reproduction after SoHOs 
have been donated and processed.   
Compensation 
It should be clearly specified that only national requirements regarding voluntary and unpaid donation in 
the Member State where the donor is donating is applicable, also if SoHOs are used for medically assisted 
treatment in another Member State.  
Parental rights and obligations   
It should be ensured that a gamete donor, who donates to a SoHO entity/SoHO establishment/ SoHO 
organization can never be granted parental rights or obligations no matter the circumstances of the 
treatment performed, unless otherwise agreed at time of donation. Transparency related to parental rights 
would ease donor recruitment and protect donors.  
 
SOHO RECIPIENT AND OFFSPRING PROTECTION: 
Standards concerning SoHO recipient and offspring protection 
There will always be a risk of transmission of genetically determined carrier status (since everyone is a 
carrier), so the wording should be changed to reflect that it is only a risk of transmission of genetically 
determined disease which needs to be limited within medically assisted reproduction. If such risk is 
identified, it should be optional to match donors and recipients.

EU SoHO PLATFORM and PROCEDURAL PROVISIONS: 
Cryos adopts a positive attitude towards a common European platform and believe that this will contribute 
to increase the safety of all patients. 
However, Cryos believes that there is a need to clarify which specific information the SoHO platform must 
retain, and to which purpose. Cryos does not agree that the SoHO Platform should retain personal data 
about donors or recipients but believe that such personal data such only be retained in the SoHO entity/ 
SoHO establishment, who collected the data.  
Neither is it necessary for competent authorities to process personal data or data concerning health about 
donors, or to have the possibility to exchange information on a confidential basis between each other or 
the Commission.

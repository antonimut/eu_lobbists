Subject: Reform of the Union customs legislation 
Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE), the association representing the plastics recycling industry, 
welcomes the reform of the Union customs legislation as an opportunity to bridge several EU 
policies while ensuring a playing level field for all products being placed on the Single Market. 
Our association considers the objective of “improved protection of consumers against non-
compliant/harmful goods through better risk management”, as a key deliverable of the reform, 
aiming, among others, at substantiating green claims for imported products in terms of 
transparency and due diligence. Because of upcoming legal requirements on products, like 
recycled content, in the Single-Use Plastics Directive (SUPD),1 the Packaging and Packaging 
Waste Directive (PPWD),2 the End-of-Life Vehicles Directive (ELVD)3 or the Ecodesign for 
Sustainable Product Regulation (ESPR),4 among others, customs must put in place efficient 
checks and inspections through the digitalization of the procedures. Using the instruments 
discussed in the mentioned legislations, like the Digital Product Passport of the ESPR, jointly 
with the EU Single Window Environment for Customs, Union Customs legislation could enact 
measures for custom borders to check compliance with EU law, at the benefit of the Member 
States designated competent authorities.5 These measures could include the existence of 
certifications to substantiate the requirements of the ESPR (like recycled content). 
Centralizing the system at the EU level would mean harmonizing the procedures at customs 
for all products being imported, hence supporting the achievement of another objective of the 
reform supported by PRE, “improved protection of the EU economy, jobs and innovation 
against unfair competition, including social and environmental dumping”.  
This is particularly relevant when it comes to the EU Circular Economy, and the steps 
undertaken by the Member States and the industry to invest in sustainable, circular, 
businesses. When it comes to plastics, it is essential to protect the EU market against unfair 
practices and unverifiable claims on recycled content products and components. EU audited 
certification systems could support the custom checks, providing an instrument against which 
to measure the claims. 
PRE remains at disposal of the policy officer in charge, to further discuss the few points raised 
in our feedback. 
 
About Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE) 
Plastics Recyclers Europe is an organization representing the voice of the European plastics recyclers who 
reprocess plastic waste into high quality material destined for production of new articles. Recyclers are important 
facilitators of the circularity of plastics and the transition towards the circular economy. Plastics recycling in Europe 
is a rapidly growing sector representing over €7.7 billion in turnover, 9.6 million tonnes of installed recycling 
capacity, more than 650 recycling facilities, and over 20.000 employees.

YUTA – MonTiN Safe contributions to the Agenda of the traveling digitalization
and the aditional linked EU agendas
The  national  association  of  travel  agencies  in  Serbia  -  YUTA  (within  the  MonTiN  Safe
administration,  in  which  is  active  and  where  MonTiN  Safe  administration  establishing  the
additional linkage of YUTA on the EU level and open channel for interactions of West Balkan -
sector where YUTA is renowned recognized) has placed, as the one of the base goals and remit - the
Implementation  of  the  digitalization  as  the  stable  solution  for  the  improving  resilient  and
sustainable impact in the everyday business cycles in the tourism ecosystem.
In that manner, YUTA and MonTiN Safe working group have recognized that the implementation of
the digitalization of the traveling document is the one of the crucial points towards placing the
fundamental tool for the efficient realization of the successful digitalization of the complete tourism
ecosystem.
Beside the obvious a great direct impact, which will be established with the placing of the digitized
travel document, our administration, within this document, would like to give a lights to very
important effect which can be triggered, if the traveling digitalization achieve well correlation with
the deployment of a few parallel and vital processes of the tourism ecosystem - where all of that is
in the fundamental link with the technology on which will be build the digitalization of the travel
document.
1. Covid - 19 crises explicitly has shown the fragility of the business processes in many
sectors. The one of the most sensitive, certainly, has been the tourism ecosystem (not just
because it is composed  from travelling,  hosting, selling, marketing  and organization &
logistic processes, but also each of those segments need to be settled during the complete
payment process with the coherence of maintaining the arranged level of realization and
preserving the customer rights). Placing the background technology, which will link the each
activity  of  the  single  tourist  -  within  his  obligatory  administration  with  the  tourism
ecosystem, regulation processes and businesses interactions, which consider the payment
processes, and if the same technology participate in the logistics of the payment procedures
(for the validation or validation of excluding the suspicious activities), where the global
database can link the single tourist activity with the business subjects activities with placing
the physical trace of the payment processes in the linked chains, this can be a great tool for
the reducing fragility in the not so resilient business processes in the tourism ecosystem,
which was evidently shown during COVID - 19 crises. The proposal of achieving of the
important  progress  may  be  within  the  current  agenda  of  the  European  Central  Bank,
(implementation of the digital euro), with the approach for integration of interoperable
technologies in the background of digitalization of traveling and digital euro - what can
result of involving the digital euro in the massive every day usage, but with parallel effect of
improving the resilience of the tourism ecosystem.

2. The  importance  for  integration  of  the  unique  and  general  database  of  traveling  in  the
achieving efficient tranzition at the border crossing was explicitly shown during the COVID
-19 crises where the information about vaccination was obligatory element for entering in
the new country or the certain territory
3.  Digitalization  of  the  traveling  -  efficiently  can  make  the  progress  in  the  faster
implementation of the multomodal travelling (especially if the background technology is
interoperable with the payment services), therefore - the current position of the EU for
placing the technology, which can be set up as the the tool for the international general
coordination, indeed, can help for involving euro (digital euro) , but much more important -
harmonized  European  approach  into  the  regions  which  are  not  the  EU  members
4. Within this document, YUTA - MonTiN Safe administration would like to note the great
importance for the involvement of digitization of the vehicle data, as well as the person's id/
passport, in the complete process of the digitization of traveling. The importance of this has
a much bigger impact, then just the obvious implications within the global securities sector.
The integration of the global database, which can collect single tourist emission fingerprints
and vehicle emission information (according to newest vehicle technical review data) and
which  can  be  set  up  with  the  background  technology  which  establishing  the  traveling
digitization, it can set up the current carbon emission price. Additionally, monitoring of the
factor  of  reducing  the  emission  in  the  time  frame  of  the  single  subject  (within  the
digitization of traveling, it can be set up - the vehicle data information need to be shared),
and, in a finial point, it can be measurable if the some subject has achieved reducing of the
pollution.  According  to  this  stable  measurable  axes,  it  is  possible  to  place  emission
taxonomy, but also - what is very important, to have a trace if the green investment achieved
the predicted and, with the contract obligation, involved goals. (YUTA, thanks to MonTiN
Safe administration, the mention approach and all consultation about it, had opportunity to
exchange during the ongoing 9th OECD Forum on Green Finance and Investment )
Coorelating  with  all  aforementioned,  and  the  whole  linked  administration  of  the  European
Commission in the parallel agendas, which are coorelating whith the Digitalization of the traveling,
YUTA - MonTiN Safe administration are very welcome to give the contribution towards the capital
integration of the foundamental basis for placing the resiliant axes and tools for the sustainable and
the harmonized implementation of the businisess and administrative processes of the future.

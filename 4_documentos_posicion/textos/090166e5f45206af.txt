EU Critical Raw Materials Act 
 
1 
 
Introduction:  
As Aurubis AG, the leading copper producer and one of the world's top copper recycler, we welcome 
European Commission’s intention to present a new initiative on Critical Raw Materials. The climate-neutral 
economy, the expansion of renewable energies, electromobil ity and digitization require large quantities of 
copper and other metals.  
 
In the immediate future it will be of utmost importance to secure primary raw materials both domestically 
and from global markets. The data shows that this is necessary until meani ngful volumes of recycled 
material become available from end -of-life clean energy technologies. However, similar focus should be 
already put on expanding the EU’s recycling industry into a leadership position. A strong legislative effort is 
needed in order to drive European investment and secure responsible global supply.  
 
This new initiative should focus on a few key points such as: 
 
- Codifying “strategic” raw materials  which expands beyond the scope of what is currently 
considered a critical raw material. The current CRM list is limited and does not cover some of the 
important materials needed for the EU’s twin transition, such as copper. 
- Ensuring a global level playing field is key. There is a need to ensure the competitiveness of EU 
industry via competitive energy price and overall enabling policy framework. 
- Guaranteeing a wider harmonized EU policy action, to make sure that there are no contradictory 
provisions that could jeopardize EU’s goals. There is a need for coherent due diligence, chemicals 
and environmental legislation. 
- Fast-tracking of permitting procedures for identified projects of strategic importance in the different 
steps of the supply chain. 
- Improving th e EU legislative framework on waste  to facilitate a wide collection of end -of-life 
products and complex waste streams with increased efficiency in recovery of the strategic raw 
materials 
- Establishing new opportunities to finance projects on strategic raw materials. 
o Strategic raw material value -chains, including mining, refining and recycling, should be 
eligible in the context of sustainable finance, by development of ambitious but operational 
criteria for sustainable contribution to the environmental objectives of the EU Taxonomy. 
Industry alignment 
 
We are fully aligned within our sector with other non -ferrous metals on the approach going forward. 
Eurometaux, the association of EU non-ferrous metal producers has produced several position papers that 
were shared with the Commission, expanding on the main asks and providing more detail. 
 
Those papers focus on:  
 
• Overall framework with the immediate need to codify strategic raw materials within an overall 
coherent approach 
• Setting a 2030 headline goal for the right strategic autonomy 
• New legislation that should prioritize projects for faster permitting and finance while adhering 
to the high ESG standards established in the EU 
o identification of priority projects for improving security of supply of European industry  
o establishing new rules for faster permitting and easier finance 
o establishing measures for competitive operational expenditure and level-playing field  
• Expanding and strengthening EU raw materials partnerships 
• Combatting unfair trade practices

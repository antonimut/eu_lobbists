High ambition of revised Packaging Waste Regulation and its targets must be 
preserved 
 
This is our feedback on a public consultation about the proposed regulation on 
Packaging and Packaging Waste (COM(2022)677) presented by the European 
Commission on 30.11.2022.  
 
The European Sustainable Business Federation - Ecopreneur.eu - welcomes the 
proposed regulation on Packaging and Packaging Waste as presented by Vice 
President Frans Timmermans on November 30. We particularly appreciate the 
provision of specific mandatory targets for waste reduction, recycled content, 
reuse and refill within the suggested timeline for implementation as well as the 
introduction of mandatory deposit-return-schemes and a grading system to 
define the level of recyclability performance, based on eco-design, to be used for 
eco-modulated EPR schemes. 
 
We also consent to only counting post-consumer recycled content towards 
recycled content targets rather than post-industrial recycled packaging material.  
 
Finally, we welcome the obligation for Member States to designate competent 
authorities responsible for implementation and enforcement of the regulation 
with its targets and timeline.  
 
For consumers, the new regulation will make reusable packaging options more 
common, reduce overpackaging, and provide clear labels for correct recycling. 
For the industry, it will create new business opportunities and employment, 
especially for smaller companies, decrease the need for virgin materials, boosting 
Europe's recycling capacity as well as making Europe less dependent on primary 
resources and external suppliers. As a result, it will put the packaging sector on 
track for climate neutrality by 2050. 
 
Considering the extraordinary stakes, impact and risks from packaging waste for 
both humans and the environment, we strongly advise all legislators involved in 
the final legislation process to preserve the level of ambition of the proposed 
PPW regulation and its targets and withstand attempts to watering them down 
any further.  
 
Corresponding to the principles of the EU Waste Hierarchy, the regulation must 
privilege consistent reduction and reuse targets over recycling and composting. 
Lower reuse targets, particularly for 2030, could make reuse systems too hard to 
establish.  
 
We call on the Commission to ensure through a delegated act the phasing out 
hazardous materials and additives that compromise the quality of recycling and 
further reuse.  
 
That is why it is crucial that all companies from inside and outside the EU that put 
packaging products onto the common market need to comply to the Packaging 
and Packaging Waste Regulation and its defined targets, - including e-
commerce. Otherwise, they will need to accept elevated costs from higher 
recycling fees and environmental taxes, for example.

Without regulating the top tiers of the waste hierarchy in the manner outlined in 
the Proposal, the goals of the Circular Economy Action Plan and the Green Deal 
might get compromised. To meet the goals, we need a regulatory framework that 
facilitates and rewards investment and innovation in circular packaging solutions 
and circular business models in general.  
 
Recycling should only apply to of packaging formats that reach the end-of-life 
stage, with mechanical recycling being the primary option and chemical recycling 
being the very last. To increase the collection, sorting and quality of mechanically 
recyclable packaging waste, we call for regulatory instruments that encourage 
ecodesign and the shift from plastics to bio-based materials, especially eco-
modulated EPR schemes and financial incentives.  
 
About Ecopreneur.eu 
Ecopreneur.eu is the European Sustainable Business Federation of currently 
seven national associations representing about 3000 sustainable companies - 
mostly SMEs. A member of the Coordination Group of the European Circular 
Economy Stakeholder Platform, Ecopreneur.eu is the only cross-sectoral EU 
business organisation committed to ambitious measures, rules and regulations 
for a low-carbon circular economy. We advocate a new economic framework in 
which sustainability is promoted, the environment respected, and ecological 
principles are followed. Ecopreneur.eu and our members bring concrete 
experience from pioneering companies into the political debate, show best 
practice examples and represent the needs of green SMEs in a credible way.

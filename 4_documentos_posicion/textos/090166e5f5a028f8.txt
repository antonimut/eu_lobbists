A one-stop shop provision should beforeseenfor theconformityassessment process. Obligations of the manufacturer vs. obligations of the user
TheCRAsets various obligations for providers of ICThardware, software, services andprocesses. However, inacontextof global supplychains, theCRAshouldalsoaccount of thecomplexmulti-vendor andhybridnetworkenvironments inwhichtheseproducts operate, andof theresponsibilityoftheusersinproperlyintegrating,operatingandmanagingthespecific ICT product. Elements like proper compliance with the manufacturer’s guidelines on software deployment,patching, and utilization should be also accounted for as to exonerate the vendor from direct liability in case ofnon-compliance with its product’s terms of use. The CRA proposal should also foreseerequirements that account foraggregator- andchannel partner contracts wheretheinitialvendorwouldhaveneithervisibilitynorcontroloverhowitsproduct has been integrated and used in a wider ICT procurement contract or service provision.

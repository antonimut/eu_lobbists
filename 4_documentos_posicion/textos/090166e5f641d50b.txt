 
 
CORPME contributions  
TO THE PUBLIC CONSULTATION ON THE PROPOSAL FOR A 
REGULATION 2022/0358 (COD) ON THE COLLECTION AND EXCHANGE OF 
DATA ON SHORT-TERM ACCOMMODATION RENTAL SERVICES  
INTRODUCTION  
The Spanish Association of Registrars congratulates the European Commission for its 
initiative in this area, which responds to a social reality that has not been add ressed with 
a comprehensive and harmonising approach for all member states.  
The Colegio de Registradores de la Propiedad y Mercantiles de España is a public law 
corporation protected by law and recognised by the State, with its own legal personality 
and full capacity to act, which is governed by mortgage legislation, by the legislation on 
Professional Associations and by its General Statutes. 
Land registrars are civil servants for all legal purposes (Article 274.2 of the Mortgage 
Act) and are obliged to be members of the College of Registrars.  
The purpose of the land register is the registration of ownership and other rights in rem 
over real estate, with the aim of ensuring publicity and protection o f legal situations 
relating to real estate. The register provides legal security for real estate and economic 
transactions by guaranteeing the accuracy of the data entered in it. The Register is public 
for those who have a legitimate interest in knowing the status of a specific property, which 
must be assessed by the land registrar. 
Public registers are usually classified into two main groups, or categories, known as legal 
registers and administrative registers. The former (legal registers) are those capabl e of 
creating legal presumptions, either iuris tantum (they admit proof against) or iuris et de 
iure (indestructible presumptions). The latter (administrative registers) have only 
informative, cognitive or transparency functions. 
The land registry is a legal registry and is not an exclusively Spanish institution, but is 
found in almost all EU member states . Proof of this is its union through the European 
association of land registries, integrated in the e -justice platform https://e-
justice.europa.eu/108/ES/land_registers__eu_level. 
The importance of registers is not unknown to the European Commission. This is clear 
from its report of 9 March 2002, CO M(2022) 87 final, addressed to the Parliament and 
the Council assessing the necessity and proportionality of harmonising the information 
contained in land registers and the need to interconnect these registers.  
HIGHLIGHTS OF THE PROPOSAL  
From the perspec tive of the College of Registrars, two recitals and one article of the 
proposal should be highlighted: 
Ref. Ares(2023)226316 - 12/01/2023
 
 
Recital 9 of the proposal states that: "Registration procedures allow competent 
authorities to collect information on hosts and units in relation to short -term 
accommodation rental services. The registration number, which is a unique identifier of 
a rented unit, should be able to ensure that the data collected and exchanged by the 
platforms are properly attributed to hosts and units. It is therefore up to competent 
authorities, when wishing to receive data from online short-term rental platform 
providers, to establish or maintain registration procedures for hosts and their units, 
whether at national, regional or local level". 
Recital 25 of t he proposal states that : "Harmonisation between the different registers 
within a Member State, as well as their interoperability with the digital points of single 
contact should be ensured in order to remove semantic and technical barriers to the 
exchange of data and to ensure m ore effective and efficient administrative procedures. 
The entities in charge of setting up the digital points of single contact at national level 
and the Commission should facilitate implementation at national level and cooperation 
between Member States. 
Article 4(1) of the proposal states that : "Any registration procedure established by a 
Member State, whether at national, regional or local level, for units located on its territory 
shall comply with the provisions of this Chapter". 
EVALUATION 
From the perspective of the legal registers, CORPME makes a positive assessment of the 
proposal in the above -mentioned rules. However, it appreciates that the final 
subparagraph of recital 9 can be qualified as incomplete. 
This subparagraph refers to registration proc edures and emphasises the following 
circumstance: "whether at national, regional or local level". 
The nuance described above is positive because, in effect, it makes it possible to respect 
the territorial and jurisdictional diversity that exists in some member states. However, it 
omits other criteria to be taken into account: 
• Newly created or already existing; taking into account how each country 
is complying with this new social reality, but without omitting the already 
existing institutions. 
 
• Of a legal or administrative nature; taking into account the different nature 
they may have in the member states. 
 
 In this case, it is considered most appropriate to assign this function to a legal 
register, the Land Registry, in the configuration that corresponds to each state, as 
in addition to fulfilling the objectives of the regulation, it will be easier for citizens 
to verify the existence of these short-term accommodations by checking the general 
state of encumbrances of the properties. 
 
 
 
PROPOSAL 
In order to complete the content of the proposed regulation and improve its 
precision, the following wording is proposed for recital 9: 
"Registration procedures allow competent authorities to collect information on hosts and 
units in relation to short-term accommodation rental services. The registration number, 
which is a unique identifier of a rented unit, should be able to ensure that the data 
collected and exchanged by the platforms are properly attributed to hosts and units. It is 
therefore up to compet ent authorities, when wishing to receive data from online short -
term rental platform providers, to establish or maintain registration procedures for hosts 
and their units in land registers, whether at national, regional or local level.

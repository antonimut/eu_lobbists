ScopeFurther clarity is needed in terms of what non-embedded software is excluded from the scope ofthe CRA. In particular, it is unclear how the exclusion of software services (including SaaS)appears to conflict with the Act’s language on ”remote data processing” in the definition of a“product with digital elements'' under Article 3(1). More specifically, we are concerned that theinclusion of remote data processing has the potential of indirectly bringing a significant numberof software services back in scope of the legislation. In addition, there is a clear regulatoryboundary between the security of devices and the networks and systems over which theyoperate, which should be marked out between the CRA and the NIS2 Directive.
We recommend that the text clearly specify that the CRA does not apply to cloud-basedservices – IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS – or any other remote processing service other than “remotedata processing solutions that are related to a product with digital elements.”

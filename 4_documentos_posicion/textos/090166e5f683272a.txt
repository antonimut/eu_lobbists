Thank you for the opportunity to comment.  
MED-EL Elektromedizinische Geraete Gesellschaft m.b.H. is an Austrian manufacturer of high-risk 
devices (implantable and non -implantable solutions for hearing loss) MDR certified for Class III, 
IIa, Ir and implantable CMD devices in March 2020. Even as an early MDR adopter, we  are still 
completing transition of legacy devices to MDR with confirmed submissions / audits for legacy 
devices throughout 2023.  
This proposal provides us and notified bodies breathing space to complete this ongoing work 
allowing for a  work-around provision for extension of existing certifi cates beyond May 2024 if 
the certification by the notified body is not completed on time.  
We provide the below points for consideration as comment on the proposal itself and issues 
arising from the proposal.

Extending the legacy certificate validity extends also extends the “no change” limbo for legacy 
devices where supply chain issues increasingly trigger expected changes to that device.  
- The longer period of validity for AIMD / MDD cert ificates mean a longer period where no 
significant changes in design can be made to those devices in the EU.  
- Due to increased disruptions within the supply chain and the extended timeline of legacy 
devices, more changes resulting from supply chain issues are expected.  
- We recomm end that these changes be addressed in an update of the MDCG 2020 -3 
guidance on significant changes to avoid further  disruptions of medical device supply in 
the EU.  
Challenge of explaining the new transitional provisions to authorities after May 2024 due to lack 
of official certificate stating a validity past May 2024 
- It would be preferred that the existing certificate was upd ated by the notified body to 
the relevant deadline date: 31 December 2027 for Class III / 31 December 2028 for other 
certificates.  
- The notified could a utomatically trigger this update / alternatively issue a new type of 
certificate for all certificates whe n a MDR application has bee n received and all other  
conditions are fulfilled as per the proposed amendment.  
- It should be noted that missing the May 202 4 deadline for issuing of the certificate is 
reliant on the notified body ’s capacity and not the manuf acturer therefore it is 
reasonable to presume that the remedy should come from the notified body.  
- In addition , EU competent authorities should all have the same process in p lace for 
issuing Free Sales Certifi cates for such cases and we recommend the development of a 
common EU format and text for these.  
- There should also be a contact point to confirm the continued validity of extended legacy 
devices contactable by authori ties o utside the EU . It is expected that the relevant 
stakeholders including the Commission will act to effectively communicate the final 
proposal externally.  
Manufacturer deadlines for May 2024 re main unchanged and the increased workload over next 
15 months is not recognized or addressed in the proposal.  
- While notified bodies tasks of reviewing and issuing certificates are eased with the 
proposal, the other key stakeholder directly in volved in this activity do  not get any 
easement: manufacturers.  
- The deadline for May 2024 remains without reprieve for ma nufacturers in respect to : 
preparing the ir QMS, MDR scope ex pansion audits , MDR techn ical documen tation 
submissions and lengthy reviews . The conversion to MDR  for the majority of legacy 
devices in the EU will occur over the next 15 months . This workload coupled with the 
new MDR requirements ( such as annual certificate surveil lance submissions for all MDR 
devices) and incre ased audits for product scope expansions to the QMS etc. are not 
addressed by the proposal. This is a concer n and inability of manufacturers to manage 
this workload in time will directly impact supply of de vices to patients  and ultimately 
patient care in the EU.  
- Manufacturers involved in transition review/audits are spending inordinate time and 
resources trying to navigate the bureaucratic loopholes of notified bod y’s or designating 
authority requirements or interpre tation. Examples of such protracted discussions are

Aligning Class III deadlines for custom made devices & allowing quicker provision of such devices 
- We welcome the inclusion of Custom -Made Class III implantable to the transitional 
provisions. Such de vices are clinician requested for an individual patient based on the 
clinician’s medical judgement and the patient’s need.   
- We would suggest that the same ti meline of December 2027  be given as th at of the 
other Class III implantable devices.  
While MDR certification of legacy devices is the goal, the i ssuing of certificat e does not mean 
the transition to MDR for that device is completed.  
- For legacy devices, certification starts the next steps for the manufacturer: the issuing of 
the declaration of conformity, translation of the relevant labelling and manuals , 
producing and planning of stock which timelines can vary from device to device.  
- Timelines on when certifi cates will be issued by notified bod ies is very unpredictable 
which means that manufacturers do not know when the certificate will be issued  and 
when to produc e MDR stock. This m eans a  delay between the issuing of a MDR 
certificate and the actual “switching” of devices from legacy to MDR on stock and to 
actual placement on the market.  
Guidance will be needed to clarify the following aspects of the proposal to ensure a common 
understanding and application across Notified Bodies:  
- the definition of a “device intended to substitute that device”  
- what is understood to meet the condition of “lodged a formal application” 
- will the September 2024 deadline allow for sufficient time  for notified bod ies to have 
“signed a written agreement” in place?

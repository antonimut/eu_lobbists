Introduction
Faktograf welcomes this initiative for strengthening the free and pluralistic media systemand thecommitment toprotect journalists andeditorialindependencewithintheEuropeanUnion.Wesharethat thesevalues aredirectlylinkedtofundamental rights, suchas freedomof expression, therightto access information in the public interest and making informed choices in elections.We are well aware of thepressingneeds toprovideanenablingenvironment for journalisminthepublic interest, mediapluralism,diversityandviability-onthelevelofthewholeEuropeanUnion.Atthe same time, we have yet to seehowthenewregulationwill impact theinformationecosystem(implementation of the Digital Services Act, as well as regulation on audiovisual media services,copyright and platform to business trading practices).Our comments on this Proposal provide some insights and perspectives that call for additionalclarifications in the text.

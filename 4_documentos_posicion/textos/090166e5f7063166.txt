Feedback on the Proposal for a Directive on EU restrictive measures 
 
In general, it is crucial to swiftly and effectively ensure the full operationalization of the UNSC Resolution 
2664 from 9 December 2022 ( S/RES/2664(2022) by which the UNSC adopted a standing humanitarian 
exemption to nearly all its asset freeze sanctions regimes. In practice, the  effectiveness of the carveout 
will now depend on swift and effective national implementation by key States and the EU. While UNSC 
2664 only covers UN sanctions regimes, it is important that other sanctioning bodies, such as the EU align 
their autonomous sanctions with the new UN humanitarian exemption – therefore ensuring respect for 
international law, including IHL, as well as enhancing coherence and consistency with regards to the use 
of humanitarian carve outs in restrictive measures. 
In particular some clauses are particularly concerning and we are putting forward some recommendations 
on how to rephrase them:

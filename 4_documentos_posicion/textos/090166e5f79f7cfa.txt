Introduction 
» Aurubis is a leading global supplier of non-ferrous metals. We process complex metal concentrates and 
various recycling raw materials. Aurubis is the world leader in copper recycling. 45% of our products come 
from recycled materials. 
» Our products are indispensable in the energy transition, digitization, and the field of e-mobility.  
» We have already significantly reduced our GHS emissions. Our installations have implemented the best 
technologies with regard to energy efficiency and direct emissions. The carbon  footprint of Aurubis’ copper 
cathode is only 40% of the carbon footprint of global competitors.  
» We are committed to reduce by half our CO2 emissions by 2030 and be come climate-neutral well b efore 
2050. This will require significant investments during this decade.  
» As energy intensive industry and price taker in global competition we are facing higher production costs due 
to the electricity costs that our competitors do not have . Our products are traded globally as a  commodity 
at the same price on the stock exchanges and we cannot pass artificial additional costs on to our customers. 
» Access to competitively priced fossil-free electricity in sufficient quantity is key for our sustainable operation 
and pre-condition for reaching our decarbonisation goals.  
 
Need for an urgent EU response to overcome the challenges related to high energy prices  and secure the 
global competitiveness of EU industry  
» The current situation of sustained high electricity prices is having a devastating impact on the competitiveness 
of electro-intensive industries in the EU. It has substantially increased the operational costs of copper smelters 
and refiners in Europe, and is causing a competitive disadvantage vis -à-vis producers in other regions in the 
world where electricity prices remain considerably lower. The unpredictability of prices is also heavily impacting 
on the ability of copper producers to make investments. 
» The transition towards a low carbon economy will need accelerated investments in green technologies and 
fossil free electricity. This will only be possible if the electro-intensive industry providing the critical raw 
materials for the wind turbines, batteries, solar panels, electrolysers and grid infrastructure can remain in 
Europe and maintain its global competitiveness. 
» Copper is a crucial raw material to achieve the EU’s objective of a carbon-neutral economy and Europe will need 
more copper to enable the fast deployment of the technologies needed to decarbonise its economy in the coming 
years.  
 
The revision of EU Electricity market design must seek to ensure globally competitively priced industrial 
electricity supply to maintain strategic energy-intensive industr ies in the EU  and support 
decarbonization through electrification 
 
The reform requires a complete package of short-term and long-term measures : 
• Ensure certainty and predictability of the European electricity market for the purpose of fostering 
investments, business and economic development  
• Act urgently to reduce EU electricity prices, by introducing a mechanism in the electricity market for 
addressing excessively high market prices and improving long-term contracts conditions, especially 
with renewable sources.  
o Introduce a  price shock absorber mechanism  that would be automatically triggered in 
situations of extraordinarily high prices to limit the ability of fossil generation to set wholesale 
electricity clearing prices in the EU in such situations. This would lower electricity prices for 
all consumers with immediate effect, while preserving the merit order curve. 
• In the mid-term: Introduction of a mechanism to foster electricity transition while preserving the global 
competitiveness of EU industry from incompatible high prices.

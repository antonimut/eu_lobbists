Puro.earth’s response to the EU’s Carbon Removal Certification Framework 
Puro.earth is a carbon removal crediting platform and through the Puro Standard, we create and verify robust 
science-based methodologies for removal of CO2 from the atmosphere. Puro issues CO2 Removal Certificates, 
CORCs, per ton of CO2 removed and durably stored. CORCs are issued and retired in the public Puro Registry, 
adding transparency to carbon markets. Therefore, Puro.earth could apply to be recognised as a Certification 
Scheme under the proposed EU Carbon Removal Certification Framework (CRC-F) to certify the supply of EU 
Carbon Removal Units (EU-CRUs), and report EU-CRUs in the public Puro Registry. 
 Solutions for permanent storage and products: we certify durable, industrial1 carbon removal through a 
wide range of solutions including biochar, enhanced rock weathering, carbonated building materials, 
bioenergy with carbon capture and storage, and direct air carbon capture and storage.  We aim in the future 
to cover more industrial solutions including the ocean environment. We ask that the EU CRC-F is open to a 
wide range of solutions as this sector continues to innovate and evolve.  
 High-quality and durable: We support the QU.A.L.ITY criteria as proposed by the EU Commission, as 
durable (stored for at least 100 years), industrial carbon removal will enable the EU to reach net-zero 
emissions.  These types of removal are Quantifiable (QU), additional (A) as they require carbon financing 
from EU-CRUs, offer a long-term storage (L), and with sustainability safeguards (ITY) in place.  These 
innovative carbon removal technologies need to scale during this decade, and be commercially deployed in 
the 2030s when these solutions are required at million tonne scale by EU member states. 
 Methodologies: we ask that EU policy makers also consider both quality options to certify EU-CRUs, as 
described in the impact assessment.  Option Q1 enables Certification Schemes to develop methodologies in 
line with the EU criteria and submit them for recognition, and Q2 enables the EU Commission to adopt its 
own methodologies with the EU Expert Group.  This legislation should give flexibility to implement both to 
options whereby the EU Commission approves methodologies developed by Certification Schemes and at 
the same time develops its own methodologies.  This would enable the timely implementation of this 
framework. 
 Geographic scope should be global resulting in the EU enabling high-quality durable carbon removal where 
supply chains stretch outside of the EU.  EU-CRUs should include their country of origin (first point of 
permanent storage) as an attribute and this could be linked to how it is used under voluntary or compliance 
markets. 
 The use of EU-CRUs should be clarified under the CRCF.  We believe that high-quality durable industrial 
carbon removals can contribute to both voluntary corporate claims such as through the Corporate 
Sustainability Reporting Directive and the Green Claims Directive as well as to compliance markets such as 
the EU Emissions Trading System given safeguards which ensure accelerated emission reductions and that 
carbon removals are permanent. 
We also welcome the EU collaborating internationally with the Article 6.4 Supervisory Body as well as other 
jurisdictions globally as the world legislates to deliver net-zero with a key role for durable, industrial carbon 
removal.  If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact Helen Bray - helen.bray@puro.earth

Our mission is to mobilise the economy to reward net-negative carbon removal. We do this by 
helping voluntary corporate buyers accelerate carbon dioxide removal at an industrial global scale 
by purchase of credits. Through the Puro Standard we create carbon crediting methodologies for 
processes that remove carbon dioxide from the atmosphere long-term for at least 100 years. Via 
3rd-party verifiers, we then certify suppliers that run those processes and issue digital tradable CO2 
Removal Certificates (CORCs) into the public Puro Registry per metric ton of carbon dioxide 
removed. CORCs are then purchased directly from suppliers or via sales channel partners by 
ambitious corporations, like our customers Microsoft, Shopify, and Zurich Insurance, to help 
neutralize their residual emissions.

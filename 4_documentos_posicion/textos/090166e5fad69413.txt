COSMED INPUT on the proposal for a regulation on packaging and packaging 
waste 
 
First of all,  COSMED support this proposal for a regulation on packaging and packaging waste to 
harmonize rules in European Union and to move towards a more circular economy.  
Cosmetic SMEs are strongly committed to reduce packaging and packaging waste  and share the 
ambition of the circular economy action plan. However, COSMED highlight that obstacles prevent the 
achievement of some objectives and stress that consideration on feasibility , operational reality,  
maturity of the market and technologies, and impact shall be taken into account to adapt some targets 
especially considering SMEs situation.  
 
✓ Regarding recycling ambition  
The proposal set that from 1 January 2030, all packaging comply with design for recycling criteria, 
prohibiting performance grade E. Calculation rules would be harmonized in a second step by a 
delegated act, but we already want to stress that this target remains very challenging in the timeline 
defined. More time is given for “innovative packaging” but better clarification on related definition of 
what may be considered as a “new material”, “new production process”, or “significant improvement 
in the functions of packaging” would be needed.  
 
✓ Regarding recycled content targets 
COSMED alerts that there is no sufficient quality and quantity of recycled materials available to achieve 
the objectives in the timeframe defined for the different sectors. Especially for cosmetic sector subject 
to strict safety and traceability requirements regarding packaging  material content and limiting even 
more the sources opportunities . Indeed, cosmetic industries need to source specific food contact 
quality materials for their packaging. Overall shortage, increase of costs and market disruption can 
already be expected and this will impact SMEs even more.  
Feasible targets and realistic deadlines must be defined, according to the maturity of recycled market 
to avoid any market fragmentation. Such targets should not jeopardize safety and traceability to fulfill 
cosmetic requirements.  
In addition, we highligh t the need to explicitly recognize chemical and enzymatic recycling as 
complementary means of achieving the overall targets.  
 
✓ Regarding harmonization of sorting information 
 
COSMED strongly support s the harmonization of E U rules to ensure right consumer sorting of 
packaging. Several unharmonized measure have been implemented in some Member States. Some 
requiring the labelling on pack.

In addition, we are concerned with the way the mandatory requirement has been set, indeed labelling 
the material composition would not give relevant information to the  consumer at sorting time when 
he needs to decide in which  waste bin he should put its packaging. So, in the end counterproductive 
towards the sorting objective as without proper consumer sorting recycling cannot happen. Existing 
national requirements may be considered and sorting information  pictograms already in place in 
France, introduced by AGEC lawi may serve as an example of a practical and relevant i nformation to 
ensure proper consumer sorting gesture.  
 
✓ To define feasible, harmonized and coherent objectives in EU 
On a general matter, we support harmonized targets  across EU. Some Member states have  already 
implemented national goals, that may be stricter than those defined in the EU draft regulation, and for 
some, already anticipated as unachievable. To avoid any competitive distortion, harmonization is 
required with realistic ambition considering all sectorial constraints and specificities.  
 
✓ Regarding recycling targets of packaging waste 
 
We express concerned on the targets defined for paper and carboard which are unrealistic respectively 
by 2030 and 2040. Achieving the objectives would imply increasing the thickness of the material which 
would be contrary to the initial goal of the circular economy and in the end counterproductive.   
 
Thus, COSMED stress that Cosmetic SMEs remain engaged to move towards a more circular economy 
but alert on the main obstacles in the achievement and feasibility of the proposed objectives:  
-Overall harmonized measures towards EU members states to avoid competitive distortion and 
ensure EU free circulation of goods 
- Accessibility of recycled material considering cosmetic requirements in terms of quality, safety and 
traceability of packaging (material with food contact quality) 
- Increased costs, amplified by expected supply tension. Consideration that SMEs will be the most 
impacted by supply tension and increased costs, specific support should be provided to SMEs. 
- Incorporation of recycled content, reuse, refill should not be made by jeopardizing consumer safety. 
Targets defined must remain compatible with high standards of hygiene, traceability and safety 
required for cosmetics.  
We ask for the possibility to review targets and timeline, especially considering those already defined 
in article 7 considering the above-mentioned elements.

About COSMED 
 
COSMED is the French cosmetic association for SMEs, a non-profit seeking association with more than 
1000 members, in France and Europe. Cosmed sit at the European Commission as the voice of cosmetic 
SMEs. 
COSMED is involved in the development of cosmetic regulations, as well as in national and international 
standardisation bodies. In this way, COSMED contributes to the development of a regulatory  
framework for the marketing of safe and effective cosmetic products.

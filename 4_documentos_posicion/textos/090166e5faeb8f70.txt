Reducing packaging waste – review of rules. 
 
About ADELMA 
 
ADELMA is a business organization joining manufacturers and distributors of detergents and 
cleaning and maintenance products, pesticides and biocides, all of them for both household and 
institutional, professional and industrial use. 
 
 
The Association represents a dynamic, tremendously competitive economic sector making a 
continuous effort to innovate  and satisfy the needs of consumers with products that are 
increasingly safer and more sustainable and compatible with the environment. 
 
The objectives of the Association are the defense of the interests of our member companies and 
the development and progress of our sector, always with  total respect to the rules of free 
competition. 
 
  
Ref. Ares(2023)2876771 - 24/04/2023
 
   
 
  
 
At ADELMA we believe that, in order for  companies to achieve environmental objectives, 
whether legal or voluntary, it is essential to have a harmonized regulatory framework that 
guarantees the necessary legal certainty to make the adaptations required in all the Member 
States of the European Union. 
 
The packaging is an essential element on many occasions and plays a key role in the sustainability 
of the product throughout the supply chain since, in addition to protecting the product itself and 
the consumer, it is also the vehicle through which an amount of mandatory and voluntary (always 
useful) information is given. 
 
The European Commission, thanks to the work it is currently developing on the revision of the 
Packaging and Packaging Waste Directive, will address some of the key points such as prevention, 
the concept of recyclability, the mandatory content of recycled material or marking/labeling of 
the packaging. The definition of these concepts and measures and how to regulate them, is being 
widely debated at European level due to the difficulty  involved in their correct application, 
measurement and communication. 
 
This new packaging regulation will harmonize the essential requirements of packaging at the EU 
level, and that is why it is of great importance to take into account those consideration s 
established in the case of Spain such as the definitions of the different agents with obligations in 
Responsibility Extended Producer since imposing other aspects could generate contradictions 
and new obligations to the newly established ones, as well as obstacles to the free movement of 
packaged goods in the internal market.

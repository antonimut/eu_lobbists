onderwerp Right to repair 
 
 
 
datum  17 mei 2023 
 
Questions ANWB: 
 
1. The proposal has to clarify the difference between repair and replacement in 
circumstances that a product is part of another product. E.g. a batterie of a car is 
being replaced but it could be seen as repair of the car.  
2. The proposal needs additional exemptions for circumstances in which repair is 
possible but not desirable. E.g. no repair possibilities within short distance, no repair 
possibilities within due time (long waiting period), if the aesthetic value of the 
outcome of repair is below the level that is was before (old timer cars). 
3. The proposal needs clarification about the content of the costs of repair. E.g. are 
diagnostic costs included, costs to get a vehicle to a repair station. Are these costs 
on the account of the repair station? And how about the situation that a consumer 
asks more addresses to offer a repair price (quotation) to compare prices? 
4. The proposal is not clear about the limits in time that is acceptable for repair. There 
is a need for clarification about offering substitute products during the time of 
repair. 
5. The proposal is not clear about the situation in which the product is broken after 
repair. Is a second repair also needed or can the consumer chose for replacement in 
that case? 
6. We missed an option or calculation of effects to use the measure of increasing the 
period of legal guarantee. This would stimulate producers to design their products in 
a more durable way. 
7. We miss a serious analysis of effects of increasing the amount of repairs on the 
costs of repair. Since 2010 there is e.g. a shortage of car mechanics in the 
automotive sector. Increasing the amount of repairs will probably increase the costs 
of repair. Which increase (estimation) is foreseen by the Commission?

8. There will be a national registration of enterprises that offer customized repair. Is 
there a quality check of the businesses that are registered? Will enterprises that 
offer revised spare parts also be included? 
 
I am confident this contribution can support to sharpen the involved proposal.

Working conditions upstreamof the supplychains need to be significantly improved, and certification schemes alone will not achieve thisgoal. Certification schemes have not proved to be effective mechanisms to prevent social andenvironmental negative impacts, are costly for SMEs and local producers and often lead to effortsbeing put on the paperwork associated with obtaining such certification, rather than in effectivelyensuring working conditions meet the adequate standards.

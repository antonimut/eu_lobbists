Plastics Recyclers Europe (PRE) feedback to the public consultation on the EC Proposal for the Net 
Zero Industry Act (NZIA) 
 
 
PRE,1 the association representing the plastic recycling industry at the EU level, welcomes the 
European Commission’s (EC) proposal for a Net Zero Industry Act  (NZIA)2 as an effective 
instrument to support the 2050 target of a climate-neutral economy in the Union. The proposal sets 
an ambitious legal framework for the industry to transition towards the circular economy, at a time 
when it has become pivotal to tackle environmental protection, reduce emissions and pollution, and 
manage resource depletion. 
 
 
With more than 730 facilities generating an annual turnover of 8.7 B€, offering an installed capacity 
of 11.3Mt and employing more than 30,000 persons, the EU plastic recycling industry is a key player 
on the global scale . Moreover, the recycling of plastic wa ste greatly contributes to lowering GHG 
emissions and reducing pollution from the (mis-)management of plastic waste. 
 
 
To achieve carbon neutrality, the European industry needs to reduce its dependency on fossil fuels. 
These are used mostly in the energy sector but, given the electrification of transport, these 
chemicals will be increasingly used for the production of plastics . It follows that, in the future, 
decoupling the production of plastics from the extraction of fossil fuels must become a key priority 
for the 2030 and 2050 targets to be achieved.  
 
 
The set of net-zero industries listed in the Annex represent technologies that are primarily designed 
and developed to reduce carbon emissions; n evertheless, any other technology introducing a 
systemic shift in the EU economy, which reaches the same objective of reducing carbon emissions, 
should be included in the annexed list and to the scope of the NZIA. 
 
Under the umbrella term “recycling” there are many technolog ies that are developed and used to 
recycle plastics. Given the EU legal framework and the technology -neutral definition for recycling 
in Art. 3(17) of the Waste Framework Directive, PRE welcomes a reference to “recycling” and not 
to a particular technolog y, but perhaps to the specific waste stream recycled (e.g., plastics, glass, 
aluminium). This way, the NZIA can prioritize material -specific technologies that contribute the 
most to its objectives. 
 
This is the case of the recycling of plastic waste, whereby the production of plastics is gradually 
decoupled from the extraction of fossil fuels.

ABOUT PLASTICS RECYCLERS EUROPE  
Plastics Recyclers Europe is an organization representing the voice of the European plastics recyclers 
who reprocess plastic waste into high quality material destined for production of new articles. Recyclers 
are important facilitators of the circularity o f plastics and the transition towards the circular economy.  
 
Plastics recycling in Europe is a rapidly growing sector representing over € 8.7 billion in turnover, 11.3 
million tonnes of installed recycling capacity, more than 730 recycling facilities, and over 30.000 
employees.

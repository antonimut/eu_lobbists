TheEuropeanCommission’s statedintentions of boththeNet-ZeroIndustry Act (NZIA) andCritical RawMaterials Act (CRMA) is to boost EU resilience by driving up EU-based supply chains for “net-zerotechnologies” and domestic extraction and processing of resources, while supporting EU2030energyandclimatetargetsanddevelopingtheskillsassociatedwiththemanufacturingofthosetechnologies.

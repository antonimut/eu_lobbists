We refer to the invitation to provide input on the European Commission's proposal for a new regulation on the 
framework for critical raw materials, the Critical Raw Materials Act, as well as the meeting on  15 May 2023. We 
appreciate the opportunity to submit our proposed amendments in writing on the regulation.  
Vianode welcomes the EU ’s initiative to ensur e more sustainable management of critical raw materials for 
technologies that are crucial in the green transition, including technologies and materials for batteries. 
There is a gap  in expected demand and expected supply o f materials in the battery value chain  in Europe. The 
production of battery materials, including graphite , in Europe must increase to ensure the EU’s access to secure, 
diversified, affordable and sustainable supply of critical raw materials  and to meet the benchmarks for domestic 
capacities set out by the regulation. 
Currently, there  are two types of graphite used in batteries for electric vehicles; synthetic and natural graphite. 
Synthetic graphite is produced in chemical industrial processes , while natural graphite  is sourced  from mining  
operations. It is important to note that only natural graphite is included in the list of Critical Raw Materials. 
It is our strong belief that synthetic and natural graphite should be considered strategic materials on an equal footing. 
Natural graphite alone will not be able to meet EU's own benchmarks ( in particular 40% annual consumption of 
processing) nor secure the properties required in modern batteries.  Both synthetic and natural graphite are equally 
important, critical materials strategic to Europe’s economy and vital to the implementation of the Joint European 
action for more affordable,  secure energy, the increased resilience of EU economy as well as of EU Green Deals 
objectives. The European Commission’s strategic foresight report already assesses both synthetic  and natural  
graphite and considers both strategic.   
More specifically, we propose the following amendments to the Critical Raw Materials Act  based on the 
reasoning above: 
• Section 2 Strategic raw materials, Annex 1, change  
from “The following raw materials shall be considered as strategic: (j) Natural Graphite – battery grade” 
to “The following raw materials shall be considered as strategic: (j) Graphite”
